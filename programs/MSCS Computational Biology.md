# MSCS Computational Biology

The Computational Biology specialization, from the 2026-27 program sheet. Foundations, breadth and electives are shared by all specializations: see [MSCS](MSCS.md).

## Depth

At least 21 units, every course letter-graded and taken for 3 or more units. At most 6 units of [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) count. Deviations need a petition to the MSCS director and are approved only in exceptional circumstances.

- **a**: [CS 173A](../courses/CS%20173A.md). Waivable with equivalent coursework from another institution, with advisor and administrator approval.
- **b**: [CS 221](../courses/CS%20221.md).
- **c**: at least two of [CS 142](../courses/CS%20142.md) (or [CS 147L](../courses/CS%20147L.md) or [CS 193X](../courses/CS%20193X.md)), [CS 145](../courses/CS%20145.md), [CS 246](../courses/CS%20246.md) and [CS 448B](../courses/CS%20448B.md) — the data and interface engineering half.
- **d**: at least three from the biomedical list, [CS 279](../courses/CS%20279.md) plus BIOMEDIN and IMMUNOL courses.
- **e**: the rest of the 21 units, from (a) to (d) or the wider (e) list. Alone among the eight sheets, this one marks nothing † — not even [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md).
