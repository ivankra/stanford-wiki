# MSCS Software Theory

The Software Theory specialization, from the 2026-27 program sheet. Foundations, breadth and electives are shared by all specializations: see [MSCS](MSCS.md).

## Depth

At least 21 units, every course letter-graded and taken for 3 or more units. At most 6 units of [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) count. Deviations need a petition to the MSCS director and are approved only in exceptional circumstances.

- **a**: both [CS 242](../courses/CS%20242.md) and [CS 243](../courses/CS%20243.md) — programming languages and compilers, the pair the specialization is built on. Neither is marked waivable.
- **b**: at least one from the (b) list.
- **c**: at least one of [CS 255](../courses/CS%20255.md), [CS 355](../courses/CS%20355.md) and [CS 356](../courses/CS%20356.md), so every student takes cryptography or security.
- **d**: the rest of the 21 units, from (a), (b), (c) or the (d) list. [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) needs advisor and administrator approval.

[CS 315B](../courses/CS%20315B.md) is on both the (b) and the (d) list.
