# MSCS Systems

The Systems specialization, from the 2026-27 program sheet. Foundations, breadth and electives are shared by all specializations: see [MSCS](MSCS.md).

## Depth

At least 21 units, every course letter-graded and taken for 3 or more units. At most 6 units of [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) count. Deviations need a petition to the MSCS director and are approved only in exceptional circumstances.

- **a**: all of [CS 144](../courses/CS%20144.md), a [CS 240](../courses/CS%20240.md) of any suffix, and one of [CS 112](../courses/CS%20112.md), [CS 140E](../courses/CS%20140E.md) or [CS 212](../courses/CS%20212.md).
- **b**: at least four from the (b) list — the widest required-count of any sheet, which is what pushes a Systems program past 21 units before (c) is reached. [CS 248](../courses/CS%20248.md) and [CS 248A](../courses/CS%20248A.md) count as one entry there.
- **c**: the rest of the units, from (a), (b) or the (c) list. CS 349, any suffix, counts only when taken for 3 or more units. [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) needs advisor and administrator approval.
