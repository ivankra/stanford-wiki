# MSCS AI

The Artificial Intelligence specialization, from the 2026-27 program sheet. Foundations, breadth and electives are shared by all specializations: see [MSCS](MSCS.md).

## Depth

At least 21 units, every course letter-graded and taken for 3 or more units. At most 6 units of [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) count. Deviations need a petition to the MSCS director and are approved only in exceptional circumstances.

- **a**: [CS 221](../courses/CS%20221.md). Waivable with equivalent coursework. [CS 229](../courses/CS%20229.md) may substitute, but then it can't also count toward (b).
- **b**: at least four courses from the (b) list.
- **c**: the rest of the 21 units, from (a), (b) or the wider (c) list. Entries marked † (CS 377, any suffix, and [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md)) need advisor and administrator approval.
