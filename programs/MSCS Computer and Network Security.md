# MSCS Computer and Network Security

The Computer and Network Security specialization, from the 2026-27 program sheet. Foundations, breadth and electives are shared by all specializations: see [MSCS](MSCS.md).

## Depth

At least 21 units, every course letter-graded and taken for 3 or more units. At most 6 units of [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) count. Deviations need a petition to the MSCS director and are approved only in exceptional circumstances.

- **a**: all four of [CS 144](../courses/CS%20144.md), [CS 155](../courses/CS%20155.md), [CS 255](../courses/CS%20255.md) and [CS 356](../courses/CS%20356.md) — networking, applied security, cryptography and a security-research seminar — plus one systems course from [CS 112](../courses/CS%20112.md), [CS 140E](../courses/CS%20140E.md) and [CS 212](../courses/CS%20212.md). This is the most prescriptive (a) of the eight sheets.
- **b**: at least three from the (b) list.
- **c**: the rest of the 21 units, from (a), (b) or the (c) list, where [CS 221](../courses/CS%20221.md) and [CS 229](../courses/CS%20229.md) count as alternatives to each other. [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) needs advisor and administrator approval; this sheet writes the mark as ‡ rather than †.
