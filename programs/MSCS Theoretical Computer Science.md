# MSCS Theoretical Computer Science

The Theoretical Computer Science specialization, from the 2026-27 program sheet. Foundations, breadth and electives are shared by all specializations: see [MSCS](MSCS.md).

## Depth

At least 21 units, every course letter-graded and taken for 3 or more units. At most 6 units of [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) count. Deviations need a petition to the MSCS director and are approved only in exceptional circumstances.

Only two categories, the fewest of the eight sheets:

- **a**: [CS 154](../courses/CS%20154.md) and [CS 261](../courses/CS%20261.md). [CS 154](../courses/CS%20154.md) is marked ‡, waivable with equivalent coursework from another institution; [CS 261](../courses/CS%20261.md) is not.
- **b**: the rest of the 21 units, from (a) or the (b) list. The research-seminar and advanced-topics numbers — CS 359 and CS 369, any suffix, [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) and [CS 468](../courses/CS%20468.md), any suffix — are marked † and need advisor and administrator approval that the course is relevant to the specialization.

This is the only sheet using both marks: ‡ for the waivable course in (a), † for the approval-gated entries in (b).
