# MSCS Information Management and Analytics

The Information Management and Analytics specialization, from the 2026-27 program sheet. Foundations, breadth and electives are shared by all specializations: see [MSCS](MSCS.md).

## Depth

At least 21 units, every course letter-graded and taken for 3 or more units. At most 6 units of [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) count. Deviations need a petition to the MSCS director and are approved only in exceptional circumstances.

- **a**: [CS 145](../courses/CS%20145.md), data systems. Waivable with equivalent coursework from another institution, administrator approval only.
- **b**: at least four of [CS 224N](../courses/CS%20224N.md), [CS 224W](../courses/CS%20224W.md), [CS 229](../courses/CS%20229.md), [CS 246](../courses/CS%20246.md) and [CS 281](../courses/CS%20281.md) — four out of five, the tightest core of the eight sheets. [CS 281](../courses/CS%20281.md), AI ethics, sits in that core rather than among the electives.
- **c**: the rest of the 21 units, from (a), (b) or the wide (c) list, which reaches from storage and networking through machine learning into computational biology. [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) needs advisor and administrator approval.

The lists are below, split between courses with a page (Courses) and those without (TODO).
