# MSCS Visual Computing

The Visual Computing specialization, from the 2026-27 program sheet. Foundations, breadth and electives are shared by all specializations: see [MSCS](MSCS.md).

## Depth

At least 21 units, every course letter-graded and taken for 3 or more units. Deviations need a petition to the MSCS director and are approved only in exceptional circumstances. This sheet states no [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) cap and lists no [CS 399](../courses/CS%20399.md) entry, so independent study cannot count toward this depth.

- **a**: at least two of [CS 248A](../courses/CS%20248A.md), [CS 248B](../courses/CS%20248B.md) and [CS 231N](../courses/CS%20231N.md) — rendering, simulation and deep learning for vision, any two.
- **b**: at least three more, from (a) or the (b) list: geometry, animation, image processing and the 348-series graphics courses.
- **c**: the rest of the 21 units, from (a), (b) or the (c) list. Nothing here is marked †.

The department's earlier sheets for this specialization are filed under "RWC", Real-World Computing; the name on the 2026-27 sheet is Visual Computing.
