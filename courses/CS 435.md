# CS 435: Math Out Loud: Collaborative Learning Analytics for Multilingual Math Discourse

A project-based workshop on how students, especially multilingual ones, learn math through small-group conversation. Students work with MathOutLoud, a new corpus of small-group math talk from grades 2–10, and build an original analytics project that they present to invited teachers and researchers.
