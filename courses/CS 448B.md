# CS 448B: Data Visualization

Maneesh Agrawala's visualization course, for students who want to use visualization in their own work or build better visualization tools. Lectures pair research readings with Observable notebooks, and the coursework builds from a static design to an interactive article.

## Materials

Everything except submissions is public; reading responses and assignments go through Canvas. The [course site](https://magrawala.github.io/cs448b-fa26/) has the full schedule with readings (many as PDFs), and links each file as it's released:

## Syllabus

Two lectures a week for nine weeks, then design reviews of final projects.

1. Purpose of visualization; data and image models
2. Design and redesign; exploratory data analysis (Polaris)
3. Chart design and small multiples; interaction
4. Introduction to D3; D3 tutorial
5. Graphical perception; visual explainers and narrative visualization
6. Color; animation
7. Network layout; network analysis
8. Deconstructing visualizations (chart classification and redesign)
9. Text visualization; visualization and natural language processing
10. Final project design reviews

## Prerequisites

None official. Familiarity with CS 147, CS 148 and CS 142 helps; most important is working knowledge of, or willingness to learn, JavaScript, Vega-Lite and D3.js.
