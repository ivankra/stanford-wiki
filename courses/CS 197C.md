# CS 197C: Computer Science Research: CURIS Internship Onramp

Same lectures as [CS 197](CS%20197.md), different substrate: instead of a project assigned by the course, each student works on the research they are already doing with a CURIS or lab mentor, who commits to a weekly thirty-minute check-in.

## Syllabus

The [schedule](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs197c/) runs one lecture a week, each releasing the assignment that builds the proposal:

1. **Weeks 1–3, framing**: what is research; research contributions as "bit flips"; arguing a research project. Assignment 1 reading a paper, 1.5 a research milestone plan, 2 related work.
2. **Weeks 4–6, doing it**: vectoring in research, velocity in research, evaluation. Assignment 3 the introduction.
3. **Weeks 7–8, writing**: writing a paper and picking projects, research career paths. Assignment 4 proposed solution and evaluation plan, 5 the draft proposal.
4. **Weeks 9–10, presenting**: giving talks, publication and peer review. Assignment 6 draft presentation, 7 research mentor feedback, 8 the final presentation, 9 the final proposal.

A research log is due before every lecture.

## Prerequisites

- Programming: [CS 106B](CS%20106B.md).
- An external mentor — a PhD student or professor — committed to a weekly thirty-minute check-in, and an ongoing spring or summer research project.
