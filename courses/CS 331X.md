# CS 331X: AI for Algorithmic Reasoning and Optimization

Four foundations lectures, then eleven paper discussions in assigned roles, on the two-way traffic between machine learning and algorithm design: learned components inside solvers, and theory about when they work. Graded 60 points on discussions and 40 on a NeurIPS-format mini-paper, empirical or theoretical.

## Syllabus

Foundations lectures open the quarter (GNNs, integer programming, constraint satisfaction, later diffusion models), then discussions in five blocks ([schedule](https://vitercik.github.io/ai4algs_25/schedule/)): algorithmic reasoning with transformers and LLMs (in-context regression, transformers as statisticians, algorithmic understanding of generative AI, LLM-evolved heuristics); reasoning with GNNs (AnyCSP, dual algorithmic reasoning, DIFUSCO); mathematical optimization (OptiMUS, contrastive predict-and-search, differentiable ILP); and theoretical guarantees (policy-gradient landscapes for solution samplers, approximation algorithms with predictions). The last week is project presentations.
