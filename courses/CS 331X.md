# CS 331X: AI for Algorithmic Reasoning and Optimization

A paper seminar on the two-way traffic between machine learning and algorithm design: learned components inside solvers, and theory about when they work. 4 foundations lectures supply the tools, then 11 paper discussions run in assigned roles. Graded 60 points on discussions and 40 on a NeurIPS-format mini-paper, empirical or theoretical. It ran as CS 331 in Spring 2023, and this offering reads all-new papers.

## Syllabus

Foundations lectures open the quarter (GNNs, integer programming, constraint satisfaction, later diffusion models), then discussions in 4 blocks ([schedule](https://vitercik.github.io/ai4algs_25/schedule/)):

1. Algorithmic reasoning with transformers and LLMs: in-context regression, transformers as statisticians, algorithmic understanding of generative AI, LLM-evolved heuristics.
2. Reasoning with GNNs: AnyCSP, dual algorithmic reasoning, DIFUSCO.
3. Mathematical optimization: OptiMUS, contrastive predict-and-search, differentiable ILP.
4. Theoretical guarantees: policy-gradient landscapes for solution samplers, approximation algorithms with predictions.

The last week is project presentations.

Spring 2023's CS 331 covered the earlier agenda: GNNs for combinatorial optimization, algorithm configuration and portfolio selection for IP and SAT, MDPs and RL for optimization, then statistical guarantees, online configuration and algorithms with predictions.
