# CS 309A: Cloud Computing Seminar

A weekly guest-lecture seminar, open to students from any school, in which public-company CEOs who deliver or use cloud services discuss how software is transforming their businesses. Credit takes attendance, a question submitted before each lecture, and a short final paper.

## Materials

For a talk series the core is the schedule and who is speaking, and this offering publishes neither: the site's newest speaker page is Autumn 2024's, and the working site is for enrolled students. What stands in is [Autumn 2022's own site](https://sites.google.com/view/cs309a-autumn-2022/Home), 4 years old and complete.

- **Syllabus**: the [requirements page](https://sites.google.com/view/stanfordcs309a/requirements) gives what credit takes and links a speaker page per year back to Autumn 2005. Autumn 2022's site adds a dated schedule and a page per speaker.
- **Slides**: none. No offering attaches a deck to its speaker pages.
- **Notes**: the older speaker pages carry each talk's background video and reading; this offering has no speaker pages at all.
- **Videos**: one session of Autumn 2022 is on Stanford Online — Timothy Chou's own ["7 Life Lessons"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-seEo6Ms_Q), captioned "Autumn 2022, Stanford University". None of the guest talks is recorded anywhere public, and the site links no video of its own.
- **Assignments**: a final paper. Autumn 2022's [prompt](https://sites.google.com/view/cs309a-autumn-2022/requirements-1) is public; this offering posts none.
- **Solutions**, **Exams**, **Projects**, **Repo**: none; the paper is the whole of the work.

## Syllabus

No topic list; each week is a guest. The Autumn 2022 run, for example:

1. Timothy Chou (instructor): opening lecture
2. VMware: private cloud computing
3. Samsara: IoT application cloud software
4. Couchbase: NoSQL document databases
5. GitLab: software development as a cloud service
6. Johnson & Johnson
7. Illumina: gene sequencing and its data
8. Morgan Lewis: law meets AI, cloud computing and big data
9. Timothy Chou: "Ten Lessons for Long Life"

Autumn 2024 guests came from HPE, Zendesk, Synopsys, Twilio and Neotribe Ventures.

## Prerequisites

None, per the site.
