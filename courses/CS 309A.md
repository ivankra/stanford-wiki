# CS 309A: Cloud Computing Seminar

A weekly guest-lecture seminar, open to students from any school, in which public-company CEOs who deliver or use cloud services discuss how software is transforming their businesses. Credit takes attendance, a question submitted before each lecture, and a short final paper.

## Materials

The [course site](https://sites.google.com/view/stanfordcs309a/Home) is public but thin: the [requirements](https://sites.google.com/view/stanfordcs309a/requirements) and a speaker page per year, the newest being [Autumn 2024](https://sites.google.com/view/stanfordcs309a/autumn-2024). No Autumn 2026 schedule is posted; the site says the working site is for enrolled students.

The [Autumn 2022 site](https://sites.google.com/view/cs309a-autumn-2022/Home) is the most complete: a dated schedule, a page per speaker with background videos and reading, and the [final-paper prompt](https://sites.google.com/view/cs309a-autumn-2022/requirements-1). No slides or class recordings were posted.

## Syllabus

No topic list; each week is a guest. The Autumn 2022 run, for example:

1. Timothy Chou (instructor): opening lecture
2. VMware: private cloud computing
3. Samsara: IoT application cloud software
4. Couchbase: NoSQL document databases
5. GitLab: software development as a cloud service
6. Johnson & Johnson
7. Illumina: gene sequencing and its data
8. Morgan Lewis: law meets AI, cloud computing and big data
9. Timothy Chou: "Ten Lessons for Long Life"

Autumn 2024 guests came from HPE, Zendesk, Synopsys, Twilio and Neotribe Ventures.

## Prerequisites

None, per the site.
