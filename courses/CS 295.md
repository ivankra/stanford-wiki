# CS 295: Software Engineering

Not a project course despite the name: the catalog puts the emphasis on "automated tools for developing reliable software" and draws the material "primarily from recent research papers" on the technology underneath them. Assignments are hands-on with those tools and with customizing them for new problems, which is what distinguishes the course from reading the papers alone. It is pitched at two audiences at once — students heading into program analysis and verification research, and students who want the tools in their working repertoire. It meets twice a week for fifty minutes, the shortest contact time of any Winter graduate CS course.
