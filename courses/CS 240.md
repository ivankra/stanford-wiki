# CS 240: Advanced Topics in Operating Systems

A discussion seminar on operating-systems research: roughly one paper per class, read beforehand (the site warns of up to 10–15 hours of reading a week), with class time spent on the key points rather than recapping. Attendance and participation are required.

## Materials

A reading course whose reading is public: the schedule links every paper as a PDF with its reading question, and so do Autumn 2024's and Autumn 2025's. The lab and eleven past exam papers, most of the recent ones with solutions, are public too. Recordings are the only thing withheld, and have been since at least Spring 2020 — the site says to log in to Canvas for them.

- **Syllabus**: the [course site](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs240/) carries the whole schedule, the unit breakdown — concurrency, memory, file systems, OS structure, systems for ML, history — and the grading split: exams 60%, participation 30%, programming assignments 10%.
- **Slides**: six class decks so far, one per class held, linked from the schedule; the site says the schedule is still being decided. [Autumn 2025](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs240/cs240.1262/) has a deck against every session.
- **Notes**: none. There is no textbook, by the course's own statement, and no reader; the papers are the reading.
- **Videos**: on Canvas. No public copy was found for any offering, and Spring 2020 and Spring 2021, taught remotely, put their pre-recorded lectures there too.
- **Assignments**: [lab 1](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs240/labs/lab1.html) — a cooperative user-level threads library in C and x86-64 assembly — with its [skeleton](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs240/labs/lab1-skeleton.zip), plus a reading question against most papers. There is no lab 2 this offering, and Autumn 2025 had none either.
- **Solutions**, **Exams**: [eleven past papers](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs240/#exams), midterms and finals from 2011 to 2016 and Autumn 2024 to Autumn 2025, with worked solutions on the 2015, 2016 and recent ones.
- **Projects**: none; the grade is exams, participation and the lab.
- **Repo**: the lab 1 skeleton zip.
- **Older offerings**: the [past-offerings page](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs240/past.html) links sites back to 2002, and every one since Spring 2020 loads. Only Spring 2023, Autumn 2024 and Autumn 2025 kept their reading lists; the Spring 2020, 2021 and 2022 pages survive with logistics and staff but an empty schedule table.

## Syllabus

Units, by weeks: concurrency (2), memory management (2), file systems (2), OS structure (1), systems for machine learning (1), history and experience (1). The site says the schedule is still being decided. Papers as listed:

1. Concurrency: Worse is Better, Eraser, Mesa monitors, cooperative task management, threads vs. events
2. Memory: receive livelock, VMware ESX memory management, superpages, software vs. hardware x86 virtualization
3. File systems: NFS and leases, LFS, Rethink the Sync, F2FS
4. OS structure: Exokernel, Dune
5. Systems for ML: Salus (GPU sharing), vLLM paged attention
6. History: Hints for Computer System Design

## Prerequisites

The catalog asks for [CS 140](CS%20140.md) or equivalent; the site assumes an undergraduate OS class such as [CS 112](CS%20112.md) or [CS 140E](CS%20140E.md).

## Related

- [CS 111](CS%20111.md): the undergraduate operating-systems course, on filesystems, processes, threads and virtual memory through system calls and C.
- [CS 212](CS%20212.md): building an operating system; its predecessor [CS 140](CS%20140.md) is this course's prerequisite.
- [CS 240LX](CS%20240LX.md): the same ground written instead of read, bare-metal on a Raspberry Pi; it can substitute for this course.
- [CS 244B](CS%20244B.md): distributed systems papers in the same seminar format.
- [CS 340R](CS%20340R.md): a later paper course that takes this one, or an equivalent, as a prerequisite.
