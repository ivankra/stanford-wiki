# CS 377P: Read, Write, Play

A reading-and-playing seminar: classic and contemporary games set beside readings that run from practical design to cultural critique, then argued about in class and written up in blog posts. The aim is a vocabulary for evaluating games as a medium and the ability to say something defensible about one.

## Syllabus

Only the catalog describes the course. It calls it "an interdisciplinary exploration into games as both interactive experiences and cultural artifacts", built on analyzing "both classic and contemporary games alongside diverse readings — from practical design to cultural critiques", to develop "a deep understanding of game design, programming and play, and games' impacts on players and society". The work is reading, playing, writing and discussion, "structured through blog posts and discussions", with the stated aim of equipping students "to critically evaluate games as a medium and articulate their insights through writing".

At 1-3 units, meeting Fridays for eighty minutes, it is the small seminar companion to the instructor's studio courses.
