# CS 407: Lytics Seminar

The seminar attached to Stanford's Lytics Lab: how to design learning environments for adult learners, how to run research inside them, and what the evidence of learning collected in an online environment actually supports. It spends deliberate time on EdTech ventures that failed, looking for patterns and causes, and on the ethics of designing and researching human learning.

## Syllabus

Only the catalog describes the content. Students "learn to design technology mediated learning environments for adult learners, conduct research in those environments, and learn from prior EdTech failures". It is "grounded in various theoretical frameworks that inform the design of learning environments", and explores "how people learn and the evidence of learning that can be collected and modeled in online environments in real world contexts". Specific case studies of failed EdTech ventures are used "to identify patterns and causes of failure", and ethical issues in the design of and research on human learning run through the course. The stated outcome is a foundation in learning theory plus the skills to design, implement and evaluate technology-mediated learning environments.

Three hours on Wednesday afternoons, 1-4 units.
