# CS 47N: Datathletics: Diving into Data Analytics and Stanford Sports

Class meetings alternate between tool instruction and a Stanford coach explaining how their sport uses data; each week's problem set applies that week's tools to that week's sport. The catalog is clear about the emphasis: "using data for strategic decision-making rather than optimizing individual human performance."

## Syllabus

No published syllabus. The catalog gives the tool list: "spreadsheets, the Tableau system for data preparation and visualization, Jupyter notebooks, relational databases and SQL, Python and many of its data-specific packages including Pandas, and machine learning."

The structure is weekly: "Class meetings roughly alternate between: (1) instruction in a variety of tools and techniques for analyzing and visualizing data; and (2) guest lectures by Stanford athletics coaches explaining how data is or could be used in their sport. Through regular problem sets, students bring each week's tools to bear on data related to the week's sport."

## Prerequisites

- Basic programming and computing, "at the level of high school computer science or [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md)". No statistics or data-analysis background is needed — and the catalog warns the reverse too: "students with extensive experience in coding or data science may not be challenged by the technical aspects of the course."
