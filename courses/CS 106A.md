# CS 106A: Programming Methodology

The department's entry point: ten weeks of Python for students with no programming background, taught in lecture plus a mandatory weekly section led by one of the course's army of section leaders, with drop-in helper hours at the Lair.

## Materials

All 29 lectures, all eight assignments, nine section handouts and six past exams with worked solutions are on the open web, and every quarter since Winter 2020 keeps its own archived site. Recordings are the gap: they go to Canvas through Panopto, and no offering publishes a set. Homework solutions aren't published either.

- **Syllabus**: [topics, units, sections and helper hours](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs106a/handouts_w2021/syllabus-cs106a.html), with the grading split in [General Course Information](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs106a/handouts/generalInfo.html). This quarter publishes no dated schedule; every archived quarter has one.
- **Slides**: the course teaches from written handouts, not decks. Lecture 21 is the exception, a public [Google Slides deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/14qBT98CUxGomEWen7VV90qoKFv63r9YzCUPFuK50rSM/edit).
- **Notes**: the instruction itself — 29 lecture handouts from [CS106A in a Picture](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs106a/handouts_w2021/lecture-1-intro.html) to Beyond 106A, nine [section handouts](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs106a/section-w2021/section1/section1.html), and two free reference texts, [Parlante's Python Reader](https://cs.stanford.edu/people/nick/py/) and the [Karel Reader](https://compedu.stanford.edu/karel-reader/docs/python/en/intro.html).
- **Videos**: lectures are recorded, but the Panopto player is a Canvas tool.
- **Assignments**: all eight, sixteen pages counting the warmup and project halves — [Bit](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs106a/handouts_w2021/homework-1-bit.html), Images, Strings, [Cryptography](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs106a/handouts_w2021/homework-4-cryptography.html), Quilt, Baby Names, Ghost, [Infinite Story](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs106a/handouts_w2021/homework-8-infinite.html).
- **Solutions**: complete worked answers to the [six practice exams](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs106a/handouts_w2021/midterm-24-2-solution.html). No homework solutions are published.
- **Exams**: three past midterms and three past finals, 2022 to 2024, behind the [midterm](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs106a/handouts_w2021/midterm-prep.html) and [final](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs106a/handouts_w2021/final-prep.html) prep handouts, each with the reference sheet the real exam hands out. Older papers too: [Winter 2020](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs106a/cs106a.1204/handouts/midterm-19-2-solution.html) still serves three 2018-19 midterms and solutions to all seven of its sections.
- **Projects**: none.
- **Repo**: the [Bit exercises](https://codeinthebrowser.org/make/bithw/bit-alpha) run and self-check in the browser with no login, six worked programs sit under [examples/](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs106a/examples/awesome/), and lecture code ships as `.zip` files.
- **Textbook**: the two readers above, both free and on Stanford hosts. The [Spring offerings](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs106a/cs106a.1266/) use a browser IDE at `ide.stanford.edu/cs106a` instead, whose examples also open without an account.

## Syllabus

The [syllabus](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs106a/handouts_w2021/syllabus-cs106a.html) lists the course by topic rather than by week: types, numbers, strings, functions, lists, dictionaries, logic, decomposition, programming style, whole-program structure, text and file processing, debugging and performance, with lambdas, comprehensions and modules touched on at the end. Python's more advanced features are deliberately out of scope.

Work starts on a browser-based server with no install; PyCharm and a local Python 3 arrive in Week 2, when the mandatory sections also begin.

## Prerequisites

- None. The course assumes no programming background; students who already program are pointed at [CS 106AX](CS%20106AX.md) (same hour, accelerated) or [CS 193Q](CS%20193Q.md).
