# CS 524: AI for Science

Organized around recurring method themes rather than scientific fields: multi-scale modeling, multi-modal architectures, differentiable simulation, search-augmented reasoning and data valuation, each week pairing a milestone with a current result. Twenty student pairs give graded lightning talks through the quarter, and ten guests from DeepMind, Arc Institute, Isomorphic Labs, NVIDIA and Osmo take a session each.

## Materials

The site publishes the syllabus in full; the readings themselves are named, not linked.

- **Syllabus**: five modules over ten weeks with topics, guest slots and milestone dates, then the grading split — project 60%, presentations and reading responses 20%, participation 20% ([course site](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs524/)).
- **Assignments**: the [project spec](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs524/#assignments) sets pitch (10%), mid-term review with a 3–4 page memo (20%), lightning talk (5%) and final report with repo (25%), with a 72-hour slip-day bank and a reproducibility checklist covering seeds, runtimes, ablations and negative results.
- **Exams**: none.
- **Slides and recordings**: not posted; Canvas, Gradescope and Ed carry the course itself.

## Syllabus

Five modules ([schedule](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs524/#schedule)). **A. Foundations** (weeks 1–3): the AI-for-science landscape, reasoning and inference-time scaling; multi-scale modeling and weather foundation models (NeuralGCM); graph-structured learning, message passing, AlphaFold and world modeling. **B. Modalities and simulation** (4–5): cross-domain models (Gato, Evo, IgLM), differentiable simulation, Earth-2/CorrDiff. **C. Reasoning and data** (6–7): the AlphaGo→AlphaZero→MuZero arc, AlphaTensor→AlphaEvolve, AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry; curriculum learning, Data Shapley, human-in-the-loop optimization and AI for fusion. **D. Knowledge and systems** (8–9): knowledge graphs, TransE, neural theorem proving, RTX-KG2, AlphaChip, virtual labs and virtual cell. **E. Symposium** (10): project lightning talks.
