# CS 244B: Distributed Systems

Twenty lectures, twenty papers, hosted openly on the Secure Computer Systems group's own server. The through-line is agreement: FLP's impossibility result in week one, then two-phase commit, Raft, Streamlet, Honey Badger and Stellar's federated variant, with Dynamo and Spanner for what you store once you have it. Modern systems papers — Ray, Spark, SkyPilot, IPFS — keep it from being a history course. The quarter ends with an all-day Saturday project demo.

## Materials

## Syllabus

[One paper per lecture](https://www.scs.stanford.edu/24sp-cs244b/sched/) across ten weeks.

1. Foundations (Apr 1-19): FLP impossibility, Zookeeper, ownership and distributed futures, Ray, Orca, two-phase commit.
2. Execution engines (Apr 22-May 10): Spark, IPFS centralization, Raft, microsecond-scale consensus, SkyPilot.
3. Byzantine agreement (May 13-17): Streamlet, Honey Badger.
4. Storage (May 20-31): Dynamo, Spanner, Groundhog as a guest lecture.
5. Frontier (Jun 3-7): Wave and Stellar, both without homework, before the Saturday project presentations.
