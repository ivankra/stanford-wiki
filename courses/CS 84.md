## Syllabus

The [schedule](http://robots.stanford.edu/cs84/schedule.html) runs ten Mondays from March 30 and moves outward from the individual to the organization:

1. **Weeks 1–4**: introduction; empathy as "Relationship Baseline"; building trust; convincing and influencing.
2. **Weeks 5–7**: dealing with conflict and giving feedback; decision making and communication; managing people.
3. **Weeks 8–10**: "Managing with a Microphone — Large scale leadership"; one-to-many relationships and "being genuine at scale"; a final class of student feedback.

The catalog adds what the site doesn't: the course aims at "how to run effective meetings or how to work in multi-disciplinary teams", for anyone who wants "to hit the ground running and be highly effective from your first day at work".
