# CS 198: Teaching Computer Science

The course that staffs the introductory sequence: section leaders take a [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md) discussion section of their own while meeting weekly to learn how to teach it. Admission is by application and interview, and the same system — `cs198.stanford.edu` — runs section sign-ups and grading for the courses it serves.

## Materials

Nothing the course teaches is public. Its site is a single-page application whose landing page describes the program — about a hundred section leaders staffing [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md), [CS 106B](CS%20106B.md) and [CS 106L](CS%20106L.md) — and offers the application; everything past that needs the sign-in. [CS 198B](CS%20198B.md), the follow-on, publishes its schedule and decks.

- **Syllabus**: none. The landing page gives the program's size and the topics section leaders cover, and links the application with its deadline, but no schedule or syllabus was found on the site or anywhere else.
- **Slides**, **Videos**: nothing found.
- **Notes**: the section-leader guide wiki on `code.stanford.edu` redirects to a GitLab sign-in.
- **Assignments**: inside the gated application, which is also the section sign-up and grading system for the courses it staffs.
- **Solutions**, **Exams**, **Projects**: none; the work is leading a section and the weekly meeting.
- **Repo**: none found.

## Syllabus

No public schedule. The catalog describes the shape: "Students lead a discussion section of 106A while learning how to teach a programming language at the introductory level. Focus is on teaching skills, techniques, and course specifics." Weekly meetings pair the teaching craft with the [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md) material being taught that week; [CS 198B](CS%20198B.md), the follow-on for [CS 106B](CS%20106B.md) section leaders, publishes its own version of that schedule.

## Prerequisites

- Application and interview. The catalog points applicants at the course site.

## Related

- [CS 198B](CS%20198B.md): the follow-on for leading [CS 106B](CS%20106B.md) sections, which does publish its slides and schedule.
- [CS 298](CS%20298.md): a seminar on teaching CS in general, from course planning to assignments, lecturing and grading; this course trains section leaders for [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md).
