# CS 198: Teaching Computer Science

The course that staffs the introductory sequence: section leaders take a [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md) discussion section of their own while meeting weekly to learn how to teach it. Admission is by application and interview, and the same system — `cs198.stanford.edu` — runs section sign-ups and grading for the courses it serves.

## Materials

Nothing readable is public. The course's address is a single-page application behind a sign-in; a plain fetch of it returns an empty page, and the strings in its bundle are section-management forms rather than teaching material.

- **Syllabus**, **Assignments**: inside the gated application.
- **Videos**: none found.
- **Exams**, **Projects**: none.

## Syllabus

No public schedule. The catalog describes the shape: "Students lead a discussion section of 106A while learning how to teach a programming language at the introductory level. Focus is on teaching skills, techniques, and course specifics." Weekly meetings pair the teaching craft with the [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md) material being taught that week; [CS 198B](CS%20198B.md), the follow-on for [CS 106B](CS%20106B.md) section leaders, publishes its own version of that schedule.

- Application and interview. The catalog points applicants at the course site.
