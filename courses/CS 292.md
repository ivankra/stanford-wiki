# CS 292: AI and Data for Science

A weekly research seminar where researchers, mostly from Stanford, talk about AI models and scientific datasets, from biology and medicine to neuroscience and social behavior. Many speakers use Stanford's Marlowe GPU cluster. Autumn 2026 is its first quarter. Talks are in person only and open to everyone, including people outside Stanford. It's organized by Emmanuel Candès, Balasubramanian Narasimhan, Brian Wandell and Gordon Wetzstein.

## Materials

The [schedule](https://aida4sci.stanford.edu/schedule.html) lists every talk with its speaker, room, title and abstract. No slides or recordings are posted, and the talks aren't streamed. The [archive](https://aida4sci.stanford.edu/past.html) will keep each quarter's schedule once it ends.

## Syllabus

Ten Wednesday talks, September 23 to December 2:

1. Baccus: interpretable CNN models of retina and early visual cortex
2. Wetzstein: from generative video world models to world agents
3. Gevaert: multimodal modeling and imputation for precision medicine
4. Langlotz: training methods for a medical imaging foundation model
5. Kundaje: decoding regulatory DNA with deep learning
6. Yamins: large-scale models at the neuroscience-AI intersection
7. Li: multimodal foundation models for precision medicine
8. Pentland: detecting coordinated behavior across encrypted domains
9. Tolias: title TBA
10. Guibas: title TBA
