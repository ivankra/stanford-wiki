# CS 193T: Thinking with AI

An AI course for the whole university rather than the department: a weekly tutorial with a TA who follows one student's work all quarter, assignments aimed at whatever that student actually has to produce, and a term project that is a working agentic system they can show an employer. Its syllabus is explicit that it isn't selling anything — "we're not purveyors of mindless pro-AI hype".

## Syllabus

From the [published topic list](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs193t/cs193t.1272/course/syllabus): how AI really works; advanced prompting; probing models' strengths and weaknesses; agentic AI and reusable workflows; two guest panels, one on research use cases and one on industry; educational AI tools and reflective self-governance of AI use; societal impacts; building audit and accountability systems; and efficiency, tokens and budget management.

The tools are named rather than abstracted: Claude Chat, Cowork and Code; Gemini Enterprise, NotebookLM, Deep Research and Code Assist; ChatGPT, ChatGPTEdu and Codex; Microsoft 365 and GitHub Copilot; and open-weight models including Deepseek, Mistral and Llama. Concerns about bias, copyright, labor and environmental cost are stated as part of the material, alongside "emotional manipulation and impaired learning" as individual risks.
