# CS 110A: Problem Solving Lab for CS110

The ACE section for [CS 110](CS%20110.md): one unit, a weekly two-hour lab in a small group. It ran five quarters and ended when its parent course did.

No topic list is published. The section followed [CS 110](CS%20110.md) week by week, giving additional problem-solving practice in a small group, in the pattern of the program's other labs.
