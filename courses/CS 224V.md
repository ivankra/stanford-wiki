# CS 224V: Agentic AI

A project course on making LLM agents dependable. Most lectures are built around one named system and the kind of hallucination it targets. The final project is 65% of the grade, enrollment is limited (waitlist only), and attendance is mandatory.

## Syllabus

Eighteen sessions: the lectures below, one on open research projects students can join, three for project pitches and proposals, and final presentations.

1. Introduction: why LLMs hallucinate; computational thinking
2. RAG without hallucination over free text (WikiChat)
3. Research agents: open-domain knowledge curation (STORM, Co-STORM)
4. Structured and hybrid data: semantic parsing and database queries
5. SUQL: a query language combining retrieval with SQL
6. Reactive agents for knowledge-graph queries in SPARQL (SPINACH)
7. Deep research across large document sets: schematization and reconciliation (SLIDERS)
8. Task-oriented agents specified in Genie Worksheet
9. Insight discovery over large tables (DataSTORM)
10. Accountable AI: constraints as SMT, a theorem prover as decision maker (SatIR, VERDICT)
11. Coding agents: LLM, tools and harness
12. Vision-language models for transcribing historical documents
13. Training instruction-following LLMs

The reading list adds meta-agent optimization (ADAS, DSPy) and LLM evaluation.
