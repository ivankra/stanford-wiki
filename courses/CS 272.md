# CS 272: Introduction to Biomedical Informatics Research Methodology

The capstone of Stanford's Biomedical Data Science program: despite the name it is mostly a build, with student teams conceiving, specifying, implementing, evaluating and reporting a piece of biomedical software in one quarter. Teams form in week one, so the catalog strongly recommends attending it. The department says the class has to date been offered on campus only.

## Materials

The course has no website: Canvas is its only site, and the syllabus it files each term is restricted to Stanford or to enrolled students. Nothing it produces, including the capstone reports, is published anywhere.

- **Syllabus**: on [Canvas course 222729](https://canvas.stanford.edu/courses/222729), which redirects to the SUNet login gateway; the syllabus repository marks the upload Stanford-only. Every Spring back to 2017 is the same or narrower, Spring 2022 and Spring 2023 being enrolled-students-only; Winter 2022 uploaded none.
- **Slides**, **Notes**, **Videos**, **Assignments**, **Solutions**, **Repo**: nothing found for any offering, under any CS, BMI/BIOMEDIN, BIOE, BMDS or GENE host pattern. The department's own list of core course websites links BMDS 215 but not this course, and the lead instructor's "Course Websites" list points 212 only at its catalog entry.
- **Exams**: none; the deliverables are a written proposal, peer reviews, status reports and a final report.
- **Projects**: the quarter's work is a team software project, and no team's report, repository or demo from any offering is public.

## Syllabus

From the catalog description; no public syllabus.

1. Conceiving, designing and specifying a team software project in biomedicine
2. Written proposals and peer review; status reports; final reports
3. Implementation and evaluation of the project
4. Research reproducibility
5. Software engineering basics, with guest lectures from professional biomedical informatics systems builders on project management

## Prerequisites

- One biomedical informatics course: BIOMEDIN 210, 214, 215, 217 or 260 — the BMI program's own core and methods courses, none of which carries a CS number in the Spring 2026 catalog.
- Consent of the instructor. Preference goes to BMI graduate students, and students in the Biomedical Data Science program must take this core course for a letter grade.

## Related

- [CS 210B](CS%20210B.md): the CS department's software capstone, built for a corporate partner over two quarters; this one is a one-quarter biomedical research-software project.
- [CS 274](CS%20274.md): the algorithms and representations course taught by the same lead instructor; this one teaches the process of building and reporting research software rather than a subject.
