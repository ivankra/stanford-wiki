# CS 272: Introduction to Biomedical Informatics Research Methodology

The capstone of Stanford's Biomedical Data Science program: despite the name it is mostly a build, with student teams conceiving, specifying, implementing, evaluating and reporting a piece of biomedical software in one quarter. Teams form in week one, so the catalog strongly recommends attending it. The department says the class has to date been offered on campus only.

## Syllabus

From the catalog description; no public syllabus.

1. Conceiving, designing and specifying a team software project in biomedicine
2. Written proposals and peer review; status reports; final reports
3. Implementation and evaluation of the project
4. Research reproducibility
5. Software engineering basics, with guest lectures from professional biomedical informatics systems builders on project management

## Prerequisites

- One biomedical informatics course: BIOMEDIN 210, 214, 215, 217 or 260 — the BMI program's own core and methods courses, none of which carries a CS number in the Spring 2026 catalog.
- Consent of the instructor. Preference goes to BMI graduate students, and students in the Biomedical Data Science program must take this core course for a letter grade.
