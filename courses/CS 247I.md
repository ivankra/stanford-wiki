# CS 247I: Design for Understanding

One of the CS 247 studio family, and the one with a rotating subject: each quarter picks "a different challenging topic to explore and explain", with fake news, electoral politics and gender among past choices. The design problem is comprehension itself — building systems, information and interfaces that make a contested subject understandable.
