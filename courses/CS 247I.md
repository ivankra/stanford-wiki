# CS 247I: Design for Understanding

One of the CS 247 studio family, and the one with a rotating subject: each quarter picks "a different challenging topic to explore and explain", with fake news, electoral politics and gender among past choices. The design problem is comprehension itself — building systems, information and interfaces that make a contested subject understandable.

## Materials

The course taught nothing publicly and published all of its output: no syllabus, no slides, no reading list, but every Autumn 2021 team's finished site is still served at [`web.stanford.edu/class/cs247i/projects/F21/`](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs247i/projects/F21/).

- **Projects**: eight teams, and the set answers the question the catalog leaves open — this quarter's "challenging topic" was **student mental health and digital wellbeing**. [Unmasking Student Anxiety](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs247i/projects/F21/team2/) is a choose-your-own-adventure game about returning to campus after the pandemic, with two print-and-play card games beside it; [Haven](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs247i/projects/F21/team3/) explains high-functioning anxiety from interviews with "dozens of our peers"; [Educate with Empathy](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs247i/projects/F21/team5/CS247-Website/) pairs an explainer with board games; [beacon](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs247i/projects/F21/team8/) and the [Friendship Laboratory](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs247i/projects/F21/team7/) take the social side. Two more, [Luna](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs247i/projects/F21/team1/) (a React app on sleep) and [The History of You](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs247i/projects/F21/team4/), load a script bundle and render blank from this path; team 6 is unbuilt Next.js source.
- **Syllabus**: closed. The class directory holds `projects/` and nothing else, `cs247i.stanford.edu` does not resolve, and the one syllabus that exists — Autumn 2021's, in Canvas — is marked Stanford-only. **Slides**, **assignments**: nothing.

## Syllabus

No public syllabus exists; the catalog entry is the only source. It states the format: "Complex problems require nuanced design approaches. In this project-based hands-on course, students explore the design of systems, information and interface for human use. Each quarter we pick a different challenging topic to explore and explain." The Autumn 2021 projects show how that cashes out: teams spend the quarter on one contested, hard-to-discuss subject and ship an explanatory artefact — a game, a site, a card deck — rather than a prototype of a product.

## Prerequisites

- An HCI design course: [CS 147](CS%20147.md) "or equivalent", as the catalog puts it. It is the standing prerequisite across the CS 247 studio family.
