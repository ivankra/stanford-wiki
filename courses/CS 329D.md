# CS 329D: Machine Learning Under Distributional Shifts

The course starts from a gap in the benchmark story: accuracy on image classification, speech recognition and question answering has climbed year on year, while the same models "fail consistently on atypical examples and domains not contained within the training data". It surveys where shift arises and then gives it theoretical treatment — characterizations and learning bounds — rather than treating robustness as an engineering patch. The instructor's own research is in distributionally robust optimization, which is the technical centre of gravity.
