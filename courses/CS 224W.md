# CS 224W: Machine Learning with Graphs

Representation learning on graphs: node embeddings, graph neural networks and their theory, then heterogeneous and knowledge graphs, recommender systems, relational databases and graph generation. Written homework, coding Colabs, one exam and a project.

## Syllabus

Nineteen lectures:

1. Introduction; node embeddings (DeepWalk, node2vec)
2. Graph neural networks: a general perspective, augmentation and training
3. Theory of GNNs and designing more powerful graph encoders
4. Graph transformers
5. Heterogeneous graphs and knowledge graphs
6. GNNs for recommender systems
7. Relational deep learning and its advanced architectures
8. Advanced GNN topics; foundation models for knowledge graphs
9. Frontiers in graph AI (two lectures)
10. Deep generative models for graphs; conclusion

## Prerequisites

The catalog asks for CS 109 and any introductory machine-learning course. The site asks for programming at the level of CS 107 or CS 145, basic probability (CS 109 or STATS 116) and basic linear algebra (MATH 51 is "much more than necessary").
