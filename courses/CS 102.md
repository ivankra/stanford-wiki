# CS 102: Working with Data - Tools and Techniques

Aimed squarely outside the major, and deliberately broad rather than deep: "Tools and techniques are hands-on but at a cursory level, providing a basis for future exploration and application." It ran for the last time in Spring 2020, and the instructor moved the material to her own site rather than let it lapse.

## Materials

The course page survives with its description, logistics and evaluation scheme; the teaching material moved off it.

- **Syllabus**: the [site](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs102/) — description, logistics, the evaluation scheme and a calendar.
- **Videos**: closed. Lectures were on Zoom with links on Canvas; bootcamp recordings went to Canvas too.
- **Assignments**: five assignments, two projects and two exams are described but not posted.
- **Code**: the instructor states the Jupyter notebooks and datasets "are being kept current" on [professorwidom.org](http://www.professorwidom.org/).
- **Slides**, **Notes**, **Solutions**: not found.

## Syllabus

From the catalog and the site: "data analysis techniques including databases, data mining, machine learning, and data visualization; data analysis tools including spreadsheets, Tableau, relational databases and SQL, Python, and R; introduction to network analysis and unstructured data."

Assessment was "5 assignments, 2 projects, and 2 exams… 33.3% each". Optional Wednesday bootcamps "provide extra setup help for the tools we are using, and additional programming examples for those who may have a weaker background in programming".

## Prerequisites
