# CS 198B: Additional Topics in Teaching Computer Science

One unit for section leaders crossing from [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md) into [CS 106B](CS%20106B.md): each week pairs a full-group workshop on teaching craft with a small-group practice teach on the week's hard topic — ADTs, recursion, backtracking, pointers.

## Syllabus

From the published [schedule](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs198b/cs198b.1266/schedule/), each week pairs a teaching theme with the [CS 106B](CS%20106B.md) topic being practiced:

1. **Week 2**: teaching as creative design; containers and ADTs.
2. **Week 3**: turning observation into experiment in teaching; big-O and recursion — first practice teach.
3. **Week 4**: designing a creative section; backtracking — second practice teach.
4. **Week 5**: inclusive section leading; class design — third practice teach.
5. **Week 6**: assessments; dynamic memory and pointers — fourth practice teach.

Surveys punctuate the quarter (beginning, section observation, midpoint), and the course ends in a final project.
