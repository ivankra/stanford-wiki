# CS 137A: Principles of Robot Autonomy I

The undergraduate listing of the Autonomous Systems Lab's autonomy course: planning, perception and decision-making for mobile robots, with every assignment run against a local ROS2 install and a staff-written textbook that is free on arXiv. [CS 237A](CS%20237A.md) is the graduate version, with its own lecture slot and its own site.

## Materials

Every offering keeps its own site, with the syllabus, the decks, the homework and a past midterm with solutions, and the textbook is a free arXiv pre-print. The recordings are the one thing held back.

- **Syllabus**: the [syllabus PDF](https://stanfordasl.github.io/PoRA-I/aa174a_aut2627/pdfs/syllabus.pdf), the site's dated schedule, and a [pre-knowledge assessment](https://stanfordasl.github.io/PoRA-I/aa174a_aut2627/pdfs/pre_knowledge_assessment.pdf) for anyone judging whether they have the background.
- **Slides**: the schedule names 20 lecture decks and 4 section decks; [lecture 1](https://stanfordasl.github.io/PoRA-I/aa174a_aut2627/pdfs/lecture/lecture_1.pdf) and section 1 are up and the rest 404 until their date. [Autumn 2025's 19](https://stanfordasl.github.io/PoRA-I/aa174a_aut2526/) and Autumn 2024's are complete.
- **Notes**: the staff textbook, [*Principles of Robot Autonomy*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.03496) (Gammelli, Lorenzetti, Luo, Zardini and Pavone, pre-print 2026), with per-lecture notes beside the later decks.
- **Videos**: the site sends students to Canvas for "course content, recordings, and announcements".
- **Assignments**: [Homework 1](https://stanfordasl.github.io/PoRA-I/aa174a_aut2627/pdfs/homework/hw1.pdf) on controlling nonholonomic wheeled robots, with a LaTeX template, and a [paper-review guide](https://stanfordasl.github.io/PoRA-I/aa174a_aut2627/pdfs/paper_review.pdf); the previous offerings' full sets are still served.
- **Solutions**: the 2023 midterm comes with [a full solution set](https://stanfordasl.github.io/PoRA-I/aa174a_aut2627/pdfs/exams/AA174A_Midterm_Sol_2023.pdf). No homework answers are published.
- **Exams**: [the 2023 midterm](https://stanfordasl.github.io/PoRA-I/aa174a_aut2627/pdfs/exams/AA174A_Midterm_2023.pdf), carried by every offering's site. Autumn 2025's [midterm](https://github.com/PrinciplesofRobotAutonomy/AA274A_midterm_FA25) and [final](https://github.com/PrinciplesofRobotAutonomy/AA274a_final_FA25) have public code bundles but no public paper.
- **Projects**: submitted through Gradescope; nothing published.
- **Repo**: the [Principles of Robot Autonomy](https://github.com/PrinciplesofRobotAutonomy) organization, with a starter repository per homework per offering — `AA274a-HW1-F24` through `-F26` — the section and example repositories and the TurtleBot stack.

## Syllabus

The site's description sets the sequence: trajectory optimization; robot motion planning; robot perception, localization and simultaneous localization and mapping; and state machines for behavior — with ROS used throughout for demonstrations and the weekly two-hour section. Students taking the course for 4 units also write a paper review by the end of the quarter.

## Prerequisites

- Programming: [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md) or equivalent; the homeworks are in Python against ROS2.
- Mathematics: CME 100 or equivalent for calculus and linear algebra, CME 106 or equivalent for probability.
