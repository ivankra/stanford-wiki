# CS 247A: Design for Artificial Intelligence

The AI version of the CS 247 studio family, building on [CS 147](CS%20147.md). Teams spend the whole course in studio, doing research, prototyping and interface design for AI products: what it means to design for AI, what human-centered AI is, and how to make AI systems that work for the people involved. In the event of a waitlist, admission is by an application handed out on the first day.

## Syllabus

No syllabus is published. The catalog describes advanced methods and tools for research, prototyping and user interface design, applied to human-centered AI experiences: the constraints, opportunities and specialized processes that make AI systems work for the humans involved, responsibly and ethically.

Every CS 247 version covers the same six topics, adapted to its design context:

1. Interaction design: affordances, task analysis, user flows, personas, rapid iterative testing
2. Information architecture: metadata, information scent, content strategy, card sorting
3. Interface and graphic design: visual principles, color, typography, layout, usability testing
4. Sketching and modeling
5. Process capability: needfinding, sensemaking, ideation, iteration, evaluation
6. Ethics: participatory design, research ethics, inclusive interface design

## Prerequisites

[CS 147](CS%20147.md) or equivalent design-thinking background, per the catalog and the family site. No programming level is required; Figma familiarity is recommended.
