# CS 325B: Data for Sustainable Development

Not a lecture course: one week of background, then seven weeks where teams of three present their own project every Tuesday, then two weeks of writing. The problems are measurement problems — estimating building heights from Sentinel imagery to infer population and energy demand, scoring road quality — where the official statistics are missing and the satellite record isn't. Grading is explicit that accuracy isn't the target: "most projects will be risky and not guaranteed to work", so the marks go to method and communication. Admission is by application, capped at 24.

## Syllabus

[Three phases](https://ermongroup.github.io/cs325b/) over ten weeks.

1. Week 1: the SDGs, and an overview of the common geospatial datasets and tools.
2. Weeks 2-8: weekly group presentations, each with its own brief — a literature review and benchmark survey in week 2, data visualization in week 3, then simple models in week 4, including a human baseline where group members attempt the task by hand.
3. Weeks 9-10: a peer feedback session on a draft, then final presentations and the paper.
