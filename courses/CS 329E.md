# CS 329E: Machine Learning on Embedded Systems

A lab course built around a Raspberry Pi 5 on the desk: each week deploys something — an LLM, an image classifier, Whisper, a translation model — then makes it fit through quantization, LoRA or distillation. Half the grade is a project measured against its own proposal, with results weighted separately from the write-up.

## Materials

Nothing sits behind a Stanford login: the schedule, the grading rules and all 8 labs are public, and the two Autumn offerings' schedules and their framework-building assignment are too, because the site is built from a public repository. What the site never publishes is the papers it asks students to lead discussions on, or a deck for any week.

- **Syllabus**: the [class site](https://ee292d.github.io/) carries the 9-week schedule of lecture and lab topics, a paragraph on each of the 8 labs, the grading split (labs 40%, reading and participation 10%, project 50%) and the project deadlines. [Autumn 2021's](https://github.com/ee292d/ee292d.github.io/tree/aa9c76510c) and [Autumn 2022's](https://github.com/ee292d/ee292d.github.io/tree/48db270145) survive as commits, with their own splits and homework lists.
- **Slides**, **Notes**: neither. Reading reflections and leading discussion "on each paper" carry 10% of the grade, and no paper list is posted.
- **Videos**: no source mentions recording, and nothing turned up under either code.
- **Assignments**: the 8 labs are in [github.com/ee292d/labs](https://github.com/ee292d/labs), `lab0` (set up your Raspberry Pi 5) through `lab7` (understand your data), by way of running an LLM locally, image classification and fine-tuning, object and pose location, Whisper speech recognition, translation and LLM fine-tuning. Each is a README with its Python scripts, notebooks, model-download scripts and sample images or audio. The Autumn offerings instead set 2 or 3 homework sets, of which the inference-framework one ships its skeleton as [ee292d/udnn](https://github.com/ee292d/udnn).

- **Repo**: the lab repository is the starter code, and `udnn` was the Autumn offerings'.

## Syllabus

Nine weeks ([class site](https://ee292d.github.io/)): edge ML and ML frameworks, with Pi setup as lab 0; CNN and transformer architectures, running an LLM locally; training and fine-tuning — transfer and multi-task learning — with image classification and object location labs; data formats and model quantization, including quantization-aware training, alongside Whisper speech recognition and translation labs; PEFT (LoRA, adapters) and knowledge distillation, with a fine-tune-and-deploy lab; hardware for DNNs and how accelerator design shapes models; MLOps pipelines and observability; a guest lecture; then final project presentations.
