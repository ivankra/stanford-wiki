# CS 237A: Principles of Robot Autonomy I

The "autonomy stack" behind self-driving cars, drones and mobile robots: planning and control, perception, localization and SLAM. Weekly two-hour lab sections put the homework algorithms on real robots with ROS, and the labs carry more of the grade than the homeworks.

## Syllabus

Four modules of roughly equal length:

1. **Motion control and planning**: maps and SE(2)/SE(3) transforms; collision checking, C-space, A*; RRT and RRT*; trajectory optimization; trajectory following (PID, LQR, gain-scheduled LQR).
2. **Robotic perception**: IMU, lidar, cameras, RGB-D; point clouds and ICP; pinhole cameras and calibration; structure from motion, features, RANSAC; learning-based and semantic perception.
3. **Localization and SLAM**: factor graphs, pose-graph optimization, bundle adjustment; occupancy mapping and frontier exploration; Kalman filters (EKF, UKF); particle filters and Monte Carlo localization; EKF localization and object tracking.
4. **Advanced topics**: imitation learning, VLAs, 3D Gaussian splatting for sim2real, world models.

Labs run from installing ROS through RViz, a Turtlebot, a heading controller, navigation to a goal, object detection and frontier exploration.
