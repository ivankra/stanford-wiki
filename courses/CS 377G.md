# CS 377G: Designing Serious Games

A hands-on game-design studio: through lectures and extensive readings, students learn the fundamentals of game design and design and prototype games that teach, aimed at social change and civic engagement.

## Syllabus

The catalog gives no unit list. The Autumn 2018 outline ran as short two-week team assignments, then a final four weeks polishing one game:

1. Game design fundamentals
2. Games for learning (tabletop)
3. Persuasive games (Twine or Inform7)
4. Games that model complexity (tabletop or a game engine)
5. Refining one chosen game, ending in an open house

Learning goals in 2018 included playtesting and iterative development, and balancing learning goals with fun.
