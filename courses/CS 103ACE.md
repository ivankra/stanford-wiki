# CS 103ACE: Mathematical Problem-solving Strategies

The ACE section for [CS 103](CS%20103.md), and the most generous of the ACE sites: every week of the Spring 2025 offering published its slides, its worksheet and full solutions, which is more worked proof practice than [CS 103](CS%20103.md) itself puts online.

- **Projects**, **Repo**: none.

## Syllabus

The published sections track [CS 103](CS%20103.md) a week behind its lectures: sets and proof methods, propositional and first-order logic, functions and set-theory proofs, induction, DFAs and NFAs and regular languages, Turing machines and decidability, and finally verifiers and P versus NP. Midterm weeks replace the section with supplementary handouts — graphs and the pigeonhole principle in one, nonregular languages, regular expressions and context-free grammars in the other.

## Prerequisites

- Concurrent enrollment in [CS 103](CS%20103.md) and the instructor's consent.
