# CS 343S: Domain-Specific Language Design Studio

Three weeks of lectures on building external and internal DSLs, then seven weeks of studio: brainstorming, group critique of each other's language ideas, interim presentations, a workshop on writing documentation, and a final demo. Taught by four PhD-student instructors, with knitting patterns and geometric constructions used as first-class examples of what a language can be for.

## Materials

The whole studio is public — labs, slides and the live-coding archives.

- **Syllabus**: the [course page](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs343s/) carries the dated schedule with instructor and topic per class, the assignment weights and every deadline.
- **Slides**: decks for the lecture weeks — [external DSLs](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs343s/slides/external.pdf), [internal DSLs](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs343s/slides/internal.pdf), [graphics DSLs](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs343s/slides/graphics.pdf).
- **Assignments**: three labs as zips — [external](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs343s/assignments/external.zip) (15% of the grade), [internal](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs343s/assignments/internal.zip), [clinic on binary formats](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs343s/assignments/clinic.zip) — and the [project handout](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs343s/assignments/project.pdf) with its subparts.
- **Code**: live-coding archives from the implementation lectures, e.g. [external1](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs343s/live_coding/external1.zip).
- **Exams**: none; submissions go through Gradescope.

## Syllabus

Ten weeks ([schedule](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs343s/)): external DSLs over three sessions, from design through implementation with live coding; internal DSLs embedded in a host language; graphics DSLs — images, simulations, knitting, diagrams; a clinic on binary formats paired with project brainstorming; then two weeks of group feedback on project ideas, interim project presentations with critique, a lecture on documentation and a documentation writers' workshop, and final project presentations in the last week.

## Prerequisites

- None stated in the catalog. The labs assume comfort implementing parsers and interpreters, at the level of a compilers or programming-languages course such as [CS 242](CS%20242.md).
