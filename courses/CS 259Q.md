# CS 259Q: Quantum Computing

The introductory quantum computing course, from single qubits to Shor's algorithm and error correction.

## Syllabus

From the catalog description:

1. Qubits, entanglement and non-local correlations
2. Quantum gates, circuits and compilation algorithms
3. Basic quantum algorithms: Simon's and Grover's
4. Shor's factoring algorithm and the hidden subgroup problem
5. Hamiltonian simulation
6. Stabilizer circuits, the Gottesman-Knill theorem, and basics of quantum error correction

## Prerequisites

Linear algebra and discrete probability, plus algorithms or quantum mechanics (or both).

## Related

- [CS 258](CS%20258.md): quantum algorithms as attacks on classical cryptography, and quantum-resistant and quantum protocols; this course covers quantum computing itself.
