# CS 278: Social Computing

How to design the systems people socialize through — social media, online communities, collaboration tools — and the social science that explains why they thrive or empty out. Teams spend the quarter launching a social system of their own and recruiting real users to it.

## Syllabus

Five units over ten weeks, per the [schedule](https://cs278.stanford.edu/).

1. **Something from Nothing**: going viral, bustling streets and ghost towns, norms, the cold start problem, social media and wellbeing.
2. **Oh #@&%, It Got Big**: growing pains, feed ranking.
3. **We Work**: strong and weak ties, wisdom of the crowd, peer production, group collaboration.
4. **Don't Feed the Trolls**: anti-social computing, content moderation, misinformation; the exam falls in week 8.
5. **AIs in Social Environments**: social AI agents, governance, unintended consequences.

Assessment: assignments 20%, exam 30%, team project 30%, reading memos 10%, participation 10%. In Assignment 3 the class writes, remixes and votes on exam questions; the top-voted ones become the bank the exam is sampled from.
