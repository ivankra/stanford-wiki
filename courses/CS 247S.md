# CS 247S: Service Design with AI

A studio course in the CS 247 design-studio family, building on CS 147. Teams design services that must work for customers, providers and other stakeholders across digital and physical touchpoints, paired with on-campus clients and learning to use AI tools responsibly along the way.

## Materials

Nothing course-specific is public: there is no CS 247S site. The shared [CS 247 site](https://cs247.stanford.edu/) has a one-line blurb for the course, the curriculum overview common to every CS 247 version, and an FAQ.

## Syllabus

The catalog describes advanced methods for design research, prototyping and interface design, integrating AI tools and designing for AI integration. Student teams take on a real design problem for a campus client; past clients include the Stanford Office of Risk, Stanford Hospital and the Cantor Art Museum.

Every CS 247 version covers the same six topics, adapted to its design context:

1. Interaction design: affordances, task analysis, user flows, personas, rapid iterative testing
2. Information architecture: metadata, information scent, content strategy, card sorting
3. Interface and graphic design: visual principles, color, typography, layout, usability testing
4. Sketching and modeling
5. Process capability: needfinding, sensemaking, ideation, iteration, evaluation
6. Ethics: participatory design, research ethics, inclusive interface design

## Prerequisites

CS 147 or equivalent design-thinking background, per the family site. No programming level is required; Figma familiarity is recommended.
