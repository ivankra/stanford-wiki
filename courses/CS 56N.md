# CS 56N: Great Discoveries and Inventions in Computing

Taught by John Hennessy, who built much of what it covers. The seminar works through the field's foundational results and the engineering that followed: "What is computable? How can information be securely communicated? How do computers" work at all.

## Syllabus

The catalog describes the course: "This seminar will explore some of both the great discoveries that underlie computer science and the inventions that have produced the remarkable advances in computing technology. Key questions we will explore include: What is computable? How can information be securely communicated? How do computers fundamentally work? What makes computers fast? Our exploration will look both at the principles behind the discoveries and inventions, as well as the history and the people involved in those events. Some exposure to programming is required."
