# CS 12SI: Spatial Computing Workshop

One unit, one evening a week, alternating hands-on work sessions with guest lectures from industry and academia. The Spring 2024 offering was built around the Apple Vision Pro, which had shipped that February.

## Syllabus

From the [posted table](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs12si/):

1. Introduction — try out headsets and brainstorm ideas.
2. Guest lecture: Marco Cavallo on spatial data visualization. Deliverable: project proposal.
3. Implementation lecture: Xcode and SwiftUI basics for visionOS.
4. Guest lecture: Jonathan Edelman on design methodologies.

The remaining weeks continue the same alternation of work sessions, guests and deliverables, ending in team prototypes.

The workshop's stated emphasis is "prototyping techniques that allow for the rapid iteration and testing of XR environments", covering "user-centered design principles and the distinct challenges and opportunities inherent in design for spatial computing".
