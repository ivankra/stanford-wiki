# CS 193P: iOS Application Development

The most openly published course in the wiki: every lecture is on YouTube, and the site exists to serve people who are not enrolled. One application — CodeBreaker in Spring 2025 — is built across the quarter, so the lectures are a single construction rather than a topic list.

The staff are explicit about the limits: "we aren't able to provide any support for you as you watch these."

## Syllabus

From the [lecture list](https://cs193p.stanford.edu/):

1. **Getting Started with SwiftUI** — Xcode and SwiftUI basics, especially Views.
2. **More SwiftUI Basics** — View modifiers; construction starts on the quarter's application, CodeBreaker.
3. **Model and UI; Swift Type System** — separating Model (logic and data) from UI, and the fundamentals of Swift's type system.
4. **Lectures 4–16** carry that application forward through the material the catalog lists: functional and object-oriented programming, computer-human interfaces, graphics and animation, reactive interfaces, MVI and MVVM, object-oriented databases, networking, and multi-threading for interactive performance.
