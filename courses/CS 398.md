# CS 398: Computational Education

Piech's course on the algorithms behind teaching at scale: knowledge tracing to model what a student knows, generative grading to mark work nobody has seen before, teachable agents that learn from the student. The catalog's stated goal is not to survey the field but to leave you "ready to lead your own computation education research project".
