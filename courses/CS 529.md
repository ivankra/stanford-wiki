# CS 529: Robotics and Autonomous Systems Seminar

The campus robotics seminar with a course number attached. One unit, satisfactory/no credit, repeatable, and the only requirement is turning up: seven attendance forms over the quarter, or paragraphs on the talks you missed. It runs every quarter, and the talks themselves are open to anyone on campus whether or not they register.

## Materials

The seminar's own record is fully public: the dated speaker table with an abstract under every talk, back to 2018, and a syllabus PDF kept for each quarter. The one thing held back is the talks themselves. Edited recordings go to a public playlist only once student images have been blurred out of them, which leaves 4 of Spring 2024's 11 talks on YouTube, while registered students get the whole set through Canvas.

- **Syllabus**: the [Spring 2024 syllabus](https://stanfordasl.github.io/robotics_seminar/assets/doc/Syllabus_AA289_sp2024.pdf) gives the room, the dates and the credit rule (7 of 9 talks attended live, a one-paragraph review on Canvas for anything beyond 2 missed). The [archive](https://stanfordasl.github.io/robotics_seminar/archive/) carries the speaker table, with each talk's full abstract, for every quarter from Winter 2018 on. The [class page](https://stanfordasl.github.io/robotics_seminar/class/) holds the standing credit and recording policy. Every offering back to Winter 2020 keeps its own syllabus PDF under `assets/doc/`, unlinked from the site but public.
- **Slides**: none. Speakers bring their own and the seminar posts no decks.
- **Notes**: none; nothing is written up beyond the abstracts.
- **Videos**: the [Stanford Online playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rMeercb-kvGLUrOq4HR6BZD) the seminar keeps adding to, 123 videos reaching back to 2016. It holds 4 of Spring 2024's 11 talks — Leung, Banerjee, Sung and Linares — and the syllabus sends enrolled students to Canvas under "Panopto Course Videos" for the rest.
- **Assignments**, **Solutions**, **Exams**, **Projects**, **Repo**: none. Credit is attendance alone, and the seminar sets nothing else.

## Syllabus

[Ten Friday talks](https://stanfordasl.github.io/robotics_seminar/archive/) in Spring 2024, all 12:30 in Skilling Auditorium.

- Apr 5, Andreas Krause (ETH Zurich): safe Bayesian optimization and model-based RL in the physical world.
- Apr 12, Karen Leung (Washington): trusted human-centric robot autonomy.
- Apr 19, Ken Goldberg (Berkeley): whether data is all you need for large robot action models.
- Apr 26, Elliot Hawkes (UCSB): physical principles of embryonic morphogenesis in robotic collectives.
- May 3, two Stanford student speakers: Somrita Banerjee on adaptation to evolving conditions, Elliot Weiss on VR headsets and vehicle safety.
- May 10, Cynthia Sung (UPenn): when design equals planning.
- May 17, Hiro Ono (NASA JPL): the EELS robot, from Mars to Enceladus.
- May 24, Richard Linares (MIT): transformer-based constraint prediction for powered descent.
- May 31, Aaron Ames (Caltech): why learning needs control.

- No Stanford-hosted site: `web.stanford.edu/class/cs529/`, `aa289/` and `engr319/` all 404, and `cs529.stanford.edu` does not resolve. The seminar runs from the Autonomous Systems Lab's GitHub Pages site.
- The home page shows only the current quarter, and links only its syllabus PDF. The whole set lives in [`assets/doc/`](https://github.com/StanfordASL/robotics_seminar/tree/master/assets/doc), named `Syllabus_AA289_<term>.pdf` up to Spring 2024 and `Syllabus_ENGR319_<term>.pdf` after; listing that directory through the GitHub API is how each offering's copy was found. A `Syllabus_AA289_wi2021.pdf` sits there too, but Winter 2021 has no CS section in the catalog and no block in the archive, so it is not in `terms_offered`.
- Every syllabus from Autumn 2022 on names `robotics.stanford.edu/seminar` as the course website. That path serves an Apache listing of CSS and images last touched in 2014, not the seminar site (checked 2026-10-02), so no `past` record records a homepage of its own.
- **Spring 2020 was dropped from `terms_offered`.** Its catalog section is TBA with no instructor, the archive has no Spring 2020 block (it jumps from Winter 2020 to Winter 2022), and `assets/doc/` holds no Spring 2020 syllabus. The line for that term page's Notes is in the migration report.
- The syllabus repository lists the Winter 2022 through Autumn 2023 uploads as Stanford-only, which is about the Canvas copy; the same documents are public on the site, so those offerings are rated from the site.
- Sources disagree on Autumn 2022's instructors: the catalog names Pavone alone, while that quarter's syllabus names Pavone and Cutkosky. The `past` record keeps the catalog's list, as the rules require.
- The site's recording notice says student images are blurred before a talk is made public, which is why only part of the series reaches YouTube.
- Playlist videos are titled by quarter only from Autumn 2025 on; older ones carry the talk title alone, and their upload dates lag the talk by months, so Spring 2024's four were identified by matching titles against the archive.
