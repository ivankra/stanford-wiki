# CS 273B: Deep Learning in Genomics and Biomedicine

Two fields that became "big data" disciplines at the same time, taken together. The course supplies the genomics itself — no prior biology is assumed — but expects you to arrive able to train a model, and treats interpreting the trained network as a goal in its own right.

## Syllabus

No syllabus is public; this is the catalog's own order.

1. Introduction to deep learning
2. Genomics and high-throughput biotechnology: what data exists and why it matters
3. Supervised, unsupervised and generative models applied to biomedical data
4. Visualizing models and extracting interpretable biological insight from them
5. Recent papers from the literature, presented and discussed, and guest lectures from experts in the field

Students work with standard deep learning frameworks and, in groups, on a final project using real-world datasets.

## Prerequisites

- Calculus and linear algebra at college level.
- Probability and statistics: CS 109 or equivalent.
- Machine learning: [CS 229](CS%20229.md) or equivalent.
- No genomics. The catalog says so explicitly; the biological background is taught in the course.
