# CS 428A: Probabilistic models of cognition: Reasoning and Learning

"How can we understand intelligent behavior as computation?" The course's answer is probabilistic programming: write a generative model of how a mind might produce a judgement, then condition on what people actually do. Topics run over causal and social reasoning, concept learning and hierarchical abstraction — the territory of Goodman's *Probabilistic Models of Cognition*.
