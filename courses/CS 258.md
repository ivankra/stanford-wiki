# CS 258: Quantum Cryptography

What quantum computers do to cryptography: quantum attacks on classical schemes, the cryptography that resists them, and protocols such as quantum money that only quantum computers make possible. Aimed at advanced undergraduates and master's students.

## Materials

No Autumn 2026 site yet. The [Autumn 2025 site](https://mzhandry.github.io/courses/2025-Fall-CS258/), same instructor, is fully public: syllabus and dated schedule, [slides](https://mzhandry.github.io/courses/2025-Fall-CS258/LN/L1.pdf) for all 19 lectures, and [five homeworks](https://mzhandry.github.io/courses/2025-Fall-CS258/HW/HW1.pdf) with their LaTeX sources. Solutions, exams and recordings aren't posted; discussion is on Ed.

## Syllabus

Autumn 2025 schedule, 19 lectures:

1. Cryptography basics, an overview of quantum mechanics
2. Quantum key distribution, quantum money
3. Quantum computing: Grover's and Shor's algorithms
4. Cryptographic group actions and Kuperberg's algorithm
5. Lattice cryptography, Regev's reduction, LWE vs dihedral hidden subgroups
6. Security proofs in post-quantum cryptography, the quantum random oracle model
7. The need for computational security; cryptography in a world where P = NP
8. Public-key quantum money; advanced topics

## Prerequisites

The catalog lists none. The Autumn 2025 site asks for linear algebra and algorithms and assumes no quantum computing, physics or cryptography.
