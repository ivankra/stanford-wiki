# CS 428B: Probabilistic Models of Cognition: Language

The language half of Goodman's probabilistic-cognition pair: pragmatics modeled as recursive social reasoning, where a listener infers what a speaker meant by reasoning about why they chose those words. The Rational Speech Acts framework is the spine, and the course works outward from it through the phenomena that make literal semantics insufficient.
