# CS 329H: Machine Learning from Human Preferences

A graduate introduction to learning from human choices, pulling together discrete choice, psychometrics, RL, information retrieval and normative theory. Graded on a research project, short written quizzes and oral exams, with mandatory attendance and weekly AI interviews.

## Materials

## Syllabus

Eighteen sessions in five units:

1. **Foundation**: choice data and Bradley–Terry; random utility models and axiomatizations; factor models.
2. **Learning**: predicting choice with MLE; Bayesian estimation and finetuning; Fisher information and optimal experimental design; RLHF; DPO; active elicitation for LLMs.
3. **Action**: assistance games; assisted vs. contextual bandits; acquisition functions and Thompson sampling; dueling bandits and preferential Bayesian optimization.
4. **Inversion**: style control and surrogates; bounded rationality and computationally tractable choice.
5. **Aggregation**: Gibbard–Satterthwaite and Arrow impossibilities; fairness and information aggregation.
