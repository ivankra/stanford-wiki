# CS 448P: Hacking the Pandemic

Stood up inside the quarter the pandemic began. Doug James gave it a venue rather than a curriculum: brief lectures, guest speakers, moderated brainstorming, and otherwise self-organised teams applying whatever they knew — algorithms, app development, HCI, remote interaction, data visualization, modelling, fabrication and 3D printing, design, games, VR, systems and networking, AI, statistics, bioinformatics — to helping people cope with COVID-19. Satisfactory/no credit, three units, one prerequisite.
