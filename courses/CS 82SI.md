# CS 82SI: Wellness in Tech: Designing an Intentional Lifestyle in a Tech-Driven World

A 1-unit workshop series, run once, in the quarter Stanford moved online. The site calls them "Workshops for Wellness in Tech".

## Syllabus

The [site's schedule](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs82si/) is the only public outline; the syllabus document behind it is not. It runs 10 Wednesday meetings in 3 rings, each a workshop with an outside speaker rather than a lecture:

1. **Inner ring — personal** (4/9-4/16): the basics of human flourishing, with Aneel Chima of the Stanford Flourishing Project; transformative tech, with Caitlin Krause of Mindful by Design.
2. **Middle ring — relationships and career** (4/23-5/7): bridging individuals, with Tia Rich of Contemplation by Design; working well in tech and online communities, with Daragh Byrne and Jeff Sheng; resilience and mindfulness in academic settings, with Denise Pope of the GSE.
3. **Outer ring — systems and society** (5/14-5/28): wellness entrepreneurship read 3 ways — AI, with Justin Lai of Atlas Mental Health; communities, with James Ehrlich of Regen Villages; applications, with Swapna Savant of Headspace for Work.
4. **Synthesis** (6/4): integrating what you have learned into your life, with John Armstrong of the Life Design Lab, then a class party.

Three reflections and a mini-project are due across the quarter, with separate late deadlines for graduating and non-graduating students.
