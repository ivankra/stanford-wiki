# CS 349D: AI Inference Infrastructure

A paper seminar on how LLMs and compound AI systems are served in production: two required papers a lecture, discussion led by two students, guest speakers from industry, and one build that runs all quarter — a minimal serving engine, in groups of 2–3.

## Materials

The site is public and carries the whole reading list; the project handouts and submissions are not.

- **Syllabus**: the [schedule](https://cs349d.stanford.edu/#schedule) lists all 19 sessions with topic, required and optional readings, and milestone deadlines, plus the [grading split](https://cs349d.stanford.edu/#lectures) (coursework 50%, leading a lecture 25%, participation 25%).
- **Assignments**: the [four Mini Serving Engine milestones](https://cs349d.stanford.edu/#coursework) are described on the site; the starter notebook, benchmark scripts and shared environment are not public, and paper summaries go through Gradescope.
- **Exams**: none; the grade is coursework, discussion leadership and participation.
- Every reading is an external link — mostly arXiv and USENIX, which open; the ACM Digital Library ones may not.
- **Extra**: the Autumn 2018 offering's site is still served from an [`old/2018/`](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs349d/old/2018/index.html) subdirectory, and it published more than the current one does — sixteen sets of discussion notes and eighteen lecture decks, in two public Google Drive folders. That offering was the cloud-computing course, so it is a different syllabus, and at eight years old it carries no rating.

## Syllabus

[Schedule](https://cs349d.stanford.edu/#schedule), 19 sessions, marked tentative.

1. Introduction; parallelism (FlexFlow, scaling transformer inference)
2. Serving engines (PagedAttention, SGLang); batching (Orca, Clipper)
3. Disaggregation (DistServe, Dynamo); speculative decoding
4. Context management and caching; efficient attention (FlashAttention, FlashInfer)
5. Hybrid LLMs; mixture of experts; load balancing and routing
6. Agentic workflows (Autellix, Parrot); memory (Cartridges, mem0)
7. Inference for reinforcement learning; multi-tenancy
8. Two project meetings, one industry guest lecture, and final presentations
