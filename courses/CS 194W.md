# CS 194W: Software Project (WIM)

[CS 194](CS%20194.md) with the writing requirement attached: the same lectures, the same team project, but the documentation the project already demands (rationale, architecture, performance indicators) is assessed as writing, and enrollment is limited to CS and EE undergraduates.

## Materials

CS 194W has no site of its own, and the shared [CS 194](CS%20194.md) page sends students to Canvas for the syllabus and the assignments, the writing assessment among them. What is public is the output: 29 of the 30 Spring 2026 team repositories in the [StanfordCS194 organization](https://github.com/StanfordCS194), which holds both courses' teams, including the written documentation this version grades.

- **Syllabus**: on Canvas. The [shared page](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs194/) gives the course description, the Monday/Wednesday/Friday lecture slot and the attendance rule, with no schedule, grading scheme or word on the writing requirement. Canvas holds a Stanford-only upload for 8 of the 13 older offerings.
- **Slides**, **Notes**, **Videos**: nothing found for any offering.
- **Assignments**: on Canvas, along with the writing milestones. The milestones and issue tracker the shared page points to, `StanfordCS194/Home`, "you can only see as a member of the organization".
- **Solutions**: not applicable to a build course, and none found.
- **Exams**: none; the deliverables are the project and its documentation.
- **Projects**: [the organization](https://github.com/StanfordCS194) lists 29 public Spring 2026 team repositories, pushed through the last week of the quarter. 11 keep a `docs/` or `wiki/` folder with the writing the course assesses: architecture notes and diagrams, a PRD, OKR and KPI write-ups, user-test plans and midpoint testing reports. No offering before Spring 2026 has a public repository, and no repository says whether its team was in this course or in [CS 194](CS%20194.md).
- **Repo**: the same 29 repositories are the teams' source. The organization's [Pages site](https://stanfordcs194.github.io/) adds only a pointer to Canvas.

## Syllabus

No public syllabus. The catalog says only: "Restricted to Computer Science and Electrical Engineering undergraduates. Writing-intensive version of CS194. Preference given to seniors." Content otherwise follows [CS 194](CS%20194.md): design, specification, coding and testing of a significant team project, documented with a rationale, key performance indicators, a weekly progress log and an architecture diagram, and demonstrated publicly at the end of the quarter.

## Prerequisites

- Probability: [CS 109](CS%20109.md).
- Algorithms: [CS 161](CS%20161.md).
- Declared in Computer Science or Electrical Engineering, as an undergraduate.
