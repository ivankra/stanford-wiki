# CS 237B: Principles of Robot Autonomy II

The second half of the robot-autonomy sequence, where [CS 237A](CS%20237A.md)'s estimation and planning give way to learning: 3 professors split 10 weeks between reinforcement learning, physical interaction with objects, and interaction with people. 3 in-class exams and 3 PyTorch assignments, with a written project report for the 4-unit option.

## Syllabus

[The schedule](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs237b/#schedule) runs 3 color-coded strands over 10 weeks.

1. Learning-based control and perception (weeks 1-4): ML for robotics, neural networks and PyTorch, Markov decision processes, model-based and model-free RL, learning-based perception.
2. Interaction with the physical environment (weeks 4-6): grasping and manipulation fundamentals, learning-based grasping, interactive perception.
3. Interaction with humans (weeks 7-9): imitation learning, learning from human feedback, interaction-aware learning and control, shared autonomy.

Week 10 is guest lectures, one by Erdem Bıyık. Exams fall on Feb 7, Feb 21 and Mar 14.

## Prerequisites

- Programming: [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md) or equivalent; the homework is Python and PyTorch.
- Linear algebra: CME 100 or equivalent.
- Probability: CME 106 or equivalent.
- The first half of the sequence: [CS 137A](CS%20137A.md) (AA 174A) or [CS 237A](CS%20237A.md) (AA 274A), the undergraduate and graduate listings of Principles of Robot Autonomy I.

## Related

- [CS 223A](CS%20223A.md): physics-based arm control with pen-and-paper problem sets, no learning.
- [CS 224R](CS%20224R.md): deep reinforcement learning in full, from policy gradients to offline RL and meta-RL, applied to language models as well as robots.
- [CS 234](CS%20234.md): reinforcement learning in general; here RL is one strand of three and always on a robot.
- [CS 237A](CS%20237A.md): the prerequisite half — estimation, localization and motion planning; this course is the learning-based half.
- [CS 238](CS%20238.md): decision making under uncertainty: MDPs and POMDPs mostly from the planning side, where this course learns policies.
