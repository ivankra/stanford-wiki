# CS 237B: Principles of Robot Autonomy II

The second half of the robot-autonomy sequence, where [CS 237A](CS%20237A.md)'s estimation and planning give way to learning: three professors split ten weeks between reinforcement learning, physical interaction with objects, and interaction with people. Three in-class exams and three PyTorch assignments, with a written project report for the 4-unit option.

## Syllabus

[The schedule](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs237b/#schedule) runs three colour-coded strands over ten weeks.

1. Learning-based control and perception (weeks 1-4): ML for robotics, neural networks and PyTorch, Markov decision processes, model-based and model-free RL, learning-based perception.
2. Interaction with the physical environment (weeks 4-6): grasping and manipulation fundamentals, learning-based grasping, interactive perception.
3. Interaction with humans (weeks 7-9): imitation learning, learning from human feedback, interaction-aware learning and control, shared autonomy.

Week 10 is guest lectures, one by Erdem Bıyık. Exams fall on Feb 7, Feb 21 and Mar 14.
