# CS 377N: Introduction to the Design of Smart Products

The course insists that the mechatronics and the interaction design are one problem. Students prototype connected devices, learn the guidelines for fitting PCBs and electrical components into mechanical assemblies, and are pushed to treat the physical form not as an enclosure around the electronics but as a central component of the product — with Norman's affordances and signifiers as the working vocabulary and interaction across networked devices as the constraint.

## Syllabus

From the catalog description; no public syllabus.

1. Techniques for rapid prototyping of smart devices.
2. Best practices for physical interaction design.
3. Fundamentals of affordances and signifiers.
4. Interaction across networked devices.
5. Design guidelines for integrating electrical components such as PCBs into mechanical assemblies.
6. The physical form of devices, treated as a central component rather than an enclosure.
