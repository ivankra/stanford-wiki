# CS 221: Artificial Intelligence: Principles and Techniques

Stanford's broad introduction to AI: the foundational principles behind learning, search, planning under uncertainty, probabilistic reasoning and logic, each with a written-and-programming homework built around an application. Graded mostly on an in-person final exam.

## Materials

Schedule, lectures and homeworks are public, and all sixteen offering sites since Autumn 2019 are still up. This offering's recordings go to Canvas, but Stanford Online carries all twenty Autumn 2025 lectures, so the course can be followed end to end from outside.

- **Syllabus**: the [schedule](https://stanford-cs221.github.io/autumn2026/#schedule) dates all 19 lectures, every homework release and due date and the five project milestones. Grading is homeworks 40%, an on-campus final 60%, project up to 1.5% extra credit.
- **Slides**: the [lectures repo](https://github.com/stanford-cs221/lectures) holds 22 executable lecture modules as Python sources — `search.py`, `games.py`, `first_order_logic.py` — with their figures; its description still reads Autumn 2025.
- **Notes**: none this offering, which runs no sections and says its modules page is not in use. [Autumn 2024](https://stanford-cs221.github.io/autumn2024/) is the one exception: 27 files under `extra/`, section problems and slides for most weeks.
- **Videos**: this offering's go to Canvas, by the site's own logistics line. The [Autumn 2025 playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rMeDqwS1yFl3j3sR_-MQNEN) covers that offering whole, overview through language models and AI supply chains to the fireside chat; the [Autumn 2021 playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rOca_Ovz1DvdtWuz8BfSWL2) gives its own in 45 short segments among 60 videos, the rest Autumn 2019, four dated only 2023 and five fireside talks.
- **Assignments**: HW1 and HW2 of seven so far, handout page plus zip apiece, and the [project guidelines](https://stanford-cs221.github.io/autumn2026/project.html) with all five milestones. [Autumn 2025](https://stanford-cs221.github.io/autumn2025/assignments/hw1_foundations/index.html) and [Spring 2026](https://stanford-cs221.github.io/spring2026/assignments/hw1_foundations/index.html) keep the complete eight, Foundations through Society.
- **Solutions**: only Autumn 2024's [section solutions](https://stanford-cs221.github.io/autumn2024/extra/week1_sol.pdf), eight weeks of them. The honor code refers to official homework solutions from previous years, without saying who sees them.
- **Exams**: the final is sat in person, no paper released; the 2017-2019 papers the Autumn 2019 and Spring 2020 sites link all 404.
- **Projects**: eight sample reports — chess, Go, Atari, a trip planner, parking, dance — in the [sample-projects repo](https://github.com/stanford-cs221/sample-projects), undated, last pushed 2019.
- **Repo**: starter code and a local grader inside each [homework zip](https://stanford-cs221.github.io/autumn2026/assignments/hw1_foundations.zip).
- **Older offerings**: the [offerings index](https://stanford-cs221.github.io/) links all sixteen, each with its own schedule and grading split — except [Spring 2020](https://stanford-cs221.github.io/spring2020/), the Zoom quarter, whose schedule still reads "Under construction!". No textbook is required; Russell and Norvig, Sutton and Barto, Koller and Friedman and Hastie are further reading.

## Syllabus

Nineteen lectures over ten weeks, grouped by unit with the homework released alongside:

1. Overview; learning I–III (HW: foundations, sentiment classification)
2. Search I–II (HW: route planning)
3. Reinforcement learning I–IV (HW: controlling mountaincar)
4. Games I–II (HW: Pac-Man)
5. Bayesian networks I–III (HW: Bayesian networks)
6. Logic I–II (HW: from language to logic)
7. AI & society; fireside chat and conclusion
