# CS 275: Translational Bioinformatics

The biomedical informatics department's translational course: how genome-scale measurements become clinical tools, taught through case studies on public data sources rather than algorithms. Runs under 4 codes, with the BIOMEDIN number as the one the department uses.

## Materials

Nothing from this course has been public since at least Winter 2021. It has no site of its own, every one of its 4 Canvas syllabi is Stanford-only, and the only readable material anywhere belongs to a Winter 2016 offering under different instructors.

- **Syllabus**: gated. Winter 2021, Winter 2022, Spring 2024 and Spring 2025 each uploaded one to Canvas, all 4 marked Stanford-only.
- **Slides**, **Notes**, **Assignments**, **Solutions**, **Exams**, **Projects**, **Repo**: nothing found for any offering since Winter 2021, and no source says what the course sets or whether anything exists to be gated.
- **Videos**: nothing. No source mentions recording, under this code or the BIOE, BIOMEDIN and GENE ones.
- **Extra**: `biomedin217.stanford.edu` still redirects to a Google Site, [BIOMEDIN217_2016](https://web.archive.org/web/20220218104912/https://sites.google.com/site/bmi2172016/), which has Syllabus, Lecture Slides, Problem Sets, Programming Tutorial, Readings and Final Project pages. The live redirect now ends at a Google sign-in and only the Internet Archive copy reads. It is Winter 2016's, under Wall, Dumontier and Gevaert, 10 years old, and it is a mention here rather than a rating.

## Syllabus

From the [catalog description](https://explorecourses.stanford.edu/search?view=xml-20200810&academicYear=20242025&q=CS&filter-departmentcode-CS=on&filter-coursestatus-Active=on), since no public syllabus was found: access and utility of publicly available data sources; the types of genome-scale measurement used in molecular biology and genomic medicine; linking genome-scale data to clinical data and phenotypes; and posing new biomedical questions with bioinformatics, taught through case studies.

## Prerequisites

- Programming ability at the level of [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md).
- Familiarity with statistics and biology.

## Related

- [CS 273C](CS%20273C.md): cloud computing for biology and healthcare, focused on infrastructure.
