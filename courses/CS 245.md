# CS 245: Principles of Data-Intensive Systems

Zaharia's course on how a data system is actually built — the layers under a query, from disk layout and indexes through the optimizer to transactions and recovery. Its distinguishing feature as a public artifact is the exam archive: three past finals with full worked solutions, which almost no other course in this wiki publishes.

## Materials

This offering left its Canvas course readable to anyone, so its decks, assignments, take-home midterm and final all download without a login — and the course host keeps 6 more past papers, every one with worked solutions, which almost nothing else in this wiki publishes.

- **Syllabus**: the public [Canvas syllabus](https://canvas.stanford.edu/courses/146728/assignments/syllabus) dates every session from 1/4, links each lecture's deck and names each reading with the question to answer: "Why did the storage system change from inversions to B-trees?", "How might the conclusions of the paper change if it ran on an NVMe SSD?" [Winter 2021's](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs245/#schedule) is the version the public site is still frozen at, and [Winter 2020](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs245/win2020/) keeps its own site at a year path.
- **Slides**: 16 decks as Canvas files, introduction through security and data privacy; the [Winter 2021 set](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs245/slides/) is 18 numbered decks plus 3 guest talks on the lakehouse architecture, Delta Lake and TensorFlow graph optimizations.
- **Notes**: the papers themselves, [served from the course host](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs245/readings/) — System R, C-Store, Spark SQL, granularity of locks, Lamport's "Time, Clocks", Aurora, Dynamo, Delta Lake, the dataflow model, PINQ, Opaque, Splinter, the Netflix de-anonymization paper — with Keshav's "How to Read a Paper" as optional week-one reading.
- **Videos**: the Canvas course carries a Panopto tab, but it is an LTI launch and no source says whether anything was recorded.
- **Assignments**: 3 of them, as Canvas PDFs this offering and as [PDFs on the site](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs245/assignments/) in Winter 2021, submitted on Gradescope.
- **Solutions**: worked solutions for this offering's midterm and final, and for every archived paper.
- **Exams**: a take-home midterm done in any 2-hour window, and a final. The [exam archive](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs245/exams/) adds midterms and finals from Winter 2021, Winter 2020 and Spring 2019.
- **Projects**: none; the grade is assignments and the 2 exams.
- **Repo**: none; the assignments are written rather than code handouts.
- **Textbook**: Garcia-Molina, Ullman and Widom's *Database Systems: The Complete Book*, 2nd edition, with a W3Schools SQL tutorial listed as background.

## Syllabus

No dated schedule is published. The catalog describes the scope: the principles behind systems that store and process data at scale, covering storage and indexing, query processing and optimization, transactions and recovery.
