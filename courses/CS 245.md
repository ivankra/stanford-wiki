# CS 245: Principles of Data-Intensive Systems

Zaharia's course on how a data system is actually built — the layers under a query, from disk layout and indexes through the optimizer to transactions and recovery. Its distinguishing feature as a public artefact is the exam archive: three past finals with full worked solutions, which almost no other course in this wiki publishes.

## Materials

The site is a frozen Winter 2021 offering and is thin on teaching material, but generous where it counts.

- **Syllabus**: partial. Staff, assignments and exams are listed; no dated lecture schedule or reading list is published.
- **Assignments**: all three, as [PDFs](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs245/assignments/assignment1.pdf).
- **Exams**: past finals from [Winter 2020](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs245/exams/final_win20.pdf), Spring 2019 and Winter 2017.
- **Solutions**: each archived final comes with its worked solutions — unusual, and the reason a self-studier would come here.
- **Slides and videos**: neither is linked.

## Syllabus

No dated schedule is published. The catalog describes the scope: the principles behind systems that store and process data at scale, covering storage and indexing, query processing and optimization, transactions and recovery.
