# CS 353: Seminar on Logic & Formal Philosophy

The catalog description is two words — "Contemporary work" — because the course is administrative: it is how Stanford students enrol in the Berkeley-Stanford Circle in Logic and Philosophy, a joint group directed by Wesley Holliday at Berkeley and Thomas Icard here. Meetings are in downtown San Francisco and pair one Berkeley student's paper with one Stanford student's, with a monthly Stanford-only meeting on campus and a cap of about twenty. Repeatable three times for credit.
