# CS 238: Decision Making under Uncertainty

Decision making under uncertainty from a computational perspective: probabilistic models and decision theory, then methods for problems with stochastic dynamics, model uncertainty and imperfect state information. Applications lean aerospace; final projects need not. Julia is used for demonstrations, but any language is allowed.

## Materials

## Syllabus

Ten weeks following the textbook's chapters:

1. Probabilistic reasoning: representation, inference, parameter and structure learning (ch. 1–5)
2. Simple decisions (ch. 6)
3. Sequential problems: exact solutions and approximate value functions, online planning, policy search, gradient estimation and optimization, actor-critic (ch. 7–13)
4. Model uncertainty: exploration and exploitation, model-based and model-free RL (ch. 15–17)
5. State uncertainty: beliefs; exact, offline and online belief-state planning (ch. 19–22)
6. Controller abstractions and multiagent reasoning (ch. 23–24)
7. World models; policy validation and summary
