# CS 100ACE: Problem-solving Lab for CS106A

One extra unit beside [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md), run by a course assistant rather than a lecturer: a two-hour Tuesday section, ACE-only office hours and review sessions, and one-on-one tutoring for students who need it. Admission is by application to the ACE program.

## Syllabus

There is no topic list of its own: the section follows [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md) week by week, giving extra practice on the same material. The published syllabus covers the four ACE components — the required weekly section, ACE-specific exam review sessions and office hours, weekly office hours, and tutoring on request.

## Prerequisites

- Concurrent enrollment in [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md), plus admission to ACE: apply through the ACE program, then receive a permission number in week 2 or 3. Auditing needs prior approval.
