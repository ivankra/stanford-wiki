# CS 547: Human-Computer Interaction Seminar

A weekly talk by an invited HCI researcher, open to the public. Enrolled students earn one unit for attending; there is no coursework beyond that.

## Materials

The talks are the whole material and they are all public: the term's speaker list, an archive of every talk since the early 1990s, and a standing YouTube playlist the seminar keeps adding to. There is no coursework, so there is nothing else to withhold.

- **Syllabus**: the [seminar site](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs547/) lists this term's speakers and titles with the attendance rules, and the archive lists every earlier talk [by date](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs547/years.php) and [by speaker](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs547/alphabetical.php) — about thirty a year, three quarters a year, back past 1991.
- **Slides**: none. The seminar posts no decks; speakers bring their own.
- **Notes**: none; nothing is written up.
- **Videos**: the [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rMyupDF2O00r19JsmolyXdD), 311 videos, which each offering adds to within a couple of weeks of the talk. Visitors may also attend in person; there is no livestream off campus.
- **Assignments**, **Solutions**, **Exams**, **Projects**, **Repo**: none. Enrolled students earn their unit by attending, and the seminar sets nothing else.

## Syllabus

Nine Friday talks in Autumn 2026 (6 and 27 November off), including:

- Daniel McDuff (Google): scaling health foundation models and agentic AI
- Kristin Williams (Emory): sensing uncertainty in sustainable HCI
- Qian Yang (Cornell): designing AI for societal good
- Philip Guo (UC San Diego): how Python Tutor became long-lasting academic software
- Steve Rathje (CMU): the psychology of engagement-maximizing technologies
- Aditya Parameswaran (UC Berkeley), Zana Buçinca (MIT), Christian Sandvig (Michigan): titles TBA; 23 October speaker TBA
