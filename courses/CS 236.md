# CS 236: Deep Generative Models

One course covering every major family of generative model in order of how they factor the likelihood: autoregressive first, then VAEs and flows, then GANs where the likelihood disappears, then energy-based and score-based models where it returns as a gradient. It ends on diffusion, including diffusion for discrete data, and on the harder question of how to evaluate any of them. 3 homeworks, an evening midterm and a poster-session project.

## Syllabus

[Twelve weeks](https://deepgenerativemodels.github.io/syllabus.html).

1. Foundations (weeks 1-3): background, autoregressive models, maximum likelihood learning.
2. Latent variables (weeks 3-5): VAEs over two weeks, then normalizing flows.
3. Implicit models (weeks 5-6): GANs.
4. Energy and score (weeks 6-8): energy-based models over three lectures, then score-based models, then evaluation of generative models.
5. Diffusion (weeks 10-11): score-based diffusion, discrete latent variable models, diffusion for discrete data.
