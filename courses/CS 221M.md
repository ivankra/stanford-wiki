# CS 221M: Mechanistic Interpretability

How to work out what a trained network is computing, taught with a bias toward causal methods over correlational ones, and around a notebook per lecture.

## Syllabus

[Schedule](https://cs221m.github.io/): five weeks of notebook-guided lectures, four of guest lectures, one of presentations.

1. **Weeks 1-2, the correlational baseline**: language-model review (annotated transformer, circuits); behavioral analysis and input attribution; probes for decoding activations.
2. **Weeks 3-5, the causal turn**: causal methods for interpretability; interventions for steering activations; theory of causal abstraction; causal mediation analysis; designing counterfactuals; automated causal interpretability, ending on DAS and boundless DAS.
3. **Weeks 6-10**: guest lectures from Potts, Merullo, Bau, Nanda, Huang, Lindsey and Saphra, a week-7 check-in and a week-10 poster session.

The project reimplements one key experiment from a recent paper as a walk-through notebook or repo plus report, in groups of 2-3 with a researcher as mentor.
