# CS 337: AI-Assisted Care

A one-hour Friday seminar run by the Stanford Partnership in AI-Assisted Care, premised on model building no longer being the bottleneck: the sessions are about problem selection, clinical integration and outcomes. Taken for 1 unit it is attendance only; 2–4 units adds a mentored project, with teams assigned in week 3.

## Materials

The site is an open announcement page with a real schedule and little else.

- **Syllabus**: the [schedule](https://cs337.stanford.edu/#schedule) lists each week's date, room and speaker with their affiliation, marks the project milestones, and states the grading rule (1 unit attendance, 2–4 units project). Several sessions still show "TBD" for the topic.
- **Exams**: none.
- **Slides, recordings, project specs**: nothing public; classes are hybrid over Zoom and course material runs through the course mailing list.

## Syllabus

Ten Friday sessions ([schedule](https://cs337.stanford.edu/#schedule)), each a speaker rather than a topic block: AI for health and what success looks like (Kaushal); dermatology and clinical AI evaluation (Daneshjou); developing algorithms and evaluating their performance (Durante); emergency medicine and clinical decision support (Walker); clinical AI in practice at an ambient-documentation company (van Nieuwenhuizen); behavioural health implementation (Kaushal and Wiltsey Stirman); healthcare computing at scale (Powell, NVIDIA); psychiatry and biomedical data science (Adeli); then project presentations in week 11. Project teams and mentors are assigned on 10 October, with a milestone on 7 November.
