# CS 337: AI-Assisted Care

A one-hour Friday seminar run by the Stanford Partnership in AI-Assisted Care, premised on model building no longer being the bottleneck: the sessions are about problem selection, clinical integration and outcomes. Taken for 1 unit it is attendance only; 2–4 units adds a mentored project, with teams assigned in week 3.

## Syllabus

Ten Friday sessions ([schedule](https://cs337.stanford.edu/#schedule)), each a speaker rather than a topic block: AI for health and what success looks like (Kaushal); dermatology and clinical AI evaluation (Daneshjou); developing algorithms and evaluating their performance (Durante); emergency medicine and clinical decision support (Walker); clinical AI in practice at an ambient-documentation company (van Nieuwenhuizen); behavioral health implementation (Kaushal and Wiltsey Stirman); healthcare computing at scale (Powell, NVIDIA); psychiatry and biomedical data science (Adeli); then project presentations in week 11. Project teams and mentors are assigned on 10 October, with a milestone on 7 November.
