# CS 180: Digital Systems Architecture

Taught under its EE number, and the only CS-listed course here whose labs ask students to design processor subsystems rather than program them. The site frames it as preparation for EE 282 on datacenter architecture.

## Syllabus

From the [schedule](https://web.stanford.edu/class/ee180/) and the site's overview:

1. **Hardware/software interface** (weeks 1–3): instruction sets, data and thread level parallelism, assembly language programming — H&P chapters 1 and 2. Lab 1 released week 2.
2. **Efficiency metrics** (week 3): performance, power, energy and cost.
3. **Processor design** (weeks 4–6): hardware design overview from Appendix B, then processor design and three lectures of pipelining. Lab 2 and Homework 1 released week 4.
4. **Memory hierarchy, virtualization and I/O**: caches and main memory, virtualization, basic I/O.
5. **Accelerators**: custom accelerator design, which the programming assignments build toward.

## Prerequisites

None listed in the catalog. The site pitches the course at students "specializing in the interrelated discipline of hardware/software systems", and at EE and CS students who want to "understand, optimize, or design their own processor based digital-system".
