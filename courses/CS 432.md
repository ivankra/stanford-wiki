# CS 432: Computer Vision for Education and Social Science Research

Computer vision for researchers who are not computer vision researchers: enough of how the algorithms work to apply current ones to image and video data — recognizing and locating objects, recognizing behaviors — plus workshop time on students' own applications. The prerequisites are Python familiarity and some experience with data, not a vision or machine learning course.

## Syllabus

Only the catalog describes the content. Computer vision, "the study of how to design artificial systems that can perform high-level tasks related to image or video data (e.g. recognizing and locating objects in images and behaviors in videos)", has seen "recent dramatic success"; the course "seek[s] to give education and social science researchers the know-how needed to apply cutting edge computer vision algorithms in their work as well as an opportunity to workshop applications".
