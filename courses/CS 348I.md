# CS 348I: Computer Graphics in the Era of AI

Four areas of graphics — rendering, geometry, animation, imaging — each revisited twice: what the conventional solution was, and what learning replaced it with. Nearly half the grade is presenting papers, and the guest list is the authors of several of them, including Ben Mildenhall on neural radiance fields. The final project is implementation, not a survey: neural rendering for image manipulation, procedural shape synthesis, or policy learning for character animation.

## Syllabus

[Twenty sessions](http://cs348i.stanford.edu/#schedule) over the quarter, paired by area.

1. Geometry and rendering (Jan 9-23): neural geometry representations such as Mosaic-SDF, then differentiable and neural rendering.
2. Imaging (Jan 25-30): computational photography, denoising, colorization.
3. Animation and simulation (Feb 1-13): character animation, motion synthesis, differentiable physics — including fluid simulation on neural flow maps.
4. Projects (Feb 14-20): proposals due, then two discussion sessions.
5. Guests (Feb 22-Mar 7): Lior Yariv, Minchen Li, Chenlin Meng, Ben Mildenhall, Alex Clegg.
6. Mar 12-15: presentations, demos, and the final project.
