# CS 402L: Beyond Bits and Atoms - Lab

The hands-on half of the pair. Students are put inside a constructionist learning environment and asked to notice their own learning while they are in it — learning 3D printers, laser cutters, Go Go Boards and sensors in order to design educational toolkits, toys, science kits and tangible user interfaces. No prior prototyping experience is required, and the theory it builds on comes from [CS 402](CS%20402.md), taken the same quarter.

## Syllabus

From the catalog description; no public syllabus.

1. A hands-on introduction to prototyping and fabricating tangible, interactive technologies, with a focus on learning and education.
2. Designing and prototyping low-cost technologies that support learning for diverse learners in all contexts.
3. State-of-the-art fabrication machines: 3D printers, laser cutters, Go Go Boards, sensors.
4. Building educational toolkits, educational toys, science kits and tangible user interfaces.
5. Reflecting on one's own learning process while inside a constructionist environment.
