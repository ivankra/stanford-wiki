# CS 341: Project in Mining Massive Data Sets

The sequel to [CS 246](CS%20246.md) with no lectures to speak of: teams of 3 are matched to a dataset and a mentor during the previous Winter quarter, get Google Cloud credits, and spend the term on one problem. The class meets Wednesdays "on a per-need basis" — 3 teaching sessions, then 2 checkpoint presentations and a final.

## Materials

The public part is the shell around the project, and the project is the course. The site still serves Spring 2019 — logistics, enrollment rules, the checkpoint schedule and 3 infrastructure decks — while the thing students actually do is assigned privately by a mentor, submitted to a gated Gradescope, and never published in any form.

- **Syllabus**: the [site](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs341/) carries the logistics, the enrollment rules, the prerequisites, the instructor, TA and mentor lists, and a dated schedule of checkpoints and report deadlines. Its staff mailing list is `cs341-spr1819-staff`, which dates it.
- **Slides**: exactly 3 — [introduction and Google Cloud overview](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs341/slides/1-intro.pdf), and advanced machine learning on GCP in [two](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs341/slides/2-ML.pdf) [parts](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs341/slides/3-Data.pdf). All 3 download.
- **Notes**, **Solutions**, **Exams**, **Repo**: none. The course writes nothing and grades nothing but the project.
- **Videos**: no recording is mentioned, and the sessions meet "on a per-need basis".
- **Assignments**: there is no problem set. Each team's project comes from its mentor, after a selection that happens the quarter before, and every deliverable goes to a Gradescope the course gates, as its Piazza is.
- **Projects**: no student report, deck or poster is posted, from this or any year.
- **Textbook**: [*Mining of Massive Datasets*](http://www.mmds.org/), by the course's own instructors, free to download.

## Syllabus

Not a taught syllabus; the schedule ([site](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs341/)) is 3 sessions of infrastructure followed by checkpoints.

1. Introduction and a Google Cloud overview.
2. Advanced machine learning on Google Cloud Platform, in 2 sessions covering the ML services and the data services.
3. Checkpoint 1 presentations, then a written report.
4. Checkpoint 2 presentations, then a written report.
5. Final presentations and final report.

## Prerequisites

- Large-scale data mining and machine learning: [CS 246](CS%20246.md) or equivalent — Hadoop, Spark, and the algorithms that go with them.
- The site also names [CS 221](CS%20221.md), [CS 224N](CS%20224N.md), [CS 224W](CS%20224W.md), [CS 228](CS%20228.md), [CS 229](CS%20229.md), CS 276 and EE 364A as helpful.

Enrollment is closed: students must be selected and matched to a project in the preceding Winter quarter, usually in March, and the number taken varies with mentor availability.
