# CS 341: Project in Mining Massive Data Sets

The sequel to [CS 246](CS%20246.md) with no lectures to speak of: teams of three are matched to a dataset and a mentor during the previous Winter quarter, get Google Cloud credits, and spend the term on one problem. The class meets Wednesdays "on a per-need basis" — three teaching sessions, then two checkpoint presentations and a final.

## Materials

The public part is the shell around the project: how to get in, what the checkpoints are, and three infrastructure decks.

- **Syllabus**: the [site](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs341/) carries logistics, enrollment rules, prerequisites, the mentor list and a dated schedule of checkpoints and report deadlines.
- **Slides**: exactly three — [introduction and Google Cloud overview](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs341/slides/1-intro.pdf), and advanced ML in GCP in [two](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs341/slides/2-ML.pdf) [parts](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs341/slides/3-Data.pdf).
- **Assignments**: there is no problem set. The project is assigned by a mentor and the deliverables go to Gradescope, which is gated; Piazza is too.
- **Projects**: no student reports, slides or posters are posted, in any year.
- The reference text, [*Mining of Massive Datasets*](http://www.mmds.org/), is free.

## Syllabus

Not a taught syllabus; the schedule ([site](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs341/)) is three sessions of infrastructure followed by checkpoints.

1. Introduction and a Google Cloud overview.
2. Advanced machine learning on Google Cloud Platform, in two sessions covering the ML services and the data services.
3. Checkpoint 1 presentations, then a written report.
4. Checkpoint 2 presentations, then a written report.
5. Final presentations and final report.

## Prerequisites

- Large-scale data mining and machine learning: [CS 246](CS%20246.md) or equivalent — Hadoop, Spark, and the algorithms that go with them.
- The site also names [CS 221](CS%20221.md), [CS 224N](CS%20224N.md), [CS 224W](CS%20224W.md), [CS 228](CS%20228.md), [CS 229](CS%20229.md), CS 276 and EE 364A as helpful.

Enrollment is closed: students must be selected and matched to a project in the preceding Winter quarter, usually in March, and the number taken varies with mentor availability.
