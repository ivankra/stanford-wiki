# CS 336: Language Modeling from Scratch

Every layer of a language model, built by hand: tokenizer, transformer, Triton kernels, multi-GPU training loop, Common Crawl pipeline, scaling law and post-training. The course takes its shape from operating-systems courses that build an OS from scratch, and gives students "minimal scaffolding"; the site warns the code volume is "at least an order of magnitude greater than for other classes".

## Syllabus

19 lectures over 10 weeks, per the [schedule](https://cs336.stanford.edu/#schedule), each assignment opening the unit it belongs to.

1. **Basics**: overview and tokenization; PyTorch and `einops`; resource accounting in FLOPs, memory and arithmetic intensity; architectures and hyperparameters; attention alternatives and mixture of experts.
2. **Systems**: GPUs and TPUs; kernels and Triton; 2 lectures on parallelism.
3. **Scaling**: 2 lectures on scaling laws, with inference and evaluation between them.
4. **Data**: sources and datasets, then filtering, deduplication, mixing and synthetic data.
5. **Alignment**: mid- and post-training (SFT and RLHF), RLVR, and multimodality.

Guest lectures from Daniel Selsam and Dan Fu close the term. The schedule shifts yearly: [Spring 2024](https://cs336.stanford.edu/spring2024/) gave data 4 lectures and had no inference lecture; [Spring 2025](https://cs336.stanford.edu/spring2025/) added inference and evaluation mid-course and split alignment into SFT/RLHF and RL.

## Prerequisites

The course site expects:

- Python and software engineering: enough to write an order of magnitude more code than a typical AI class.
- Deep learning and systems: strong familiarity with PyTorch, and systems concepts like the memory hierarchy, because much of the course is making models run fast across GPUs and machines.
- Calculus and linear algebra: MATH 51 or CME 100.
- Probability and statistics: [CS 109](CS%20109.md) or equivalent.
- Machine learning: [CS 221](CS%20221.md), [CS 229](CS%20229.md), [CS 230](CS%20230.md), [CS 124](CS%20124.md) or [CS 224N](CS%20224N.md).

## Related

- [CS 224N](CS%20224N.md): NLP with deep learning and one of the accepted prerequisites; it gives pretraining 2 lectures, where this course builds the whole stack.
- [CS 224V](CS%20224V.md): building applications on top of language models, where this course builds the model.
- [CS 229S](CS%20229S.md): optimizing and serving models that already exist; this course writes its kernels and parallelism while training one.
- [CS 312](CS%20312.md): also trains small language models, but as ablation experiments rather than an end-to-end stack.
- [CS 349D](CS%20349D.md): serving and inference infrastructure, which CS 336 covers in 1 lecture.
