# CS 51: CS + Social Good Studio: Designing Social Impact Projects

Teams take a problem domain supplied by a partner organization and work it up to a tested prototype, coached by industry designers and non-profit domain experts. It is a Cardinal Course, designated by the Haas Center for Public Service, and it feeds directly into [CS 52](CS%2052.md) in spring.

## Syllabus

No readable syllabus, so the catalog is the source: "Students in CS51 work in small teams to develop high-impact projects around problem domains provided by partner organizations, under the guidance and support of design/technical coaches from industry and non-profit domain experts. Main class components are workshops, team collaboration, guest speakers and mentorship. Studio provides an outlet for students to create social change through CS while engaging in the full product development cycle on real-world projects. The class culminates in a showcase where students share their project ideas and Minimum Viable Product (MVP) prototypes with stakeholders and the public."

The public partner directory shows the kind of organization involved: the San Fernando Valley Refugee Children's Center, the Mayor's Office of Technology and Innovation in San José.
