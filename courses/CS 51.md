# CS 51: CS + Social Good Studio: Designing Social Impact Projects

Teams take a problem domain supplied by a partner organization and work it up to a tested prototype, coached by industry designers and non-profit domain experts. It is a Cardinal Course, designated by the Haas Center for Public Service, and it feeds directly into [CS 52](CS%2052.md) in spring.

## Syllabus

The catalog is the source for the current offering: "Students in CS51 work in small teams to develop high-impact projects around problem domains provided by partner organizations, under the guidance and support of design/technical coaches from industry and non-profit domain experts. Main class components are workshops, team collaboration, guest speakers and mentorship. Studio provides an outlet for students to create social change through CS while engaging in the full product development cycle on real-world projects. The class culminates in a showcase where students share their project ideas and Minimum Viable Product (MVP) prototypes with stakeholders and the public."

The public partner directory shows the kind of organization involved: the San Fernando Valley Refugee Children's Center, the Mayor's Office of Technology and Innovation in San José.

The Winter 2020 schedule image fills in the shape of the quarter. Its second half runs: iterating on prototypes and a second brainstorm (week 6), high-fidelity prototyping and usability testing with a design review (week 7), pivoting and a speaker (week 8), stand-ups on prototype feedback then an evaluation and reflection session (week 9), showcase rehearsals and the final showcase (week 10). The deliverables underneath are four prototyping interviews, two design-review presentations, a tested final prototype, and a seven-part partner report submitted in two halves. The companion image adds the assessment: the report "provide[s] the partner organization with the valuable insights they've uncovered", and each quarter ends in a showcase — a high-fidelity design in winter, "a fully functional implementation" in spring.
