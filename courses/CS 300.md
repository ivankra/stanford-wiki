# CS 300: Departmental Lecture Series

A research tour for incoming CS PhD students: department faculty each give a 40-minute talk on their research, so first-years can see the research areas before choosing where to commit. Senior PhD students introduce their labs in Friday sessions.

## Materials

The department's [CS300 seminar page](https://www.cs.stanford.edu/phd-program-requirements-cs300-seminar) carries the format and the dated speaker schedule. No slides or recordings are linked. There is no coursework: credit is attendance, recorded through a CS login.

## Syllabus

Two 40-minute talks back to back per session, from Wednesday 23 September to Friday 4 December.

- **Monday and Wednesday**: faculty talks, among them Ron Dror, Azalia Mirhoseini, Sebastian Thrun, Carlos Guestrin, Keith Winstein, Oussama Khatib, Dan Boneh, Subhasish Mitra, Michael Bernstein, Diyi Yang, Emily Fox, Monica Lam, Jure Leskovec, Kunle Olukotun, Marc Levoy, Mark Zhandry, Chris Ré, Barbara Engelhardt, Aviad Rubinstein, Kayvon Fatahalian, Yejin Choi, Caroline Trippel and Fredrik Kjolstad.
- **Friday**: senior PhD students introduce their labs and research topics; optional but encouraged.

## Prerequisites

None stated; the catalog admits CS master's students if space is available.
