# CS 163: The Practice of Theory Research

Reingold's answer to the students who have the coursework and no idea how research starts. The subject is the practice rather than any body of knowledge: read a paper critically, search the bibliography around it, propose a direction, work out which technical areas it needs, and produce and communicate an insight. Everyone runs a limited-scope project, and the class is small.

## Syllabus

From the catalog description; no public syllabus. The project is the course, and it moves through:

1. Reading research papers from a critical point of view.
2. Conducting bibliography searches.
3. Suggesting new research directions.
4. Identifying the relevant technical areas.
5. Producing and communicating new insights.

Alongside the project, lectures on "the main ingredients of research".

## Prerequisites

- Algorithms: [CS 161](CS%20161.md).
- Automata and complexity: [CS 154](CS%20154.md).

Research experience is not required, but basic theory knowledge and mathematical maturity are expected. Class size is limited.
