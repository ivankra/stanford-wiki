# CS 182W: Ethics, Public Policy, and Technological Change (WIM)

[CS 182](CS%20182.md) with the writing requirement attached. The extra work is a revision of the philosophy paper and a mandatory session with a Technical Communication Program tutor — "This is a requirement for WIM credit." Enrollment is capped at 100 and limited to the majors that can use it.

## Materials

Everything is the shared [CS 182](CS%20182.md) site, and open there, back through every offering in the class archive. Only the WIM deadlines and the grading weights differ, and the site publishes both.

- **Syllabus**: the [syllabus handout](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs182/handouts/01Syllabus.pdf) gives this course its own grading column — the philosophy paper split 9% original and 21% revision, against a single 20% in [CS 182](CS%20182.md) — and the [announcements log](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs182/announcements.html) carries the CS 182W-only dates: the revision deadline was moved to March 3.
- **Slides**: [all 18 lecture PDFs](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs182/lectures/index.html).
- **Notes**: the [published readings document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vR36FMcjxQIj5eLoEFuSJBDpn93AHFW3jwOyk5mX38Ofh_30bz3jVjmzovvsdm4hNBW5i6OqItzo7ww/pub), the same list the syllabus prints.
- **Videos**: no source mentions recording, here or on any archived offering.
- **Assignments**: the [shared set of 5](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs182/assignments/index.html), with 2 starter-code zips, writing guidelines and the how-to deck — plus the sign-up link for the Technical Communication Program feedback session that WIM students must attend.
- **Solutions**: 4 sample philosophy papers with a cover note; the technical assignments have no key.
- **Exams**: the same take-home [Final Reflection](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs182/handouts/07FinalReflection.pdf), worth 15% here.
- **Projects**: none.
- **Repo**: the shared [software page](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs182/software/index.html).

## Syllabus

Identical to [CS 182](CS%20182.md): algorithmic decision-making and bias, data privacy and civil liberties, the power of private computing platforms, and the impact of generative AI. The catalog directs students to [CS 182](CS%20182.md) for meeting times, and warns: "Enroll in either [CS 182](CS%20182.md) or CS 182W, not both."

## Prerequisites

- Programming: [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md).
- Declared in Computer Science, Engineering Physics, STS, Math/Computational Science or Data Science. Enrollment opens to seniors and coterminal students first.

## Related

- [CS 182](CS%20182.md): the same course without the WIM requirement, open to any major.
