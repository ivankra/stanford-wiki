# CS 182W: Ethics, Public Policy, and Technological Change (WIM)

[CS 182](CS%20182.md) with the writing requirement attached. The extra work is a revision of the philosophy paper and a mandatory session with a Technical Communication Program tutor — "This is a requirement for WIM credit." Enrolment is capped at 100 and limited to the majors that can use it.

## Materials

Everything is the shared [CS 182](CS%20182.md) site, and open there; only the WIM deadlines differ.

- **Syllabus**: the [announcements log](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs182/announcements.html), which carries the CS 182W-only dates — the philosophy-paper revision deadline was moved to 3 March.
- **Slides**: [one PDF per lecture](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs182/lectures/index.html).
- **Notes**: the [published readings document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vR36FMcjxQIj5eLoEFuSJBDpn93AHFW3jwOyk5mX38Ofh_30bz3jVjmzovvsdm4hNBW5i6OqItzo7ww/pub).
- **Assignments**: the [shared set](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs182/assignments/index.html), with sample philosophy papers and the how-to deck.
- **Exams**: the same take-home final.
- **Videos**, **Solutions**: not found. **Projects**: none.

## Syllabus

Identical to [CS 182](CS%20182.md): algorithmic decision-making and bias, data privacy and civil liberties, the power of private computing platforms, and the impact of generative AI. The catalog directs students to [CS 182](CS%20182.md) for meeting times, and warns: "Enroll in either [CS 182](CS%20182.md) or CS 182W, not both."

## Prerequisites

- Programming: [CS 106A](CS%20106A.md).
- Declared in Computer Science, Engineering Physics, STS, Math/Computational Science or Data Science. Enrolment opens to seniors and coterminal students first.

## Related
