# CS 351: Open Problems in Coding Theory

A seminar organized around problems nobody has solved. Students read recent papers, present them, then work in small groups on the open questions themselves and report progress — with the explicit reassurance that "solving an open problem is not required". The topic list bends to whoever enrolls.

## Syllabus

From the catalog description; no public syllabus.

1. Coding theory as the study of encoding data to protect it from noise, at the intersection of CS, EE and mathematics.
2. Reading and presenting recent research papers.
3. Working in small groups on open problems, and presenting progress.
4. Topics chosen by student interest, possibly including: locality, coded computation, index coding, interactive communication, and group testing.
