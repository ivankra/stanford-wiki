# CS 191: Senior Project

Restricted to Computer Science students, in the section of their research instructor. The project must consist of publishable research, may result in a paper for a journal or conference, and requires a public presentation of results. Prerequisite: at least 135 units completed and the instructor's consent; a project proposal form is due before the quarter begins.
