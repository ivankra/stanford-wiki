# CS 129: Applied Machine Learning

Formerly CS 229A, and the inverse of [CS 229](CS%20229.md): the algorithms are learned from Ng's Coursera videos at home, and the one weekly Stanford session is spent deriving what the videos asserted and working practice problems. The quarter ends in an open-ended project and a poster session. Enrollment is limited.

## Materials

The Stanford half — problem sets, solutions, schedule — is public; the Coursera half is not.

- **Syllabus**: the [syllabus page](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs129/syllabus) is a table pairing each Thursday session with the Coursera weeks due the following Tuesday, and marks where the PSETs and the project fall. The front page carries the grading split: problem sets 20%, Coursera work 25%, midterm 25%, project 30%.
- **Slides**: none. The lecture content is the Coursera videos — about ten ten-minute videos a week.
- **Videos**: on Coursera, in a private session students are enrolled into during week 1.
- **Assignments**: each discussion session posts a [problems PDF](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs129/discussion-sessions/cs129_ds_1.pdf), and the graded problem sets post their own handouts, e.g. [PSET 1](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs129/pset_2026/HW1_2026.pdf). The Coursera quizzes and programming assignments are gated.
- **Solutions**: a [solutions PDF](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs129/discussion-sessions/cs129_ds_1_sols.pdf) beside every discussion-session problem set — rare for a Stanford course.
- **Exams**: the midterm is a quarter of the grade; no paper is posted.
- EdStem and Gradescope are gated.

## Syllabus

Ten sessions tracking two Coursera courses ([syllabus](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs129/syllabus)).

1. Jan 8 — the AI world, logistics, the syllabus. Coursera: *Supervised Machine Learning: Regression and Classification*, weeks 1-2.
2. Jan 15 — linear regression: derivations and practice problems. Coursera: week 3.
3. Jan 22 — logistic regression, text processing, derivations. Coursera: *Advanced Learning Algorithms: Neural Networks*, week 1. PSET 1 out.
4. Jan 29 — neural networks, vectorized gradients, softmax.
5. The remaining sessions continue through decision trees, unsupervised learning and k-means, reinforcement learning and Q-learning, anomaly detection and recommender systems, in the same video-then-derivation pattern, to the midterm and the final poster session.

## Prerequisites

- Programming: [CS 106B](CS%20106B.md) or CS 106X.
- Probability: [CS 109](CS%20109.md) or STATS 116.
- Linear algebra: MATH 51.
