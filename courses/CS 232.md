# CS 232: Digital Image Processing

Pre-deep-learning image processing, taught as general principles and worked in Matlab: what a histogram, a morphological operator or an eigenimage does, rather than what a network learns. Seven weekly homeworks, a take-home midterm, and a term project in the last two weeks — optionally shipped on an Android phone.

## Syllabus

[The Winter 2020 schedule](https://web.stanford.edu/class/ee368/schedule.html) gives eight teaching weeks, then two for the project.

1. Weeks 1-2: introduction and examples, point operations, histograms, color science and color balancing, segmentation and region processing.
2. Weeks 3-4: morphological image processing, linear filtering, template matching and matched filtering.
3. Weeks 5-7: eigenimages and Fisher images, edge detection and the Hough transform, keypoint detection, scale-space processing, feature-based matching and retrieval.
4. Weeks 8-10: a 24-hour take-home midterm, then the term project — report and source code.
