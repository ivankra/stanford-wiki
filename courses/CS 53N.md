# CS 53N: How Can Generative AI Help Us Learn?

A seminar that uses its students as its evidence base: each team becomes expert in one aspect of AI and one way it could change education, drawing on their own recent experience as learners. The catalog puts optimism and risk side by side — automated personal tutors and revolutions in assessment against unequal access and shallower thinking.

## Syllabus

No published syllabus. The catalog describes the ground: "This seminar course will explore the science behind generative AI, the likely future of tools such as DALL-E, ChatGPT, GPT-4, and Bard, and the implications for education, both in and outside of structured school environments. Students in the course will work in teams to each become experts in some aspect of AI and in some way that generative AI could create a new future for education."

The questions it poses are its outline: "ChatGPT has sparked dreams of automated personal tutors, customizable teaching assistance, AI-led collaborative learning, and revolutions in assessment… will AI-assisted learning be culturally appropriate and equally available to all? Can it increase opportunity for underprivileged learners worldwide, or will it accentuate privilege and privileged views? Will it help us learn faster, or distract us from thinking deeply about difficult problems ourselves?"
