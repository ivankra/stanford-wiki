# CS 170: Stanford Laptop Orchestra: Composition, Coding, and Performance

An ensemble of more than twenty laptops, controllers and hemispherical speaker arrays, each giving its instrument a sonic identity in the room. The quarter ends in two concerts, at the CCRMA Stage and Bing Concert Hall. It may be repeated four times for credit.

## Materials

Nothing teaching-facing is public. The CCRMA page is an application notice with a paragraph of description; the schedule, instrument assignments and repertoire are not posted.

- **Projects**: students craft and perform works for the two concerts; the pieces are not published as coursework.

## Syllabus

The only published account of content is the course summary: "Topics and activities will include issues of composing for laptop orchestras, sound synthesis, programming, instrument design, live performance. Coursework includes designing laptop-mediated software musical instruments, crafting musical works, and live performance as part of the laptop orchestra. Students will craft and perform works for two concerts in CCRMA Stage and Bing Concert Hall, respectively."

Rehearsal happens partly outside class in the "slorkshop", the CCRMA Listening Room, where stations stay set up.

## Prerequisites

- Consent of instructor, by application. The form asks about interest in SLOrk, commitment to class meetings, rehearsals and the performance, and any music or programming experience — "neither is strictly necessary if one is willing to learn as part of the course".
