# CS 359D: Quantum Complexity Theory

A small advanced theory course — 11 students in Winter 2025 — that treats quantum computation as a complexity class rather than a programming model, and runs out to open problems: the quantum PCP conjecture, and what it would take to verify that a sampling experiment really did what it claimed.
