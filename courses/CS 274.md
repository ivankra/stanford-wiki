## Syllabus

From the catalog description (no public syllabus):

- Sequences: pairwise and multiple sequence alignment of sequences and structures; phylogenetic tree construction; hidden Markov models
- Protein structure: basic structural computations, structure prediction, protein language models, molecular dynamics and energy minimization, statistical analysis of 3D biological data
- Data and knowledge: integrating data sources; knowledge representation and controlled terminologies for molecular biology; intro to transcription analysis
- Applications: chemoinformatics, pharmacogenetics, network biology
