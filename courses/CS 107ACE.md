# CS 107ACE: Problem-solving Lab for CS107

Two hours a week of [CS 107](CS%20107.md) in a small room: practice problems on the current topic, ACE-only office hours and exam review sessions. Undergraduates only, by application, graded on participation.

## Materials

The syllabus and the class's slide folder are public, but both belong to the Spring 2026 offering — the site has not been rolled over.

- **Syllabus**: a [Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tvintDn4eKDFi5PYuvNg-D36WriW49E-b3m7HWsALYc/edit) on enrolment, attendance and grading.
- **Slides**: a [public Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1R9XOS4qdLeCBiQeNDh56cZYMT7ptssXM) of handouts and slides, which the site says is updated before every class.
- **Assignments**: none — the section works practice problems in the room.
- **Videos**, **Exams**, **Projects**: none.

## Syllabus

No topic list of its own: the section tracks [CS 107](CS%20107.md) week by week, adding practice on whatever that course is covering, plus review sessions before its midterm and final.

## Prerequisites

- Concurrent enrollment in [CS 107](CS%20107.md). Open to undergraduates only — the site says explicitly that graduate and SCPD students cannot enrol — and by application, with permission codes issued before the week 3 add/drop deadline.
