# CS 107ACE: Problem-solving Lab for CS107

Two hours a week of [CS 107](CS%20107.md) in a small room: practice problems on the current topic, ACE-only office hours and exam review sessions. Undergraduates only, by application, graded on participation.

## Materials

Everything this section produces is readable, across two sites: the current page's Spring 2026 class Drive, which turns out to hold the whole quarter, and the site from its CS 107A years, still up with the reference handouts and the assignment walkthrough videos.

- **Syllabus**: a [Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tvintDn4eKDFi5PYuvNg-D36WriW49E-b3m7HWsALYc/edit) on enrollment, attendance and grading.
- **Slides**: [Class Slides/Handouts](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/13ndaRrJhOvpjDNHZErMhEWQjAr_PT87Y) in the class Drive, one deck per meeting from week 2 to week 10 — Unix, bits and bytes, bitwise operators and C-strings, pointers and memory, stack and heap, generics and `void *`, function pointers, assembly in three parts, the implicit heap and the explicit heap. Fourteen decks; the three empty week folders are the two "No Class" sessions and midterm review.
- **Notes**: the CS 107A site's [quick-start references](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs107a/extra_refs/command_line_ref.pdf) — the command line, Emacs, and the anatomy of a [CS 107](CS%20107.md) assignment — plus extra practice sets on Unix and on assembly puzzles.
- **Videos**: [seven walkthroughs](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs107a/walkthrough.html) of [CS 107](CS%20107.md) assignments 0 through 6 on YouTube, sectioned with timestamps, recorded by a former CA in Winter and Spring 2021.
- **Assignments**: none of its own — the section works [CS 107](CS%20107.md)'s problems in the room.
- **Solutions**: answers to both practice papers, filed with them.
- **Exams**: a [practice midterm](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1CIygqBeEzPYxFlMEnNwDQFLsHPFk91vR) and a [practice final](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/14buOuZzNe5VCyY-k8jek-0qlr6pj-Sl4) for Spring 2026, each with an answer key, alongside the two review-session documents. They are practice papers written for the section, not [CS 107](CS%20107.md)'s own past exams.
- **Projects**: none.

## Syllabus

No topic list of its own: the section tracks [CS 107](CS%20107.md) week by week, adding practice on whatever that course is covering, plus review sessions before its midterm and final.

## Prerequisites
