# CS 402: Beyond Bits and Atoms: Designing Technological Tools

The theory half of a two-course pair — students must register for the lab, [CS 402L](CS%20402L.md) or EDUC 211, at the same time. Its subject is designing educational technology that is cheap enough to reach every kind of learner, argued from constructivist and constructionist positions and sharpened by critical pedagogy, Universal Design for Learning and interaction design for children. Every week an industry practitioner with a shipped evidence-based product presents their own work as a case study.

## Syllabus

From the catalog description; no public syllabus.

1. Theories and design frameworks from constructivist and constructionist learning perspectives.
2. The lenses of critical pedagogy, Universal Design for Learning, and interaction design for children.
3. Case studies of schools and educational technologies.
4. Weekly presentations by industry experts with proven records designing evidence-based educational technology products.
5. The design of a new educational technology, prototyped in the paired lab.

## Prerequisites

- Concurrent registration in [CS 402L](CS%20402L.md) or EDUC 211, the lab half.
- An application submitted before the quarter starts, and attendance at the first class.

No prototyping experience is required.
