# CS 42SI: From Player to Maker: 2D Engine-Based Game Development

Twenty students, two units, and several finished games each: the course runs the game-development lifecycle repeatedly in Unity, alone and in teams, and ends by publishing the results into a public game jam that anyone can play.

## Syllabus

The [published syllabus](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PsrNgMwiYHUNRDHz7Ueq-maVvIi6Eu5X6uMMY35iYAw/edit) runs 10 weeks in 5 named phases, 2 sessions a week:

1. **Welcome to Unity** (weeks 1–2): the editor, then math and movement and object-oriented programming in C#, with HW0 "From Player to Maker".
2. **Game effects** (week 3): game juice and user interfaces, with HW1, a Suika-style game.
3. **Game modding and showcase** (weeks 4–5): coding together, publishing a game, a gallery session, then the competition kick-off. HW2 is "Let's Talk Games", HW3 "The Big Game".
4. **Final projects** (weeks 6–8): miscellaneous content, a session on vibe coding, then work days, with a proposal and a milestone.
5. **Showcase and awards** (weeks 9–10): final work day, the Final Project Showcase, game-feedback write-ups, and a winners-and-next-steps session.

The stated outcome is "functional video game prototypes displayable on a portfolio". Class time is activities, discussion and group work, so attendance and weekly deliverables are both expected: the pass bar is every deliverable above 70% with at most 2 unexcused absences.
