# CS 52: CS + Social Good Studio: Implementing Social Good Projects

The build-and-ship half of the studio. Teams arrive with a tested MVP, a defined target user and a community partner, and spend the quarter making it something that can keep running after the quarter ends.

- **Syllabus**, **Assignments**, **Exams**, **Videos**: not found.

## Syllabus

No published syllabus. The catalog gives the shape: "Teams enter the quarter having completed and tested a MVP with a well-defined target user, and a community partner. Students will learn to apply scalable technical frameworks, tools for deployment, and user testing techniques. The purpose of the class is to facilitate students to build a sustainable infrastructure around their product idea. Main class components are workshops, team collaboration, and software development."
