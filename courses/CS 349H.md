# CS 349H: Software Techniques for Emerging Hardware Platforms

A research seminar on the benefits of novel hardware technologies, what keeps them from broad adoption, and how software techniques can make them more usable. Guest lecturers are hardware designers from research and industry.

## Syllabus

The substrates covered change from term to term. The catalog lists in-memory computing platforms, dynamical-system-solving mixed-signal devices, flexible and bendable electronics, neuromorphic computers, intermittent computing platforms, ReRAMs, DNA-based storage and optical computing.

The public micro-assignments:

1. Stochastic computing: bitstream arithmetic, non-idealities, static analysis of error
2. Hyperdimensional computing: binding, bundling, item memories, an HDC classifier
3. Delay-based computing: delay encodings and gates, digital logic built from them
4. Analog computing: cellular non-linear networks for images, phase- and frequency-domain oscillator computing
