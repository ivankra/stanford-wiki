# CS 141: Sports and Data

An applied data-science course with a single domain: every method — regression, clustering, dimensionality reduction, neural networks — is taught on real professional sports data, and this quarter adds a video pipeline that turns game footage into tracking data. Guest lectures come from Stanford athletics and professional leagues, with the catalog naming Stanford Football and the SF 49ers as collaborators for Autumn 2026.

## Syllabus

No schedule is public. The catalog gives the arc: exploratory data analysis, regression, classification, clustering, dimensionality reduction and neural networks, taught from real professional-sports examples, with "a major new emphasis this quarter" on video — "player and ball detection, multi-object tracking, pose estimation, and event recognition, and how the resulting tracking data supports analyses that box scores cannot". Weekly assignments and a final team project build Python tooling for both video and conventional tabular workflows.
