# CS 421: Designing AI to Cultivate Human Well-Being

A two-unit workshop, not a lecture course: three long afternoons a week with a marketing professor and the director of HAI, aimed at students who will build AI products and have never been asked what "better" would mean. The organizing device is the PACE framework — a technology should have a clear purpose, augment human dignity and autonomy, create inclusivity and collaboration, and create shared prosperity — and teams spend the term designing something against it. Graded satisfactory/no credit.

## Syllabus

From the catalog description; no public syllabus.

1. AI's dual potential: mitigating climate change, diagnosing and treating disease, enhancing learning, improving access to capital — against exacerbating human bias, destroying trust in information, displacing industries and amplifying inequality.
2. The argument that building AI must be an inclusive, interactive and introspective process guided by an affirmative vision.
3. Basic science on what brings about the conditions for human flourishing.
4. The PACE framework: clear and meaningful **p**urpose; **a**ugments human dignity and autonomy; creates a feeling of inclusivity and **c**ollaboration; creates shared prosperity and a sense of forward movement, or **e**xcellence.
5. A final project in interdisciplinary teams, proposing a solution to a significant societal challenge.

None stated. The class is deliberately interdisciplinary, and the workshop format assumes teams that mix technical and non-technical students.
