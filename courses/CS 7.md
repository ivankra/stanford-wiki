# CS 7: Personal Finance for Engineers

One unit, ten Tuesday evenings, and a syllabus aimed squarely at the money decisions a new engineer meets first: what an offer is actually worth, what to do with it, and what debt and a house cost. Taught by an industry practitioner, in its ninth year, with every deck published publicly.

## Syllabus

The published session list:

1. Introduction.
2. Behavioral finance — "Predictably Irrational".
3. Compensation, including equity and stock options.
4. Saving and budgeting.
5. Assets and net worth.
6. Debt.
7. Investing.
8. Financial planning and goals.
9. Real estate.
10. Additional topics.

The catalog adds the framing: "actual industry-based financial information from technology companies and realistic financial issues", with no finance or economics background required.

## Prerequisites

- None. The catalog states no prior finance or economics experience is needed.
