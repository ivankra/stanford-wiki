# CS 448V: Topics in Computer Graphics: Computational Video Manipulation

One instance of the rotating CS 448 topic slot, given over to video: acquiring it, representing it, editing it and remixing it. The bias is toward building — several popular manipulation algorithms are presented "with an emphasis on using these techniques to build practical systems", and students shoot their own footage and implement the tools to process it. Most of the grade is a substantial final project. Advanced undergraduates were welcome.

## Syllabus

From the catalog description; no public syllabus.

1. A survey of recent work on computational video analysis and manipulation.
2. How to acquire, represent, edit and remix video.
3. Several popular video manipulation algorithms, presented with an emphasis on building practical systems from them.
4. Students acquire their own video and implement the processing tools needed to analyze and manipulate it.
5. A substantial final project.
