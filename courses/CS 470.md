# CS 470: Music and AI

Ge Wang's course at CCRMA, and the only one in the Winter catalog whose stated first learning goal is to be able to frame a critical question rather than to build something. The making is real — four projects in ChucK, ChAI, Wekinator and PyTorch, from a word-embedding etude called "Poets of Sound and Time" to a final piece titled "A Story of You" — but each is paired with a written reading response and an in-class reading where the work is discussed rather than demoed. Enrolment is by application: prospective students email the instructor with something they are curious about and something they are genuinely worried about in AI and music. No prior AI or music experience is assumed.

## Syllabus

No lecture schedule is published. The course is structured by its four assignments, per the [home page](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/courses/356/):

1. **Weeks 1–2, "Poets of Sound and Time"**: a programming etude built on a word embedding model, due 18 January, followed by an in-class reading of the work.
2. **Weeks 3–5, "Featured Artist"**: a programming project with a milestone, final deliverables and in-class presentations.
3. **Weeks 6–9, "Wekinate Your World"**: an etude in interactive machine learning with Wekinator — training a mapping from gesture to sound by example rather than by code.
4. **Weeks 9–11, "A Story of You"**: the final project, closing with a reading on 19 March.

Readings are a curated list of articles on the technical, philosophical, social and cultural dimensions of AI and music, with students bringing their own.

## Prerequisites

- One to two years of programming. No prior AI or music experience is needed.
- Recommended but not required: MUSIC 256A / [CS 476A](CS%20476A.md), or MUSIC 220B — both CCRMA courses in computer music.
- Instructor consent, by application, is required to enrol.
