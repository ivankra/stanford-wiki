# CS 230: Deep Learning

Stanford's wrapper around Andrew Ng's [Deep Learning Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/deep-learning) courses. The fundamentals, from logistic regression to CNNs, sequence models and transformers, come from two Coursera modules a week with autograded quizzes and notebooks. Stanford adds weekly lectures on extra topics, such as adversarial robustness, deep RL and LLM agents; a pen-and-paper midterm; and a TA-mentored team project worth nearly half the grade.

## Materials

Nearly all of Stanford's side of the course is public: lecture decks, TA section notes, project brief and starter code, past midterms with solutions, and every Autumn 2025 lecture on YouTube. The Coursera half with quizzes and assignments is gated, though most of its videos are free on YouTube and unofficial copies circulate.

- **Syllabus**: the [home page](https://cs230.stanford.edu/) gives format, grading, late days and AI policy; this offering's dated schedule is a private Google Sheet. The [schedule page](https://cs230.stanford.edu/syllabus/) still shows Autumn 2025's; older ones are in the Internet Archive, e.g. [Autumn 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20241113044952/https://cs230.stanford.edu/syllabus/).
- **Slides**: Autumn 2025's schedule links decks for 8 of its 9 meetings, and earlier folders such as [`fall_2024/`](https://cs230.stanford.edu/syllabus/fall_2024/) and [`winter2020/`](https://cs230.stanford.edu/winter2020/) hold up to 11 each. The 17 Coursera module decks are in [`files/`](https://cs230.stanford.edu/files/).
- **Notes**: 9 [TA section pages](https://cs230.stanford.edu/section/), from scoping a project and backpropagation to evaluation metrics and report writing, 4 with decks. 7 of their 11 Colab notebooks open without a sign-in.
- **Videos**: this offering's lectures go to Canvas. [Autumn 2025](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rNRRGdS0rBbXOUGA0wjdh1X) was posted on YouTube. ## Syllabus

From the [Autumn 2025 schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20251208110119/https://cs230.stanford.edu/syllabus/), the latest public one, grouped by the 5 Coursera courses:

1. **Neural networks and deep learning**: logistic regression, shallow and deep networks. Lectures: deep learning projects, AI case studies, the full cycle of a DL project.
2. **Improving deep neural networks**: initialization, regularization, gradient checking, optimization algorithms, hyperparameter tuning, batch normalization. Lecture: adversarial robustness and generative models.
3. **Structuring ML projects**: ML strategy, with bird-recognition and autonomous-driving case studies. Lecture: deep reinforcement learning.
4. **Convolutional networks**: ResNets, transfer learning with MobileNet, YOLO detection, neural style transfer, face recognition, U-Net segmentation. Lecture: career advice, reading papers, AI and healthcare.
5. **Sequence models**: RNNs and LSTMs, word embeddings, sequence-to-sequence with attention, transformers. Lectures: LLM agents, prompts and RAG; model interpretability.

The in-class lectures hardly changed from Winter 2020 to Spring 2023; Autumn 2024 added stable diffusion, RAG and AI agents. The Coursera notebooks switch to TensorFlow with an introduction in week 5 and use it through the closing transformer assignment: the specialization's April 2021 update refactored them for TensorFlow 2 and kept the framework. Stanford's own material uses both frameworks: section 5 compares TensorFlow with PyTorch, and the project starter code comes in each.

## Prerequisites

- Programming in Python, enough to write a non-trivial program.
- Linear algebra, matrix and vector multiplication: MATH 51.
- Probability: [CS 109](CS%20109.md) or STATS 116, plus basic statistics; the course site asks for these, the catalog doesn't.
- Machine learning isn't required first: [CS 229](CS%20229.md) may be taken concurrently.

## Related

- [CS 129](CS%20129.md): machine learning beyond deep learning, in the same flipped format.
- [CS 221](CS%20221.md): AI beyond deep learning, from search and MDPs to games and Bayesian networks.
- [CS 229](CS%20229.md): the mathematical foundations of machine learning, from linear models through EM to reinforcement learning.
- [CS 231N](CS%20231N.md), [CS 224N](CS%20224N.md): one domain, vision or language, at a faster pace, with more focus on assignments than a project.
