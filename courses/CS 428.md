# CS 428: Computation and Cognition: The Probabilistic Approach

Goodman's framing is that learning and reasoning are inference in complex probabilistic models, and that the same formalism covers phenomena psychology usually treats separately — how children acquire concepts, how people infer causes, how listeners read speakers' intentions. Formal modelling is taught next to the empirical findings it is meant to explain, not apart from them.
