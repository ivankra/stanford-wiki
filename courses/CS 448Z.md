# CS 448Z: Physically Based Animation and Sound

Simulation where the picture and the sound come from the same physics: a bubble's resonance, a shell's modes, a fracture, a flame, air moving past an object. Four projects of increasing scope — three set pieces and a self-directed final — each ending in a show-and-tell. Lectures read the research papers behind each method; there are no exams.

## Syllabus

Per the [schedule](https://graphics.stanford.edu/courses/cs448z-26-spring/#schedule), the lectures track the four projects.

1. **Foundations** (Mar 31-Apr 7): acoustic waves and radiation.
2. **Modal sound** (Apr 9-14): modal vibration analysis and synthesis, the basis of HW2.
3. **Making noise** (Apr 16-May 5): bubble-based liquid sounds; acceleration noise for rigid-body impacts; fire sound; fracture sound; elastic rods; thin shells.
4. **Frontier topics** (May 12-28): aerodynamic sound; inverse-Foley animation and control; atmospheric and underwater sound propagation; time-domain wave-based synthesis; a guest lecture from Kangrui Xue on blended-domain sound-source animation.

Grading is the four projects: 20% each for HW1-3 and 40% for the final, with show-and-tell participation folded into each.
