# CS 327A: Advanced Robotic Manipulation

Khatib's own course on his own framework: the operational space formulation, developed from the control structure of a simple arm up to interactive whole-body control of a humanoid — coordinating many degrees of freedom, managing contacts, holding constraints, and coping with underactuation and balance in one unified scheme. Six problem sets in the SAI simulator, a student mini-symposium of paper reviews, and a take-home final.

## Syllabus

[The timeline](https://cs327a.stanford.edu/) builds the framework in order.

1. Weeks 1-2: introduction, simulation and control in SAI, kinematics and task representations, Jacobian inverses.
2. Weeks 3-5: the operational space framework and its dynamics, unified motion and force control, redundancy, inertial properties, cooperative robot dynamics and the virtual linkage model.
3. Weeks 6-8: mobile manipulation, whole-body control of humanoids, artificial potential fields and elastic planning, haptics, human-friendly robot design.
4. Weeks 9-10: guest lectures, advanced topics, the student mini-symposium and the take-home final.
