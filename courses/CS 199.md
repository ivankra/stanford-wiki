# CS 199: Independent Work

Special study under faculty direction, in the section led by the research instructor, usually leading to a written report. The catalog directs students to [CS 199P](CS%20199P.md) where a letter grade is not appropriate. Prerequisite: consent of instructor.

## Related

- [CS 199P](CS%20199P.md): the same, graded S/NC.
- [CS 195](CS%20195.md): supervised research with a required written report and public presentation.
- [CS 399](CS%20399.md): the master's-level equivalent.
