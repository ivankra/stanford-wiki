# CS 224R: Deep Reinforcement Learning

Algorithms for learning behaviour from experience with deep networks mapping high-dimensional observations to actions, taught through robotics and language models. Twice-weekly lectures, three PyTorch homeworks, an in-class midterm and a final project. The catalog calls it complementary to [CS 234](CS%20234.md), neither being a prerequisite for the other, with a more applied and deep-learning focus.

## Syllabus

Ten weeks of paired lectures, per the [timeline](https://cs224r.stanford.edu/), each anchored to one or two papers rather than a textbook.

1. **Weeks 1–2**: MDPs and imitation learning; policy gradients; actor-critic methods.
2. **Weeks 3–4**: off-policy actor-critic; Q-learning; offline RL; reward learning.
3. **Week 5**: RL for LLMs — preference optimization, then reasoning, both guest lectures.
4. **Weeks 6–7**: model-based RL; multi-task and goal-conditioned RL; meta-RL, then the midterm.
5. **Weeks 8–9**: hierarchical RL and imitation; sim-to-real transfer; RL for vision-language-action models; frontiers.
6. **Weeks 10–11**: poster session and final report.

Homeworks are 40% of the grade, the project 35% and the exam 25%.

## Prerequisites

- Machine learning: [CS 229](CS%20229.md) or equivalent — stochastic gradient descent, cross-validation, probability, multivariable calculus and linear algebra.
- Deep learning: backpropagation, convolutional networks and transformers; assignments are in PyTorch, with a review tutorial in week 1.
- Reinforcement learning basics: the site points at [CS 221](CS%20221.md)'s MDP and RL lectures or chapters 3–4 of Sutton & Barto.
