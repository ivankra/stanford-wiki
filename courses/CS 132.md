# CS 132: AI as Technology Accelerator

A teaching version of the Stanford Emerging Technology Review — the School of Engineering, HAI and Hoover's joint primer on frontier technologies. Each week a different Stanford faculty member or fellow presents one domain: the foundational concepts, where AI has changed what is possible, and what follows for users, communities and policy.

## Syllabus

No published syllabus. The catalog names the domains — "cybersecurity, biotechnology, neuroscience, space technologies, and material sciences" — and the shape of each week: "a leading Stanford faculty or fellow will present a seminar on one or multiple domains, emphasizing foundational concepts and computational challenges, current technical advances with AI, and over-the-horizon implications for individual users, communities, and broader society."

It runs in both directions: "How is AI accelerating breakthroughs in fields like biotechnology, neuroscience, and cybersecurity? How are other fields (semiconductors, material sciences advances) influencing the development of AI?" Beyond mechanism, the catalog names "economic incentives, security risks, and ethical considerations" as the factors it takes up.
