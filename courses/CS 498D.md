# CS 498D: Design for Learning: College in the Age of AI

## Syllabus

From the catalog description; there's no week-by-week schedule.

1. Teams imagine possible futures shaped by AI, working through questions on consciousness and values, careers when specialized skills are widely available, lifelong learning, and human relationships.
2. Build to think: prototyping in many mediums, with AI and without.
3. Mid-quarter: short science-fiction films set in complicated futures, neither utopias nor dystopias.
4. What might replace current college in those scenarios.
5. A showcase for the Stanford community, plus public artifacts.
