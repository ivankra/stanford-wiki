# CS 46N: Working with Data: Delights and Doubts

Widom's freshman seminar on data as something to be skeptical of as well as good at — the title's "doubts" are the point, and the site's own heading for the course is "Big Data, Big Discoveries, Big Fallacies".

## Materials

Nothing from this offering, or either of the two before it, is public: all three uploaded a syllabus to Canvas and all three are Stanford-only. The one site under this code is eleven years old. It is also generous — the Autumn 2015 offering published its whole class table, every handout and the students' own work, and all of it still loads — but the course has been retitled twice since and rebuilt around a quarter-long quantified-self project that the 2015 schedule does not contain.

- **Slides**, **Notes**, **Videos**, **Assignments**, **Solutions**, **Exams**, **Projects**, **Repo**: not found for any offering the wiki records.

## Syllabus

The [2015 class table](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs46n/) alternates technique, guest and workshop: it opens with motivating examples, data analysis techniques and data visualization, then hands the room over — CourseRank's Filip Kaliszan, the Copenhagen Networks Study, Facebook ad targeting, the Amazon recommendation system — with student visualization demos, student-led discussions, sampling, baseball analytics and a field trip to Google in between. Two projects run underneath: a visualization of a dataset of your choosing, and movie-rating prediction, settled in a real-time in-class competition.

The catalog describes the current course differently, around a quarter-long "quantified self" project: pose questions about yourself or your surroundings, collect the data, analyze and visualize it.
