# CS 377U: Understanding Users

How technology actually gets used, studied with the methods that find out: qualitative ones — interviews, contextual inquiry, diary studies, ethnography — and quantitative ones — A/B tests, instrumentation, analytics, surveys — combined over one quarter-long project in which students design, build and evaluate a novel mobile application.

## Syllabus

No source describes the Spring 2026 offering beyond the catalog, which puts the whole course inside one project: students "learn generative and evaluative research methods to explore how systems are appropriated into everyday life in a quarter-long project where they design, implement and evaluate a novel mobile application". It names the method families explicitly — quantitative (A/B testing, instrumentation, analytics, surveys) and qualitative (diary studies, contextual inquiry, ethnography) — "and their combination", with practical experience applying them in the project.

The Spring 2019 schedule, which is not evidence about this offering, ran the same arc: why to study system use, then domain-specific needs, then qualitative analysis by affinity mapping and open coding, then from analysis to design.
