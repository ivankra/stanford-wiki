# CS 247G: Design for Play

Studio work combines rapid prototyping with user research, critique and repeated playtesting.

## Materials

The syllabus, slides, notes, lecture videos and student work are public. Detailed schedules and assignment briefs are on Canvas.

- **Syllabus**: [week plan, grading and policies](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/sample-page/); the dated schedule is on Canvas.
- **Slides**: published decks in thirteen lecture pages linked from the [syllabus](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/sample-page/).
- **Notes**: [The Formal Elements of Game Design](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/sample-page/the-formal-elements-of-game-design-2/) and the [reading library](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/the-library/).
- **Videos**: six-part lecture series embedded in [Formal Elements](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/sample-page/the-formal-elements-of-game-design-2/).
- **Assignments**: [public outlines of both projects](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/sample-page/); per-deliverable briefs on Canvas.
- **Exams**: none.
- **Projects**: [student critical plays, project write-ups and reflections](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/category/cs247g/).

## Syllabus

The [published syllabus](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/sample-page/) gives this nine-week outline:

1. **Week 1**: game-design fundamentals and critical play.
2. **Weeks 2–4, Social Mediation Game**: target players, community-building mechanics, game architecture, aesthetics, graphic and information design.
3. **Weeks 5–9, Environmental Storytelling**: spatial narratives, puzzles, level design, playtesting and game balance.

Studio sections teach tools including Unity.

## Prerequisites

- Design research, prototyping and interface design: [CS 147](CS%20147.md) or equivalent background. The catalog waives [CS 147](CS%20147.md) for non-CS majors; the syllabus states no technical requirements.
