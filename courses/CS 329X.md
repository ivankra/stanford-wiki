# CS 329X: Human Centered NLP

How to design, align and evaluate LLM systems around the people who use them, taught as a paper-reading lecture course with many guest lectures and a team research project.

## Materials

Spring 2023 homework was on Canvas.

## Syllabus

By week; week 10 is Thanksgiving:

1. Introduction to human-centered LLMs
2. Crash course on LLMs and prompting; learning from human preferences (RLHF, DPO)
3. Data for alignment (guest); human-AI interaction
4. Evaluating human-AI interaction; human-agent collaboration
5. Building full-stack HAI systems; generative interfaces
6. LLMs and the future of work; midway project showcase
7. Guest lectures, incl. emerging topics in human-AI interaction
8. AI companions; guest lecture
9. Overreliance and sycophancy (guest); safety and privacy in LLM use
11. Late-2026 topics; final presentations

## Prerequisites

The catalog lists none. The site strongly prefers [CS 224N](CS%20224N.md) or [CS 229](CS%20229.md), or equivalent NLP or ML background.
