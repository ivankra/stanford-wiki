# CS 468: Topics in Geometric Computing

CS 468 changes subject every offering; the catalog's own description lists past editions from computational topology to data-driven shape analysis. Autumn 2024's is "Foundation Models for 3D/4D Scene Understanding and Content Creation", built on the observation that foundation models are trained on text, images and video but "little 3D" — so the course first measures their spatial reasoning, then surveys ways to improve it. No homework: students present papers, discuss, and do a collaborative project.

## Syllabus

[The schedule](https://graphics.stanford.edu/courses/cs468-24-fall/schedule.html) runs Sep 23 to Dec 4.

1. Foundations (Sep 23-Oct 2): Transformers, GPT-3 and diffusion as background; geometry representations from DeepSDF to NeRF and 3D Gaussian splatting; two sessions surveying language and language-vision models through ViT, CLIP, DINO, SAM and BLIP-2.
2. What models already know (Oct 7-9): probing the 3D awareness of visual foundation models, semantic correspondence out of diffusion features, in-context learning for 3D with MeshGPT and Img2CAD.
3. Making them know more (Oct 14-30): fine-tuning and low-rank adaptation for 3D queries, parametric human models, 2D-for-3D neural rendering and feature distillation, neural approaches to point clouds and meshes.
4. Generation (Nov 4-20): foundation-assisted agents for 3D/4D content creation, shaping latent spaces for geometry, topology and physics, token-based and diffusion architectures, 3D from language, images and video, with guest lectures.
