# CS 331B: Interactive Simulation for Robot Learning

A survey of what robots can be taught inside a simulator, read paper by paper and then tried: every project runs on iGibson, the Stanford simulator the instructors built. The second half leaves single-agent tasks for multi-agent and human-robot settings — social navigation, collaborative manipulation, assistive dressing — with six guest lecturers from Google, Washington, AI2, Yale and MIT-IBM.

## Syllabus

Two parts after three introductory sessions ([schedule](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs331b/)).

1. **Introduction** (Mar 31-Apr 7): framing, then two sessions of iGibson tutorial and demos.
2. **Agent-environment interaction** (Apr 12-21): simulation environments compared — Habitat, SAPIEN, ThreeDWorld, AI2-THOR, MuJoCo; reinforcement and imitation learning from demonstrations (AC-Teach, human-in-the-loop teleoperation); manipulation and mobile manipulation (Dex-Net 2.0, 6-DOF GraspNet, ReLMoGen); sim2real by domain and dynamics randomization, and closing the loop with real-world experience.
3. **Multi-agent and human-robot interaction** (May 3-19): social navigation and HRI in simulation (VirtualHome, Assistive VR Gym, SEAN-EP); interactive tasks and character control (DeepMimic, Motion VAEs); collaborative manipulation and reactive handovers.
4. **Guest lectures** (Apr 26-May 17): Erwin Coumans (Google), Xiangyun Meng (Washington), Roozbeh Mottaghi (AI2), Marynel Vázquez (Yale), Chuang Gan (MIT-IBM).
