# CS 220: Researching, Presenting and Publishing Work in AI & Education

A seminar for PhD and advanced master's students doing research between AI and education, built around the questions the catalog puts first: "Which conference or journal is the right venue for my AI+education paper? How do I get CS folks to care about my education-focused work? How do I explain AI methods to a non-technical audience in education?" Work is done through discussion, presentations and peer feedback rather than lectures.

## Syllabus

From the catalog description, the seminar covers choosing a venue for AI+education work, addressing computer-science and education audiences in turn, explaining AI methods to non-technical readers, and the differing research and publication norms of the two communities. Students present their own work and give each other feedback.

## Prerequisites

None stated. Intended for PhD students and advanced master's students in education, computer science and related fields who have research of their own to publish.
