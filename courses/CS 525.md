# CS 525: Data for AI

Ludwig Schmidt's seminar on the half of modern machine learning that gets the least teaching: the corpus. It surveys the datasets contemporary language and multimodal models are actually trained on and the processing they go through — filtering, deduplication, synthetic generation, attribution — and then the parts that are not technical at all, copyright and privacy. The deliverable matches the subject: over the quarter each student builds a training set for a learning problem of their choice, rather than a model. Format is faculty lectures, student presentations and guest lectures.

## Syllabus

No schedule is published. The catalog states the scope:

> CS525 surveys the landscape of data for AI with a focus on contemporary learning problems such as training language models or multimodal models. Students will learn about important datasets and common data processing methods including filtering and deduplication. Further topics will include synthetic data, data attribution, and environments for reinforcement learning. The course will also cover ethical and legal aspects of training data such as copyright and privacy. Over the course of the class, students will build a training set for a learning problem of their choice. The class will consist of faculty lectures, student presentations, and guest lectures.

## Prerequisites

None stated. Building a training set for a language or multimodal model assumes the machine-learning background of [CS 229](CS%20229.md) or [CS 224N](CS%20224N.md) and enough engineering to run a large-scale filtering pipeline.
