# CS 106M: Enrichment Adventures in Programming Abstractions

Twenty [CS 106B](CS%20106B.md) students in a room once a week, taking the week's topic further than the main course can: discussion, hands-on activities and collaborative coding, with the excursions chosen partly by the students.

## Materials

Nothing but the course description is public. The one-page site publishes no schedule, no topic list and no exercises; announcements and materials go to Ed Discussion, which needs an account.

- **Syllabus**: the [site](https://cs106m.stanford.edu/) explains the format, enrolment cap and grading, but not what is covered week by week.
- **Videos**: none mentioned.
- **Assignments**: prep readings and follow-up exercises exist, but are distributed on Ed.
- **Exams**, **Projects**: none.

## Syllabus

The course follows the [CS 106B](CS%20106B.md) syllabus and picks supplemental material alongside it; the catalog says past topics have included search engines, pattern recognition, data compression and encryption, error correction, digital signatures and numerical recipes. The specific excursions are announced per quarter and are not published.

## Prerequisites

- Concurrent enrollment in [CS 106B](CS%20106B.md). Enrollment is capped at 20; credit requires attending every session, and auditing is offered to anyone who needs flexibility.
