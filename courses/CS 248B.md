# CS 248B: Fundamentals of Computer Graphics: Animation and Simulation

An introduction to computer graphics through animation and physics simulation, built around five programming assignments (P0–P4, starting with a JavaScript and OpenProcessing warm-up). There are no exams.

## Syllabus

From the Autumn 2026 schedule (19 lectures, marked tentative):

1. Particle systems and ODEs: forward/backward/midpoint/symplectic Euler, stability, stiffness
2. Particle collisions and collision detection (2 lectures)
3. Deformable models (3 lectures) and deformable collision processing
4. Fluids
5. Character modeling, forward and inverse kinematics
6. Interpolation and orientation
7. Rigid body simulation and constrained rigid body systems
8. Differentiable simulation and animation control; physics-based character animation; guest lecture

## Prerequisites

- Computer systems programming: [CS 107](CS%20107.md), computer organization and systems.
- Linear algebra: MATH 51.
