# CS 522: Seminar in Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

A weekly guest-speaker seminar on AI, machine learning and big data in healthcare, open to students of any background. It's run by student organizers from Stanford Students in Biodesign; there is no technical coursework.

## Materials

What a talk series owes a reader — the schedule and who is speaking — is public for this offering and for 4 of the 6 before it. What was said is not: the recordings go to Canvas, and no offering has a public archive of its talks.

- **Syllabus**: a public [syllabus document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1lkVKta57jw-ZJ-SIDsKeFIbz6wsVQeTj6P-9sUONdUg/edit?usp=sharing) and the [course page's](https://stanfordstudentsbiodesign.squarespace.com/cs-522) dated speaker list. Each offering keeps its own page: [Autumn 2025](https://stanfordstudentsbiodesign.squarespace.com/cs-522-25), [Autumn 2024](https://stanfordstudentsbiodesign.squarespace.com/cs-522-1), [Autumn 2023](https://stanfordstudentsbiodesign.squarespace.com/cs-522-copy), and Autumn 2020 in the Internet Archive.
- **Slides**: the speakers' decks are not collected or posted.
- **Notes**: none; a talk series with no readings of its own.
- **Videos**: recordings go to Canvas when they are made. Auditors can join that Canvas course through a form on the site, but only with a Stanford account.
- **Assignments**: the only written work is a make-up reflection for a missed seminar, and no prompt is published.
- **Solutions**, **Exams**, **Projects**, **Repo**: none; the grade is attendance at a 1-unit seminar.

## Syllabus

Ten 50-minute sessions from September 23 to December 2, each 5 minutes of opening remarks, a 35-minute talk and 10 minutes of Q&A. Autumn 2026 speakers come from Google DeepMind, Stanford biomedical data science, Abridge, Averin, OpenAI, Chai Discovery, Oura, Network Bio, Intuitive Surgical and NEA. The catalog frames the themes as diagnosis, genomics, surgical robotics, drug discovery, lower costs and earlier detection.

## Prerequisites

None; the catalog says no AI or healthcare background is needed.
