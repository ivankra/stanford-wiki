# CS 140M: Introduction to Embedded Systems

Four units on one microcontroller: lab assignments that work an ARM Cortex-M part through GPIO, clocks, interrupts, buses and storage, ending in a team-built wireless device. The course is an adaptation of Michigan's EECS 373 and is listed primarily as EE 186.

## Syllabus

The site states the arc without dates: the principles and practice of embedded system design — specification, design, development and testing — covering computer architecture, general-purpose input/output, clocks, interrupts, buses and storage, with lab assignments on an ARM Cortex-M microcontroller. "The class will culminate in a final project where teams of students will apply what they have learned throughout the quarter to design, build, and test the[ir] own wireless embedded system."
