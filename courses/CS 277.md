# CS 277: Foundation Models for Healthcare

Three instructors from radiology, biomedical data science and IBM Research run this as a survey of what it takes to put a foundation model in front of a clinician. The first seven weeks are general LLM and VLM material read through a medical lens — evaluation with MedArena and LLM-as-a-judge, RAG, PEFT, CLIP and latent diffusion, mechanistic interpretability. The last three turn to what the rest of the field skips: how the FDA actually evaluates and re-approves an AI device, what insurance-claims data says about clinical adoption, and where EHR foundation models stand. Two homeworks and a conference-style group paper; no exams.

## Syllabus

Eighteen lectures over ten weeks, per the [calendar](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs277/calendar/), split into "Background", "Applications" and "Opportunities".

1. **Weeks 1–4, language models**: foundation models and LLMs; agentic systems for research and discovery; two lectures on evaluation, covering MedArena, LLM-as-a-judge and the leaderboard illusion; RAG and mixture of experts; PEFT, alignment and inference scaling.
2. **Weeks 5–7, vision and multimodality**: vision-language models from CLIP and SigLIP; mechanistic interpretability of VLMs; generative VLMs for images (latent diffusion, MedVAE, synthetic chest X-rays) and for text (visual instruction tuning, BLIP-3); segmentation models.
3. **Week 8, deployment**: the EchoNet case study on how medical AI devices are evaluated, updated and deployed, with the FDA-approval and insurance-claims literature; SleepFM, a multimodal sleep foundation model.
4. **Weeks 9–10**: advanced architectures and multiplexed graph neural networks; EHR foundation models (MOTOR, generative medical event models); ethics and bias; then project presentations.

## Prerequisites

- Machine learning at the level of [CS 229](CS%20229.md), [CS 231N](CS%20231N.md) or [CS 224N](CS%20224N.md). No clinical background is stated.
- Access to a frontier language model is expected but not required; the course points students without one at the free Gemini Pro Education Plan.
