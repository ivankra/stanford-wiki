# CS 83N: Playback Theater

A theatre course in the CS department, and the catalog argues the connection rather than apologizing for it: "In playback, as in research, we are always moving together, from the known, to the unknown, and back." The quarter's work is to become a troupe.

## Syllabus

No published syllabus. The catalog describes the practice: "Playback combines elements of theater, community work and storytelling. In a playback show, a group of actors and musicians create an improvised performance based on the audience's personal stories. A playback show brings about a powerful listening and sharing experience. During the course, we will tell, listen, play together, and train in playback techniques. We will write diaries to process our experience in the context of education and research."

Its stated aim is "to strengthen listening abilities, creativity and the collaborative spirit, all integral parts of doing great science."

## Prerequisites

None. Enrolment is limited and by application, with preference to first-year students. The IntroSem listing notes attendance matters more than usual, the goal being to build a working troupe in ten weeks.
