# CS 194A: Android Programming Workshop

One unit, one hour a week, and the aim is portfolio rather than coverage: "build several Android apps together, empowering you to extend them, create your own apps, and build a portfolio."

## Materials

Everything is public. The slides and assignment briefs are Piazza-hosted PDFs, which looks gated and is not — Piazza's resource endpoint serves them to anyone.

- **Syllabus**: the [outline PDF](https://piazza.com/class_profile/get_resource/ktlu0ly5db84bb/kubmh9lrs5cyu), nine weeks of topics against their assignments, plus the page's own schedule, prerequisites and the grading rule — "You must complete each of the 3 assignments plus any extension to gain credit."
- **Slides**: [eight decks](https://piazza.com/class_profile/get_resource/ktlu0ly5db84bb/kubkxvhvj8g66m), one per teaching week.
- **Videos**: a [walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL7NYbSE8uaBCMVBVg6cskGzdYguj3CUP-) for every assignment, plus an Android Studio setup video, "Kotlin in 12 minutes", a shortcuts playlist and a RecyclerView explainer. Week 9 is a panel with four Android veterans; that one is not recorded.
- **Assignments**: three project [overviews](https://piazza.com/class_profile/get_resource/ktlu0ly5db84bb/kubsrado6ok1s6) — a tip calculator, a Google Maps client, a Yelp clone — each with its walkthrough and a required `README.md` template in the student's own GitHub repo.
- **Code**: the week-2 [BiggerNumber app](https://github.com/rpandey1234/BiggerNumberWWCode) and a public gist of the Kotlin Leetcode solutions.
- **Notes**, **Solutions**: not found. **Exams**: none. **Projects**: nothing students build is published.

## Syllabus

The [outline](https://piazza.com/class_profile/get_resource/ktlu0ly5db84bb/kubmh9lrs5cyu) runs nine weeks, one hour a week, each paired with something to build:

1. **Weeks 1–2**: "Why Android?" and a first app; Kotlin, exercised on two Leetcode problems written in it.
2. **Weeks 3–4**: layouts and responding to user input, then more UI and RecyclerView — the tip calculator, first as a walkthrough and then extended as the deliverable.
3. **Weeks 5–6**: activities, the activity lifecycle and intents — a Google Maps app on the same walkthrough-then-extend pattern.
4. **Weeks 7–8**: networking and working with APIs; Firebase and cloud storage, with a closing survey of fragments, testing strategies, app architectures and "alternatives to pure Android development".
5. **Week 9**: a panel with working Android engineers.
