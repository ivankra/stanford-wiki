## Syllabus

From the [site](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs194a/): "Learn basic, foundational techniques for developing Android mobile applications and apply those toward building a single or multi page, networked Android application."

"Topics include: the Android ecosystem, introduction to Kotlin, UI elements, lists, multiple activities and intents, working with APIs, and cloud storage." The schedule runs week by week from "Week 1: Introduction to Android".
