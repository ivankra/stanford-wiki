# CS 279: Computational Biology: Structure and Organization of Biomolecules and Cells

Computational methods for the structure and dynamics of biomolecules and cells, at three scales: atomic-level molecular modeling, solving structures from experimental data, and the spatial organization of cells. Covers both foundations and recent AI methods for structural biology.

## Materials

Almost everything is public, and more of it on this offering's own host than the schedule lets on: three unlinked directories — [`lectures/`](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs279/lectures/), [`notes/`](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs279/notes/) and [`handouts/`](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs279/handouts/) — are browsable. The schedule links lectures 1-2 so far, but `lectures/` already holds thirteen decks carried over on 3 September, most with an annotated version; `notes/` has the computer-vision, Fourier-transform, image-analysis and crystallography notes; and `handouts/` collects four practice finals (2019, 2020, 2022, 2024) and two sets of exam practice questions, **each with its solutions**, alongside the setup and remote-machine guides for this term. The [Autumn 2024 site](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs279/cs279.1252/) is a complete public offering: every lecture's slides and annotated slides, notes on [Fourier transforms](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs279/cs279.1252/notes/FT-notes.pdf) and [crystallography](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs279/cs279.1252/notes/X-RayCrystallography.pdf), all three assignments with starter code, [project guidelines](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs279/cs279.1252/assignments/Project%20Guidelines%20Handout.pdf), and a [practice final](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs279/cs279.1252/handouts/Practice_Final_Fall2024.pdf) with [solutions](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs279/cs279.1252/handouts/Practice_Final_Solutions_Fall2024.pdf). Each optional reading has a public publisher link. Gated: recordings (Canvas/Panopto), Stanford-only reading PDFs, sample past projects.

## Syllabus

Autumn 2026 schedule:

1. Introduction; biomolecular structure
2. Energy functions and molecular conformation
3. Molecular dynamics simulation
4. Structure prediction for proteins and other biomolecules, including machine learning methods such as AlphaFold
5. Protein design
6. Fourier transforms and convolution; image analysis; microscopy
7. Diffusion and cellular-level simulation
8. Project topics
9. Ligand docking and virtual screening
10. X-ray crystallography; cryo-electron microscopy
11. Review

Assignments: (1) biomolecular structure and visualization, (2) atomic-level molecular modeling, (3) cellular structure and dynamics. All in Python.
