# CS 242: Programming Languages

PL theory from small calculi upward, then applied to a survey of real languages. Seven weekly programming assignments, an in-class midterm and a cumulative final; all work is individual.

## Materials

The theory half is public and the programming half is not. Every offering since Autumn 2021 keeps its schedule and its complete slide set, and eight past exam papers come with worked solutions — but the seven programming assignments, 35% of the grade and about ten hours a week, are named on the schedule and linked from nowhere, and the recordings are on Canvas.

- **Syllabus**: the [schedule](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs242/materials.html) dates all eighteen lectures with their readings and the assignment dates, and [coursework](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs242/coursework.html) gives the split — homeworks 35%, in-class midterm 25%, cumulative final 40%.
- **Slides**: two of this offering's decks so far, posted as the lectures happen. The archived schedules carry the complete sets: [Autumn 2025](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs242/cs242.1262/materials.html) 20 decks, and 17 to 19 for each offering back to Autumn 2021.
- **Notes**: one handout, on [Haskell](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs242/materials/lectures/haskell-notes.pdf). The rest of the reading is published papers the schedule links — Backus's Turing lecture, MapReduce, CompCert, seL4.
- **Videos**: on Canvas, whose course page redirects to the Stanford login. No public copy was found for any offering.
- **Assignments**: the seven are named and dated on the schedule but published nowhere. What is public is their setup: Racket and Rust guides, a Racket 7.9 tarball and a test patch under [materials/assignments/](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs242/materials/assignments/). The course's GitHub organization has only [Autumn 2018](https://github.com/stanford-cs242/f18-assignments) and Autumn 2019 assignment repositories, both before `cutoff_term`.
- **Solutions**, **Exams**: [past exams](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs242/exams.html) has eight papers — midterms and finals, Autumn 2021 to Autumn 2024 — each with its worked solution. Nothing for the homeworks.
- **Projects**: none; the course sets no project and all work is individual.
- **Repo**: nothing beyond the Racket tarball and patch above.

## Syllabus

Eighteen lectures:

1. **Foundations:** course overview; combinator calculus (two lectures); lambda calculus; typed lambda calculus; polymorphic types.
2. **Language features:** state, continuations, monads, objects. Midterm follows.
3. **Language survey:** Rust, Prolog, Haskell.
4. **Verification and types:** program verification via type theory, with CompCert and seL4 papers as reading; Agda; gradual typing.
5. **Array programming**, then wrap-up.

[CS 103](CS%20103.md) and [CS 110](CS%20110.md).
