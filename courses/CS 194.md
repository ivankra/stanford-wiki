# CS 194: Software Project

The capstone build: a team ships one substantial piece of software over a quarter, working the way industry does — a shared repository, a weekly progress log, an architecture diagram, and a public demo at the end. Preference goes to seniors, and it may be repeated for credit.

## Materials

What the course hands out is on Canvas: "Canvas will be used for course annoucements, syllabus and assignments." What the teams build is not. 29 of the 30 Spring 2026 team repositories in the [StanfordCS194 organization](https://github.com/StanfordCS194) are public, code and project documentation together, so a reader can see what a quarter here produces but not what was asked for.

- **Syllabus**: on Canvas. The [course page](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs194/) gives the course description, the Monday/Wednesday/Friday lecture slot, the attendance rule and the enrollment form, and no schedule or grading scheme. Canvas holds a Stanford-only upload for 8 of the 13 older offerings.
- **Slides**, **Notes**, **Videos**: nothing found for any offering; the site mentions no recording and links no lecture material.
- **Assignments**: on Canvas. The milestones and issue tracker the site points to, `StanfordCS194/Home`, "you can only see as a member of the organization", and membership follows from an enrollment form. The 2015–2017 handouts the site once served (a syllabus, a project-proposal spec, a project-log spec) are gone from the host.
- **Solutions**: not applicable to a build course, and none found.
- **Exams**: none; the deliverables are the project, its documentation and the public demonstration.
- **Projects**: [the organization](https://github.com/StanfordCS194) lists 29 public Spring 2026 team repositories (`spr26-Team-2` to `spr26-Team-30`, one of them empty), pushed through the last week of the quarter. 11 keep a `docs/` or `wiki/` folder with the documentation the catalog requires: architecture notes and diagrams, a PRD, OKR and KPI write-ups, user-test plans and midpoint testing reports. No offering before Spring 2026 has a public repository.
- **Repo**: the same 29 repositories are the teams' source, in TypeScript, Python, Swift and Dart, from 429 KB to 327 MB. The organization's [Pages site](https://stanfordcs194.github.io/) adds nothing but a pointer to Canvas.

## Syllabus

No public syllabus exists, so the catalog is the only source: "Design, specification, coding, and testing of a significant team programming project under faculty supervision. Documentation includes capture of project rationale, design and discussion of key performance indicators, a weekly progress log and a software architecture diagram. Public demonstration of the project at the end of the quarter."

The site adds the one thing the catalog does not: "You will be exposed to industry practices in managing larger code bases and larger scale product objectives", with GitHub used for source control, documentation and project management.

Lectures are Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and attendance is required.
