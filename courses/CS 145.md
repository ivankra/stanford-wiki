# CS 145: Intro to Modern Data Systems

A database course organized as twenty questions with names attached — how Spotify searches 100 million tracks, how one Stripe charge becomes four writes that all commit or none, how an agent remembers anything between sessions — and written as a public website that invites readers who aren't enrolled.

## Materials

The whole course reader, its slides, its videos and its problem sets are on the open web, and the site is written for outside readers as much as for the class. The sign-in the videos and PSETs ask for takes any Google account or an email link, so it isn't limited to Stanford; for the PSETs it only saves progress. The Autumn 2026 term is in progress, so the exam material is still to come.

- **Syllabus**: the [Autumn 2026 schedule](https://cs145.web.app/schedule-fa26.html), weeks 1 to 10, with every PSET, project and exam deadline and the "resolve any conflict before enrolling" rule for the December 7 final.
- **Slides**: the [lecture PDF archive](https://cs145.web.app/pdfs/lectures/) keeps a fixed copy of each deck by date; the live slides sit in the module pages.
- **Notes**: the course reader is the site — [module pages](https://cs145.web.app/Module1-SQL/joins.html) and the twenty case studies, required reading unless a page marks itself optional.
- **Videos**: a five-minute video mode on each notes page, behind that free sign-in, plus roughly four-minute CA walkthroughs of the section Colabs. These are the course's video content: the FAQ sends a student who missed a lecture to those pages and their videos, not to a recording.
- **Assignments**: the [PSET scheme](https://cs145.web.app/psets/psets.html) and [project pages](https://cs145.web.app/projects/projects.html) are public — five graded sets with unlimited attempts before the deadline, and two projects.
- **Solutions**: no answer keys are posted, and nothing shows whether enrolled students get any; the PSETs bank correct answers between attempts instead.
- **Exams**: one in-person final, December 7, "40 MCQs plus a couple of FRQs"; the paths page promises practice exams in week 8, and none are up yet.
- **Projects**: two are set, and no student work is published.
- **Repo**: the eight [section Colabs](https://cs145.web.app/projects/projects.html) run query plans, WAL traces and distributed jobs.
- **Self-study**: the [paths page](https://cs145.web.app/paths.html) routes a reader who isn't enrolled three ways — the 27 case studies in course order, a three-to-five-hour SQL-screen sequence, and a six-to-ten-hour systems-design sequence — and its [playground notebook](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/19raF0_aeJs-dQdQC9tZ8GhveXVhAIdji) downloads for anyone and runs real queries against a Spotify schema, so the work can be checked without the course.

## Syllabus

Six modules, worked through the case studies listed on the [front page](https://cs145.stanford.edu/):

1. **SQL and intermediate SQL**: joins and query semantics, then the correctness problem of LLM-generated SQL — ambiguity, unit tests, eval sets, grounding.
2. **Systems**: Bloom filters, vector indexes and HNSW search.
3. **Storage and indexing**: B+Trees and inverted indexes, index selection and IO cost models, columnar storage, LSM trees.
4. **Transactions**: ACID, atomicity, two-phase locking.
5. **Distributed data**: replication, sharding, two-phase commit, Kafka's append-only log, GFS-style storage on unreliable drives.
6. **Data systems in practice**: data quality and observability, privacy and re-identification, SQL injection, KV-cache reuse for agents.

Five PSETs with unlimited attempts before the deadline, two projects, a final exam.
