# CS 227A: Robot Perception

Sensor hardware on a robot (vision, tactile, acoustic), the algorithms that process its raw data, and how to act on the result. Over the homeworks, students build their own vision-based robotics system in simulation; 4-unit students also submit a final written report.

## Syllabus

From the Winter 2024 schedule (19 sessions, several guest lectures):

1. Transformations, coordinate frames, image formation
2. 3D cameras and 3D representations
3. Reconstruction and mapping; pose estimation with noise
4. CNNs, learning-based 3D vision, neural radiance fields, pretrained vision models for robotics
5. Robot definition and kinematics; simulation and planning
6. Vision-based pick-and-place (pose estimation)
7. Behavior cloning; self-supervised learning and action affordance; scalable robot data collection
8. Tactile sensing; acoustic sensing

## Prerequisites

The catalog asks for Python and basic linear algebra, reviews most of the maths, and assumes strong programming: all homework is programming-heavy. The Winter 2024 site also lists data structures.
