## Syllabus

The course's stated span, in its own words: "From energy, silicon and models to applications, security and deployment policy, the infrastructure stack is undergoing a generational rewrite. Each week, this course puts you in the room with the global leaders solving the biggest bottlenecks on frontier technology progress."

The speaker list maps the stack rather than a week order: energy and materials (General Matter, Periodic Labs), silicon (NVIDIA, MatX, Tesla Autopilot), cloud and datacenter (Google, Microsoft), frontier models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Black Forest Labs, ElevenLabs, Luma AI), routing and evaluation (OpenRouter, Arena), security (a former Uber, Facebook and Cloudflare CSO), and capital and policy (a16z, Y Combinator, the Norwegian Sovereign Wealth Fund, the US government).

The readings document opens with a list of "Key Foundational Papers": AlexNet, Word2Vec, the Atari DQN paper, Attention is All You Need, BERT, the GPT-1/2/3 papers, DDPM, Scaling Laws, InstructGPT, Chinchilla, and Sutton's "The Bitter Lesson".

The project is "The One-Person Frontier Lab": "One person with the right tools can now produce what once required an organization. 10 weeks. One goal: create value for the world."
