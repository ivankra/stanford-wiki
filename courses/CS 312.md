# CS 312: Deep Learning Alchemy

Students run more than 100 small training runs per unit, then sit a 40-minute quiz predicting, from a code diff, how experiments they haven't seen turn out. The 7 quizzes are nearly the whole grade; the assignments count only as evidence to cite in them. Units 1–4 pre-train small language models on DCLM web text; units 5–7 will train them on DNA sequences instead.

## Syllabus

7 units, per the [schedule](https://cs312.stanford.edu/#schedule) and decks. Each runs an assignment, 1 or 2 lectures, a weekly 30-minute tutorial for 4 students and a staff member, a report due the day before the quiz, and the 40-minute quiz itself, followed by its answers.

1. **Basics**: why deep learning is now an experimental science; hyperparameter surfaces over learning rate and batch size; scaling laws as cheap proxies, back to 1993, and how they fail; run-to-run randomness, down to chaotic dynamics, and paired significance tests; diagnostics from loss curves, activations and QK norm, with examples from Marin's training runs.
2. **Optimization 1, hyperparameter invariants**: the noisy quadratic model for batch size, momentum and preconditioning; norm drift in norm-invariant transformers, weight decay recast, and the Hyperball optimizer.
3. **Optimization 2**: sharp and flat basins.
4. **Architecture 1**: extending model capacity.
5. **Architecture 2**: stability across depth and time.
6. **Generalization**: data-efficient algorithms.
7. **Exotic**: not yet announced.

A supplementary LLMs 101 session covers the default training stack for students new to deep learning.

## Prerequisites

- Calculus and linear algebra: MATH 51 or CME 100.
- Probability and statistics: [CS 109](CS%20109.md) or equivalent.
- Python, enough to read the provided codebase and the short diffs quizzes are built on; the course supports AI tools for implementation.
- Recommended, not required: some deep learning ([CS 221](CS%20221.md), [CS 229](CS%20229.md), [CS 230](CS%20230.md), [CS 124](CS%20124.md) or [CS 224N](CS%20224N.md)), since backpropagation and efficient implementation get little time. The site welcomes students with no deep learning background, who face more work at the start.
