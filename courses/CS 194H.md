# CS 194H: User Interface Design Project

The follow-on to [CS 147](CS%20147.md): where that course teaches the design process, this one runs a single project the length of the quarter and ends in a public presentation. Branded "dt+UX2" on its own site.

## Materials

This offering keeps its briefs private, but [Winter 2023](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs194h/2023/wi/) publishes the identical 17 in full, and the calendar, the course reader and 4 years of finished projects are open.

- **Syllabus**: the [calendar](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs194h/2024/wi/calendar.html) gives the 10 weeks session by session with readings and due dates; the [course details](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs194h/2024/wi/logistics.html) page the grading split (individual work 30%, participation 20%, group project 50%) and the late policy, and the landing page the 17 deliverables. Winter 2020, Spring 2022 and Winter 2023 each keep their own site, with the term in the path.
- **Slides**: an unlinked [lectures listing](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs194h/2024/wi/lectures/) carries 5 decks: the introduction, the Studio Task 1 and 2 takeaways, UX writing and the project launch. The studio and presentation sessions, most of the quarter, have none.
- **Notes**: the [course reader](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs194h/2024/wi/readings/restricted/) is open despite its directory name: 15 PDFs the calendar assigns by name, 7 of them chapters of *Universal Principles of Design* and the rest papers on business models, the lean start-up, value-sensitive design and AI UX guidelines. The Resources document the header links answers 401; [Winter 2020's copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ByWf9fYl2gzhHpWmZzm35L1NcPca1wb4zR0_zZlXT1Y/preview) opens, a list of design guides and prototyping tools.
- **Videos**: no source says the sessions are recorded. The one published recording is [Winter 2020's launch party](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs194h/2020/wi/videos/cs194h-wi-20-expo.mp4), the public presentation the quarter ends in.
- **Solutions**, **Exams**: none; "There are no quizzes or exams", and the studio work has no answer key.
- **Assignments**: this offering's 21 briefs all answer 401, as Spring 2022's do. [Winter 2023's 17](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs194h/2023/wi/) export without an account: 5 studio tasks (tagging and hierarchy, Gestalt, color, control, an online user study), 4 project presentations, the Lean Business Model Canvas, the team website, 2 prototype checkpoints, the poster, the video and the final report.
- **Projects**: 6 Winter 2024 team sites at [`class/cs194h/projects/`](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs194h/projects/), with archives for 2017, 2020 and 2023. Each walks its own process: QueerX, "where the good doctors are", publishes needfinding, POVs and prototyping decks across the 2 quarters it spans with [CS 147](CS%20147.md).
- **Repo**: none found; the deliverables are prototypes and team web sites, not code.
- **Extra**: `class/cs194h/studio/` holds about a hundred per-student studio sites, 2017 to 2024.

## Syllabus

From the site's own description: "Advanced methods for designing, prototyping, and evaluating user interfaces to computing applications. Novel interface technology, advanced interface design methods, and prototyping tools. Substantial, quarter-long course project that will be presented in a public presentation."

The structure is the assignment table: around a dozen numbered deliverables with due dates, carrying one project from brief to public presentation.

## Source notes

- **The project and studio work is on the class host, not the course site.** `hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs194h/2024/wi/` is the course site and publishes no student work; `web.stanford.edu/class/cs194h/` is an open Apache listing that holds all of it — `projects/`, `projects_2017/`, `projects_2020/`, `projects_2023/`, `studio/`. Nothing links the two. It also holds a `restricted/` folder that redirects to SSO, the Stanford convention, so some web material is gated here too (checked 2026-09-28).
- One Winter 2024 project site, `projects/turno/`, is a React app that renders "You need to enable JavaScript to run this app." to a plain fetch.
- **Sharing is set per document, per offering, and the only reliable test is the export endpoint.** All 21 documents the Winter 2024 site links answer 401 to `/export?format=txt`, `/preview` and `/edit` alike, as do all 15 of Spring 2022's; all 22 of [Winter 2023's](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs194h/2023/wi/) export except the Resources document and one other, and both of [Winter 2020's](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs194h/2020/wi/) export (checked 2026-10-02). A `/preview` URL renders through JavaScript, so it returns a plausible-looking page whatever its permissions.
- **Every offering keeps its own site under the HCI group's host**, at `hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs194h/<year>/<term>/`: `2020/wi`, `2022/sp`, `2023/wi` and `2024/wi` all resolve, and the bare `/courses/cs194h/` path serves Winter 2023's. `cs194h.stanford.edu` does not resolve (checked 2026-10-02). An earlier pass recorded `web.stanford.edu/class/cs194h/` as holding nothing but a Python script for creating AFS directories; the script is there, and so are the 6 directories of student work above it.
- **`readings/restricted/` is not restricted.** Under every offering's site it is an open Apache listing of the scanned chapters and papers, served without a login, where the identically named folder under `web.stanford.edu/class/cs194h/` redirects to SSO. The decks in `lectures/` are likewise reachable only by guessing the path; the calendar links individual files, never the directory.
- The site titles the course "User Experience Design Project" where the catalog says "User Interface Design Project".
