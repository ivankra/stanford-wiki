# CS 377E: Designing Solutions to Global Grand Challenges

A studio built around one theme a year. Spring 2023's was foundation models for educational equity, and teams ran the full design-thinking arc — preliminary research, needfinding, journey maps, lo-fi prototypes, rapid experimentation — to a public project expo in June with awards for best design and best use of foundation models. No exams; 60% of the grade is the group project.

## Materials

## Syllabus

[Studios run twice weekly](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs377e/2023/sp/calendar.html), A and B.

1. Week 1: course introduction and team formation, then opportunity-area pitches; preliminary research assigned.
2. Week 2: equitable AI, background-research share-outs, needfinding and empathy work, with two AIGA readings on giving and receiving design critique.
3. Middle weeks: journey maps, lo-fi prototyping, rapid experimentation, interaction design, and an individual heuristic evaluation.
4. Week 10: the project expo, June 7, with awards.
