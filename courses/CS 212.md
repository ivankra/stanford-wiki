# CS 212: Operating Systems and Systems Programming

Operating systems in one quarter: lectures from threads through virtual machines, reinforced by the four Pintos kernel projects. The site calls it "effectively an accelerated class combining CS111 and CS112" — for students who have not taken [CS 111](CS%20111.md), typically graduate students, and closed to those who have. Lectures, site and exams are shared with [CS 112](CS%20112.md).

## Materials

Every page the course has is public, and the notes index grants permission to copy and redistribute: the schedule, 23 decks with their LaTeX sources, the Pintos manual, the 4 project handouts and the kernel source. No source says whether the lectures are recorded, and no public set turned up. Each offering since Winter 2022 keeps its own copy at a term path.

## Syllabus

Ten weeks, Monday and Wednesday lectures with five Friday sections, from the [schedule](https://www.scs.stanford.edu/26sp-cs212/sched/). Readings are given as "Text: Ch. …" without naming the book.

1. Intro, threads and processes, concurrency, scheduling. Lab 0 due.
2. Virtual memory, hardware then OS techniques; synchronization over two lectures. **HW 1 (Threads)** due.
3. Linking, memory allocation, midterm quiz. **HW 2 (Userprog)** due.
4. I/O and disks, file systems, networking, protection. **HW 3 (VM)** due.
5. Security, virtual machines. **HW 4 (File system)** due; final exam 2026-06-06.

## Prerequisites

None in the catalog. The course's own FAQ expects:

- No prior operating systems course; you must not have taken [CS 111](CS%20111.md) or [CS 112](CS%20112.md), whose material this compresses.
- A strong C background, since the projects are kernel code from week one.

## Related

- [CS 111](CS%20111.md) then [CS 112](CS%20112.md) (the unaccelerated sequence) and [CS 140E](CS%20140E.md) (kernel work without Pintos) are the alternatives the FAQ names; all three are undergraduate, so no page here.
- [CS 240](CS%20240.md): OS research papers rather than building an OS; its prerequisite is [CS 140](CS%20140.md), this course's predecessor.
- [CS 340LX](CS%20340LX.md): kernel labs on bare-metal Raspberry Pi hardware instead of Pintos in a simulator.
