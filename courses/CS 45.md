# CS 45: Software Tools Every Programmer Should Know

Written and taught by students, and the direct ancestor of [CS 104](CS%20104.md) — the site announced the succession itself before the course ended. Two units, twice a week, S/NC.

## Materials

The course names what it gives a student who misses class — "lecture notes as well as slides for each lecture" — and both are public for all 17 sessions, along with every assignment handout and the starter repositories they need. The one thing held back is the recording, which the course itself calls a best-effort extra of uncertain quality.

- **Syllabus**: the [front page](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs45/) gives the description and meeting pattern, and the [course-info page](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs45/course_info) the whole structure — 8 weekly assignments and a final project, the 25 points that earn an S, 3 late days, and "There will be no exams in this course."
- **Slides**: [17 lecture PDFs](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs45/lectures/), one per session, from shell tools through networking, version control, CI/CD, debugging, cryptography, containers, serverless and media encoding.
- **Notes**: 9 of those lectures also have a [written notes PDF](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs45/lectures/2-shell-tools.notes.pdf) beside the deck.
- **Videos**: "We will try to post lecture recordings to Canvas on a best-effort basis, but we can't make any guarantees about their quality (or even existence)". Winter 2023's page added that the room's recording system gives poor audio and video. Nothing public exists.
- **Assignments**: [9 handouts](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs45/assignments/), assign0 through assign8, each with its parts and point values, plus the final-project brief. Submission is on Gradescope; the handouts themselves are open, and assignment 8 pulls a 57 MB file bundle from the course host.
- **Solutions**: none posted, and the site doesn't say whether enrolled students get any.
- **Exams**: none, by the course's own statement.
- **Projects**: the final-project brief is public; no student project is.
- **Repo**: [`stanford-cs45`](https://github.com/stanford-cs45), 10 public repositories — starter code for both offerings' version-control, CI/CD and message-board assignments, 2 lecture examples, and the site's own source. Nothing links to it except the handouts.
- **Winter 2023**: that whole offering survives in [Stanford's class archive](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs45/cs45.1234/) — 16 decks, 8 notes PDFs, and 8 handouts as both pages and PDFs. Spring 2023's archived copy is the live site, byte for byte.

## Syllabus

The site's description is the one [CS 104](CS%20104.md) later inherited nearly verbatim: "there's one critical subject that's rarely covered, and is instead left to students to figure out on their own: proficiency with their tools. This course will teach you how to master the key tools necessary for being a successful computer scientist, such as the command line, version control systems, debuggers and linters, and many more. In addition, we will cover other key topics that are left out of standard CS classes… security and cryptography, containers and virtual machines, and cloud computing."

## Source notes

- **The site is a SvelteKit app, but its prerendered HTML names every file** — as long as the URL carries its trailing slash. `…/class/cs45/lectures` 301s to `…/lectures/`, and only the second serves the markup that lists the 26 PDFs; a fetch that stops at the redirect sees an empty page. All 26, plus the final-project PDF and the 9 assignment pages, answered 200 on 2026-10-02.
- `cs45.stanford.edu` redirects to `web.stanford.edu/class/cs45/`, but assignment 8's 57 MB file bundle is served from the vanity host's own `res/` path and only from there.
- The [`stanford-cs45` GitHub organization](https://github.com/stanford-cs45) is not linked from any navigation; its repositories are named inside the assignment handouts, which is why they turn up only by reading the assignments.

- The two courses are kept on separate pages rather than merged: [CS 104](CS%20104.md) is described by its own staff as "an expanded offering of CS45", with different units, instructors and level, which is a succession rather than a renumbering (checked 2026-09-27).
