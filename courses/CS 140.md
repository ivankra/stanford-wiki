# CS 140: Operating Systems and Systems Programming

The course the current OS sequence came out of. The staff's own account of why it ended is on the [CS 112](CS%20112.md) site: CS 140 "suffered from several problems", chiefly that "many students found the Pintos projects challenging to complete while simultaneously learning about operating systems for the first time", and its workload "was more comparable to a 200-level class". It was split into [CS 111](CS%20111.md) and [CS 112](CS%20112.md) in 2022.

## Materials

Everything but the recordings is public, one offering back: Winter 2021's archive carries the schedule, the full lecture-notes set, the Pintos manual and projects, the Pintos source and an exam archive with solutions. The final Winter 2022 offering left no archive at all, and its syllabus went to Canvas.

- **Syllabus**: Winter 2022's is on Canvas, Stanford-only. [Winter 2021's schedule](https://www.scs.stanford.edu/21wi-cs140/sched/) and [Winter 2020's](https://www.scs.stanford.edu/20wi-cs140/sched/) are open, dated week by week with the textbook chapters, the homework due dates and the linked readings (Birrell on threads, the Unix paper, the Orange Book). Spring 2020, the one offering Ousterhout taught, left nothing but a Canvas upload.
- **Slides**: the [Winter 2021 notes index](https://www.scs.stanford.edu/21wi-cs140/notes/) publishes 19 topics and 4 section decks three ways each — a slide PDF, a six-up print PDF and a LaTeX source tarball — from the introduction through linking, memory allocation, file systems, networking, protection, security and virtual machines. [Winter 2020's](https://www.scs.stanford.edu/20wi-cs140/notes/) is the same set.
- **Notes**: the [Pintos manual](https://www.scs.stanford.edu/21wi-cs140/pintos/pintos.pdf), which is the written instruction the projects run on, plus the example C programs (`type.c`, `minish.c`, `redirsh.c`, `pipesh.c`) that ship beside the decks that discuss them.
- **Videos**: lectures went over Zoom, joined in Canvas; the landing page's own video link is commented out, and no public recording of any offering turned up.
- **Assignments**: [Lab 0 and the 4 Pintos projects](https://www.scs.stanford.edu/21wi-cs140/labs/project.html) — threads, user programs, virtual memory, file systems — each with its manual chapter, its FAQ and a design-document template. Lab 0 is Stanford account setup and nothing else.
- **Solutions**: posted for 29 of the archived exams; nothing for the projects or the homeworks.
- **Exams**: the [exam archive](https://www.scs.stanford.edu/21wi-cs140/exams/) holds 31 midterms and finals, but all of them from 1999 to 2007, and the staff's own disclaimer says they "were written by previous professors". Winter 2021 serves it unlinked; Winter 2020's landing page still names it.
- **Projects**: none; the Pintos assignments are the whole build.
- **Repo**: the [Pintos source](http://cs140.stanford.edu/pintos.tar.gz) still downloads from the course's old host, so the projects can be attempted off a Stanford machine.

## Syllabus

The [notes index](https://www.scs.stanford.edu/21wi-cs140/notes/) gives the sequence: introduction, processes and threads, concurrency, scheduling, virtual memory hardware, virtual memory in the OS, synchronization in two parts, and onward through file systems — the structure [CS 212](CS%20212.md) still follows.
