# CS 224S: Spoken Language Processing

Speech as a full stack in 10 weeks: phonetics through TTS, ASR and dialogue, with 90% of the grade on 4 build-something homeworks and no exams. Students are told to budget about $60 for Colab compute instead of a textbook, and homework 4 may be swapped for a research project with a spotlight talk.

## Materials

Lectures, readings and most of the homework are public; the recordings are not, and neither is this offering's fourth assignment. Spring 2022's page still carries all 4.

- **Syllabus**: the [Spring 2025 syllabus](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224s/semesters/2025-spring/syllabus) lists each lecture with its readings, mostly chapters of Jurafsky and Martin's [SLP3 draft](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/) plus Tacotron, WaveNet and FastSpeech, and the homework dates; the [home page](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224s/) has the grading split and policies. [Spring 2024's](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224s/semesters/2024-spring/syllabus) and [Spring 2022's](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224s/semesters/2022-spring/syllabus) are still on the live host, and [Winter 2021's](https://web.archive.org/web/20210419010209/http://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224s/syllabus/) survives in the Internet Archive.
- **Slides**: a deck per lecture, [lecture 1](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224s/semesters/2025-spring/lecture-slides/224s.25.lec1.pdf) to lecture 15, all resolving. Spring 2022 posted 18 and Spring 2024 13.

- **Assignments**: handouts for [assignment 1](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224s/semesters/2025-spring/assignments/a1), [2](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224s/semesters/2025-spring/assignments/a2) and [3](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224s/semesters/2025-spring/assignments/a3); assignment 4 never appears, and the [project page](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224s/semesters/2025-spring/project) sets out the optional research project that replaces it. [Spring 2022's set](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224s/semesters/2022-spring/assignments/) runs a1 to a4, the only public fourth assignment.

## Syllabus

10 weeks ([syllabus](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224s/semesters/2025-spring/syllabus)): introduction and acoustic phonetics (articulation, acoustics, ARPAbet); speech synthesis — history, text normalization, letter-to-sound, prosody, then data collection, evaluation, concatenative and parametric TTS; TTS with deep learning (Tacotron, WaveNet, FastSpeech); spoken dialogue overview and system and user-experience design; automatic speech recognition foundations; state-of-the-art ASR and social meaning extraction; speech foundation models and non-English languages; low-resource systems and dialogue control with LLMs; societal impacts of conversational AI; and industry specialization plus project spotlights.

## Prerequisites

- Machine learning or NLP foundations: [CS 124](CS%20124.md), [CS 129](CS%20129.md), [CS 221](CS%20221.md), [CS 224N](CS%20224N.md) or [CS 229](CS%20229.md) — the site says it doesn't enforce a specific one.
- Python, with PyTorch, Jupyter and the Alexa Skills Kit used in homeworks.
