# CS 234: Reinforcement Learning

Emma Brunskill's survey of reinforcement learning, from tabular MDPs to RLHF. What distinguishes it from a deep-RL course is where the weight falls: 3 of the 14 lectures are on exploration and strategic data gathering, and the analysis criteria the course keeps returning to are regret and sample complexity, not just returns. On-campus students carry a mandatory weekly tutorial worth 24% of the grade, which off-campus students do not take — the two groups are graded on different schemes.

## Syllabus

14 lectures over 10 weeks, per the [schedule](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs234/) and [Lecture Materials](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs234/modules.html).

1. **Weeks 1-2, tabular methods**: introduction to RL; MDP planning; policy evaluation; Q-learning and function approximation. Readings from Sutton and Barto chapters 1, 3–6.
2. **Weeks 3–4, policy gradient**: three lectures on policy search, then imitation learning and offline RL.
3. **Weeks 5–6, learning from fixed data**: offline RL and RLHF, then bandits.
4. **Weeks 6–8, exploration**: strategic data gathering and three lectures on exploration, drawing on the Bandit Algorithms book; then RL with Monte Carlo tree search and Go.
5. **Weeks 9–10**: a guest lecture on world models, then alignment and impacts, closing with a poster session.

## Prerequisites

- Python: every assignment is in it.
- Multivariable calculus and linear algebra: MATH 51 or CME 100.
- Probability and statistics: [CS 109](CS%20109.md) or equivalent.
- Machine learning foundations: [CS 221](CS%20221.md) or [CS 229](CS%20229.md) — the course formulates cost functions and optimizes them with gradient descent. Convex optimization is helpful but not required.

## Related

- [CS 224R](CS%20224R.md): deep RL with a robotics and motor-control emphasis; neither is a prerequisite for the other, and its own catalog entry describes itself as the more applied of the two.
- [CS 238](CS%20238.md): decision making under uncertainty, covering MDPs and POMDPs mostly from the planning side, with RL as one unit.
